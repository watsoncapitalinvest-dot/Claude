HOW 2024 WAS BUILT
==========================================================
Everyone played their three division rivals twice, as usual. But eight
clubs also played a fourth opponent twice, from outside the division,
and the other eight did not — so half the league played 4 opponents
twice and 6 once, and half played 3 twice and 8 once.

The extra home-and-aways were:
  Big Blue             v Smoke Dragons          (Black and Blue v Spectre Syndicate)
  Guido Haters         v Pork Chop Express      (Spectre Syndicate v Black and Blue)
  Hairy Gumbas         v Lil Chops              (The Four Horsemen v Spartan)
  Powers of Pain       v The Beaver Eaters      (Spectre Syndicate v Black and Blue)

That is the only season since at least 2021 with an uneven shape.
2023, 2025 and 2026 all give every club exactly three doubles.
