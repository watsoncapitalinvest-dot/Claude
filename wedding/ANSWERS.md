## What these answers determine

**Location resolves to West Palm Beach.** Winter (Jan–Mar) + oceanfront resort +
outdoor ceremony cannot happen in Westchester — January on Long Island Sound rules
out an outdoor ceremony entirely. Westchester's own peak is Sept–Oct foliage. The
app keeps the comparison visible, but the recommendation is Palm Beach County.

**The tension to manage:** 150 guests × peak Palm Beach winter × oceanfront resort ×
full weekend is the single most expensive combination in the research — while the
stated worries are cost creep and "cheap but not white trash." Their instincts already
cut cost in three places: cocktail-style over plated (saves ~$40–60/guest), DJ over
live band (saves $5–12K), month-of coordinator over full-service (saves $5–10K).
The remaining levers are venue tier, guest count, and day of week.

**Timing is urgent-ish:** peak-season Saturdays at name Palm Beach venues book
12–18 months out. A Jan–Mar 2028 date means venue hunting should start now.

**Dates to avoid in the target window (2028):** MLK weekend Jan 15–17,
Super Bowl weekend Feb 4–6, Presidents Day weekend Feb 19–21.
