# Wedding Research Brief: West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County, FL
*Compiled July 2026 — for a couple planning a Palm Beach County wedding*

---

## 1. Costs: What Weddings Run in Palm Beach County

**Headline numbers**
- **National average (The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study):** ~$34,200 for a ceremony + reception (survey of 10,474 couples married in 2025). Note the *median* U.S. wedding is far lower (~$10K–$20K) — averages are pulled up by luxury markets like Palm Beach.
- **Florida average:** roughly **$25,000–$40,000+**, with a per-guest average around **$263** (Pix Wedding / Priscila Del Cristo Photo 2026 guides).
- **Palm Beach specifically (Wedding Report estimates):**
  - 100–125 guests: **$51,550–$63,006** (~**$458/guest**)
  - 50–75 guests: **$25,510–$31,178** (~$378/guest)
  - West Palm Beach proper (slightly less than the island): **$37,168–$45,428** for a typical wedding
- **South Florida planner consensus (Elegant Events by Michalea, Barn 305):** a realistic full-service 100-person wedding in the Miami–Palm Beach corridor lands at **$35,000–$50,000**; true Palm Beach island/luxury-resort weddings routinely run **$100K–$300K+**.

**Takeaway:** Palm Beach island venues price 30–75% above the national average per guest; West Palm Beach and inland Palm Beach County are closer to (though still above) the Florida norm.

## 2. Venues

### Luxury oceanfront resorts (Palm Beach island & barrier islands)
- **The Breakers (Palm Beach)** — the region's flagship. Capacity **75–500**; **~$50,000 minimum spend**, typical range **$50K–$300K**; packages start ~**$399/person** plus venue fees; a 150-guest wedding realistically ~**$215,000** all-in (Tulle Together, PartySlate). Italian-Renaissance oceanfront ballrooms (Mediterranean, Venetian, Ponce de Leon), Ocean Lawn ceremonies. Books 12–18+ months out for winter Saturdays.
- **Eau Palm Beach Resort & Spa (Manalapan)** — Forbes Five-Star, 30,000+ sq ft of event space, capacity 10 to ~1,000. **Outdoor venue fees $6,000–$12,000 + 7% tax; F&B minimums $40,000–$120,000** + 7% tax + 24% service charge (Eau Palm Beach FAQ). Oceanfront lawn + grand ballroom.
- **Four Seasons Resort Palm Beach** — capacity up to **250**, 13 event spaces, hosts **one wedding at a time**; beachfront ceremonies + ballrooms (Four Seasons weddings pages). Expect Breakers-adjacent pricing; six-figure norms for full weekends.
- **The Boca Raton (Boca Raton Resort & Club) / Boca Raton Beach Club** — 200 waterfront acres, private beach, grand ocean-inspired ballroom (The Knot). Luxury tier.
- **Jupiter Beach Resort & Spa (Jupiter)** — the only true oceanfront resort in northern Palm Beach County; mid-luxury tier.
- **The Colony Hotel (Palm Beach)** — pink colonial icon off Worth Avenue; intimate, high-style Palm Beach weddings (The Scout Guide).

### Historic / museum / garden venues (West Palm Beach)
- **Flagler Museum (Whitehall, Palm Beach)** — Gilded Age mansion; access via membership tiers: **$5,000** (up to 50 guests), **$10,000** (up to 125), **$15,000** (up to 250), *plus* member event fees, catering, rentals on top.
- **Norton Museum of Art (West Palm Beach)** — full weddings from **$17,500 off-peak / $21,100 peak**, up to **400 seated** (Zola).
- **Ann Norton Sculpture Gardens (West Palm Beach)** — lush 2-acre garden estate on the Intracoastal; moderate pricing ($–$$ range per PartySpace); a favorite for garden-editorial weddings. Contact venue directly for current packages ((561) 832-5328).

### Waterfront / Intracoastal
- **Pelican Club (Jupiter, Lessing's Hospitality)** — waterfront, purpose-built wedding venue; **from ~$14,500 off-peak / $18,500 peak** full weddings (The Knot); smaller events from ~$3,000–$5,750 for 50 guests.
- **Wyndham Grand Jupiter at Harbourside Place** — Intracoastal rooftop/waterfront.
- **Out of the Blue Celebrations at Loggerhead Marina (Jupiter)** — marina/Intracoastal views.
- **Waterstone Resort & Marina (Boca Raton)** — Intracoastal boutique hotel.
- **The Seagate (Delray Beach)** — three venues, only Delray resort with private beach access.
- **Sundy House (Delray Beach)** — historic inn with tropical Taru Gardens; popular mid-range garden option (pricing on inquiry).

### Country clubs & golf resorts
- **PGA National Resort (Palm Beach Gardens)** — full-service golf resort weddings.
- **Breakers West Country Club (West Palm Beach)** — Breakers-run club at lower price point than the oceanfront flagship (The Knot).

**General benchmark:** Eventective lists 92+ venues in West Palm Beach alone; The Knot's "affordable" WPB list shows realistic venue-only rentals from roughly **$3,500–$12,000** outside the resort tier.

## 3. Seasonality & Timing

- **Peak season: November–April.** Dry season, low humidity, highs in the 70s–low 80s. **January–March are the premium months** — best weather, highest prices, venues and top vendors book **12–18+ months in advance** (Rimas Films, Priscila Del Cristo month-by-month guide).
- **Sweet spots: late October–November and April–early May.** Near-peak weather with somewhat better pricing/availability (Florida Weddings, Sugar Beach Weddings).
- **Hurricane season: June 1–November 30**, with **September–October the highest-risk window**. Direct hits are rare but insurance (wedding/event insurance with hurricane coverage) is strongly advised for any June–November date; ask venues about rebooking/force-majeure policies.
- **Summer (June–September):** hot (90s), very humid, near-daily afternoon thunderstorms — but deepest discounts and wide-open availability; plan indoor or sunset-timed events with rain backup.
- **Pricing effect:** peak-winter Saturday dates can run 20–40% above summer/off-peak at the same venue (see Pelican Club and Norton peak vs. off-peak spreads above); hotel room rates for guests also spike in Jan–Mar.
- **Lead times:** 12–18 months for peak-season Saturdays at name venues (The Breakers, Eau, Four Seasons); 8–12 months is workable for shoulder/off-peak or weekday dates. Photographers/planners at the top tier book on similar timelines.

## 4. Vendor Landscape & Pricing Tiers

- **Planners:** Handy Dallaire Events, Lausch Events, DTL EVNTS, Live N Lavish Events (WeddingWire/Wezoree top-rated). Tiers: month-of coordination ~$1,500–$3,500; partial planning $4,000–$8,000; full-service luxury $10,000–$25,000+ (often 10–15% of budget at the Breakers tier). Note: The Breakers and similar venues effectively require a professional planner.
- **Photographers:** Capehart Photography (40+ years of Palm Beach society weddings), Mark Salner Photography, Peper Love Photography, Joey G Photography. Tiers: $2,500–$3,500 entry-pro; **$3,500–$6,500 established South FL average**; $7,000–$15,000+ editorial/luxury names.
- **Florists:** Jill Heaton Floral Design and similar studio florists serve the luxury market; typical spend $2,000–$2,400 for standard weddings, $10K–$50K+ for resort-scale installations.
- **Caterers** (for non-resort venues like Flagler, Ann Norton, private estates): Corks + Forks Catering, Sabatinos Catering (West Palm Beach), Zest Kitchenz, Wicked Chefs, Made You Hungry (Yelp/The Knot/Zola listings). Off-site catering roughly $80–$150/person plus bar, staffing, and rentals; budget options from ~$20/pp exist for casual events (Camareras).
- **Music:** DJs $1,500–$3,000; live bands (standard for Palm Beach ballroom weddings) $5,000–$15,000+.

## 5. Logistics

### Florida marriage license (Palm Beach County Clerk & Comptroller)
- **Cost: $86.00 standard; $61.00 with premarital course** ($25 discount for completing a state-registered premarital preparation course, min. 4 hours, within 12 months of applying — online courses qualify and cost as little as ~$15–$20/couple). *(One source cited $93.50; the Clerk's published standard fee is $86 — verify at mypalmbeachclerk.com when applying.)*
- **Waiting period: 3 days for Florida residents — waived with the premarital course. No waiting period for out-of-state couples** (key for destination weddings).
- **Validity: 60 days** from issuance; ceremony must occur in Florida. Both parties appear in person with photo ID (and SSN if applicable); no blood test, no witnesses required on the license. Apply at any Clerk location, including the South County Courthouse, 200 W. Atlantic Ave., Delray Beach.

### Beach permits
- **Palm Beach County beaches: $50/day permit**, generally required only when a professional photographer/videographer or setup is involved (Gulf Beach Weddings / county guidance). Municipal beaches (Town of Palm Beach, Delray Beach, Boca Raton) each have their own rules on chairs, arches, guest counts, and alcohol (alcohol is broadly prohibited on public beaches) — confirm with the specific city's parks department. Small unstructured ceremonies often need no permit; anything with vendors/equipment does.

### Destination logistics for Northeast guests
- **Fly into PBI (Palm Beach International)** — 10–15 minutes from most WPB venues; far easier than FLL (45–60 min) or MIA (75–90 min). PBI has heavy Northeast nonstop service: **~63 weekly flights to New York-LGA alone**, plus JFK/EWR/HPN, and **~23 weekly nonstops to Boston** on JetBlue, Delta, and American (FlightsFrom.com, thepalmbeaches.com). Avelo/Breeze/Allegiant serve smaller Northeast cities.
- **Brightline** rail connects West Palm Beach to Fort Lauderdale/Miami/Orlando — useful for guests combining trips.
- **Hotel blocks:** reserve **9–12 months out for a January–April wedding** — winter is high season and Palm Beach room rates jump sharply (island hotels $600–$1,500+/night in peak winter). Budget-friendlier blocks: downtown West Palm Beach (The Ben, Hilton West Palm Beach, Canopy, Hyatt Place) at roughly $200–$400/night in season. Most hotels offer courtesy (no-attrition) blocks of 10–20 rooms.

## 6. What's Trending for 2025–2026 South Florida Weddings

(Sources: Bella Collina, Saint Patrick Palace 2026 forecast, Sun & Sea Beach Weddings, Jill Heaton Floral Design, SWFL Wedding Rentals)

- **Multi-day wedding weekends** — welcome party, wedding, recovery brunch; standard at the resort tier and a fit for destination guests flying in from the Northeast.
- **Hyper-personalization over "copy-paste" weddings** — custom monograms, signature scent/cocktails, personal storytelling in design.
- **Bold color is back:** monochromatic saturated palettes (deep berry, terracotta, electric blue) displacing all-neutral; though champagne/olive/blush still hold in classic Palm Beach ballrooms.
- **Sculptural/grounded florals:** meadow-style aisle plantings instead of arches; architectural greenery; fruit-and-taper-candle editorial tablescapes.
- **Editorial "paparazzi-style" photography** and content creators alongside the main photographer; the overly posed look is out.
- **Black-tie dress codes and sunset ceremony times** for the destination-glam look.
- **Experiential guest hospitality:** interactive food/bar moments (raw bars, espresso martini carts, cigar rollers — Palm Beach staples), elevated welcome bags.
- **Cakes:** textured sculpted buttercream (stucco, wave, knife-painted florals) over fondant.
- **"Destination-at-home":** couples choosing Palm Beach precisely to get a destination feel with easy Northeast access.

### Quick planning cheat sheet
- **Realistic budget:** $40K–$65K for a 100–125-guest non-resort wedding; $150K–$250K+ at The Breakers/Four Seasons/Eau tier.
- **Ideal dates:** late Jan–March (premium) or early Nov / late April (value); avoid Sept–Oct.
- **Book now for:** winter 2027–28 at flagship venues (12–18 mo. lead).
- **License:** $61 with online premarital course; no wait for out-of-staters; valid 60 days.
