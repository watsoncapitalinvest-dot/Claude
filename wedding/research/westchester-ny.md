# Research Brief: Getting Married in Westchester County, NY (2025–2026)

Prepared July 2026. Figures are drawn from current web sources (The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study covering 2025 weddings, The Wedding Report's 2025 Westchester market data, venue marketplace listings on The Knot/WeddingWire/Wedding Spot, and local planner/vendor blogs). Treat venue prices as starting points — nearly every Westchester venue quotes custom pricing by date, day of week, and guest count.

**Bottom line:** Westchester runs roughly **1.5–3x the national average**. A realistic planning band for a 125–150 guest wedding at a recognizable Westchester venue is **$60,000–$110,000**; budget-conscious couples can do it for $40,000–$50,000 at value venues or off-peak; luxury estate weddings routinely exceed $150,000. Westchester and Long Island carry premium pricing partly due to the regional tradition of large, formal celebrations (per Pix Wedding's NY cost analysis).

## 2. Venues: The Westchester Short List

Westchester's venue market breaks into five styles: **historic mansions/estates**, **castles**, **waterfront (Hudson River or Long Island Sound)**, **country clubs**, and **farm/barn** (mostly just north in the Hudson Valley). Prices below are per marketplace listings and venue pages; "pp" = per person, typically food + 5-hr open bar, before ~8.375% tax and service charge (usually 20–25%).

### Mansions & estates
- **Tappan Hill Mansion (Abigail Kirsch), Tarrytown** — the flagship. Former Mark Twain estate on a hilltop over the Hudson, 45 min from Midtown; recently renovated (new Promenade + Pavilion Ballroom/Terrace, per Westfair). Pavilion Room 150–250 guests; Hudson Room 75–150. **~$285–$295 pp + tax; full weddings from ~$20,000 off-peak / ~$25,000 peak** (per The Knot listing). Abigail Kirsch is the exclusive caterer. This is the benchmark high-end Westchester venue.
- **Lyndhurst (Tarrytown)** — Gothic Revival "castle" mansion, 67 riverside acres; tented Carriage House events up to ~300 guests. Pricing not published; plan on outside-caterer model (site fee + catering), typically a $60k+ all-in proposition.
- **Sleepy Hollow Country Club, Briarcliff Manor** — former Vanderbilt mansion with dramatic Hudson views, indoor/outdoor ceremony options, on-site accommodations; historic-elegant style. Pricing by inquiry.
- **Blue Hill at Stone Barns, Pocantico Hills** — farm-to-table icon (Dan Barber); the Hayloft event space at a working farm, ~80% of peak-season menu sourced from surrounding fields (per WeddingWire). One of the most expensive options in the county — commonly cited among the priciest wedding venues in the NY area; budget well into six figures for a full wedding.
- **Le Château, South Salem** — 1907 hilltop stone mansion on 32 acres in northern Westchester. **Weddings run ~$20,000–$50,000** before service fees/tax/gratuity (per the venue's own pricing page and The Knot).
- **The Garrison (Garrison, NY, Putnam County — 15 min north)** — modern glass venue with sweeping Hudson Highlands views; popular Westchester-adjacent choice. Pricing by inquiry (typically ~$200–$300 pp range historically).

### Castles
- **Whitby Castle at Rye Golf Club, Rye** — 1852 turn-of-the-century castle on 126 acres overlooking Long Island Sound. **Capacity ~250. Reception pricing from ~$112–$125 pp; "Complete Wedding Package" from ~$18,995 ($19,995 Saturday)** including 5-hr reception, top-shelf bar, in-house catering, no separate rental fee (per Tulle Together / Wedding Spot / The Knot). One of the best value-to-wow ratios in the county.
- **Castle Hotel & Spa, Tarrytown** — boutique castle hotel; smaller, luxe weddings with on-site rooms.

### Waterfront
- **Glen Island Harbour Club, New Rochelle** — landmark Long Island Sound waterfront venue on Glen Island Park; **up to 600 seated / 1,000 cocktail-style**; one-wedding-at-a-time. Reviews cite **~$105 pp off-season** quotes; peak Saturday pricing is meaningfully higher (per Yelp/The Knot). Classic glamorous ballroom-on-the-water style.
- **The Surf Club on the Sound, New Rochelle** — Sound-front ballrooms with terrace ceremonies; **listings show pricing "from $5,000"** (site fee basis) with per-person catering quoted directly (per Zola/The Knot).
- **Hudson-side options**: Abigail Kirsch's **Water Club-style** properties and Tarrytown waterfront event spaces (e.g., Hudson Loft, Washington Irving Boat Club area) serve the industrial/loft-on-the-river niche.

### Country clubs (many are non-member-accessible for weddings)
- **Trump National Westchester, Briarcliff Manor** — Grand Ballroom; **$3,500 ceremony fee, no separate reception rental with 5-hr event** (per The Knot); per-person catering by quote.
- **Westchester Country Club (Rye)**, **Brae Burn CC (Purchase)** — among the largest ballrooms in the county, **Old Oaks CC (Purchase)** — up to ~250 guests; pricing by inquiry.
- **Hampshire Country Club, Mamaroneck** — **~$166 pp off-peak with ~$16,500 minimum** (per Wedding Spot) — a relative value play on the Sound shore.

### Rules of thumb
- All-inclusive per-person venues (Whitby, Glen Island, country clubs): **$110–$200 pp** gets you in the door off-peak; **$180–$300 pp** for premium venues/peak Saturdays.
- Estate/raw-space venues (Lyndhurst, historic sites): add site fee ($8,000–$20,000) + off-premise catering ($150–$250 pp via Abigail Kirsch, X&O, Great Performances tier).
- Saturday-night minimums at top venues frequently imply **$50,000–$75,000+ food-and-beverage spend** before photo, flowers, music.

## 3. Seasonality & Timing

- **Peak months:** In the New York market, **September is the single biggest month (~23% of weddings), followed by June (15%) and May (14%)** (per Carats & Cake's real-weddings data). Late September–October in Westchester is its own foliage-driven super-peak — Hudson River views + fall color make October Saturdays the hardest dates in the county.
- **Weather:** June is warm and reliable (highs ~80°F); July–August are hot and humid (85–90°F, thunderstorm risk — relevant for tented/outdoor estates); September is the sweet spot (70s, lower humidity) but carries residual tropical-storm-remnant risk; peak foliage in Westchester is typically **mid-to-late October**; November–March is off-season (indoor mansions and ballrooms still show beautifully).
- **Pricing swing:** Peak-season Saturday minimums run **30–50% higher** than the same venue on an off-peak winter Saturday (per Love & Lustre / planner guides). Concrete example: Tappan Hill starts ~$20,000 off-peak vs ~$25,000 peak; Whitby's Saturday package carries a ~$1,000 premium over other nights. Fridays and Sundays typically save 15–30%; weekday weddings can save ~35% (per WedStay 2026 trends).
- **Booking lead times:** For a September/October Saturday in the NY market, plan **14–24 months out** for venue and top photographers; assume the best peak dates are gone inside 12 months (per Bliss & Bone / planner guidance). Off-peak dates (Nov–Apr) can often be booked 6–9 months out with strong vendors still available.
- Note: September–early October also collides with the High Holidays — check the calendar both for venue availability and guest conflicts (per local Westchester planning guides).

## 4. Vendor Landscape & Pricing Tiers

- **Caterers:** **Abigail Kirsch** is the dominant Westchester name (exclusive at Tappan Hill; off-premise across the tri-state), widely regarded as the county's top caterer (per Westchester Magazine). NYC caterers (Great Performances, etc.) regularly work Westchester estates. Expect **$150–$300 pp** all-in for premium off-premise catering with bar and staff.
- **Photographers:** ~72% of Westchester photographers rate 4+ stars on The Knot. Tiers: budget **$895–$1,700** (Lily & Lime, Melo Photo + Video, Lensoftrinh), mid **$2,900–$3,600** (the Westchester average per The Wedding Report), premium **$3,900–$8,000+** (Rob Spring Photography from $3,900; Allan E. Levine from $4,500; editorial NYC studios higher).
- **Florists:** typical Westchester spend **~$2,500**; full-service floral design for estate weddings runs $8,000–$20,000+. (Local names surface via The Knot/WeddingWire "Best of Westchester" lists.)
- **Music:** DJ **$1,550–$1,900**; live band **$4,350–$5,300** locally, with NYC society bands (Hank Lane–tier, common at Glen Island/country clubs) at $8,000–$15,000+.
- **Planners:** Day-of/month-of coordination from ~$1,100–$3,000; **full-service from ~$5,000 to $10,000+** (per The Bash/GigSalad and local planner pages). Notable local firms: **Kelli Teglas Events** (White Plains), **LCR Events** (Amawalk), **Rose Ahn Events** (Westchester/NYC).

## 5. Logistics

### NY marriage license (per NY State Dept. of Health / NYC City Clerk)
- **Cost:** **$40** from any town/city clerk outside NYC ($35 within NYC). A license from any NY clerk is valid anywhere in the state — you do not need to get it in Westchester.
- **Both parties must appear in person** with photo ID (and know details of any prior marriages); no blood test; no residency or citizenship requirement.
- **24-hour waiting period** between issuance and ceremony (waivable only by a judge).
- **Valid for 60 days** (180 for active-duty military), starting the day after issuance — so get it 1–8 weeks before the wedding.
- **One witness (18+)** required at the ceremony, plus a registered officiant.

### Guest travel & hotels
- Airports: Westchester County (HPN) is in-county; LGA/JFK ~35–60 min; EWR ~1 hr. Metro-North gets NYC guests to Tarrytown, White Plains, Rye, or New Rochelle in 35–45 min from Grand Central.
- **Hotel-block staples:** The Ritz-Carlton Westchester (White Plains, 146 rooms), The Opus Westchester, Tarrytown House Estate (weddings + lodging on one property; note it does not run its own shuttle), Castle Hotel & Spa, plus Marriott/Hilton/DoubleTree properties in Tarrytown and White Plains. White Plains hotels cluster within blocks of each other, which makes multi-pickup shuttles easy, and most Tarrytown-area hotels are a **10–20 minute shuttle** from Tappan Hill, Lyndhurst, and similar venues (per Danielle Caldwell Events' Westchester hotel-block guide). Some hotels offer courtesy (no-attrition) blocks — ask.
- **Transportation:** Charter shuttles are near-standard for estate venues with limited parking (Lyndhurst, Blue Hill, Sleepy Hollow CC). Budget ~$1,500–$3,000 for evening shuttle loops; local operators (e.g., Silver Star Transportation) publish Westchester/Hudson Valley venue route experience.

## 6. Current Trends (2025–2026, NY metro)

Per The Knot's 2025 study, Zola's 2025 First Look Report, and NY planner trend reports (BLB Events, Rose Ahn, Ashley AV):
- **Multi-day "wedding weekends":** ~31% of couples now host a welcome party and ~22% a farewell brunch on top of the rehearsal dinner (Zola 2025) — a natural fit for Tarrytown, where hotels, venues, and Main Street are walkable/shuttle-able.
- **Micro-luxury and weekday weddings:** micro weddings (<50 guests) up ~23% YoY into 2026; weekday dates save ~35% (WedStay). Restaurant buyouts and chef's-table formats (Blue Hill energy) are surging.
- **Experience-driven, personality-forward design:** bold color and pattern-play over all-white minimalism; textured layers; immersive guest moments; editorial/documentary-style photography (2026 NY Bridal Fashion Week + planner reports).
- **Warm candlelit lighting:** amber uplighting mimicking candlelight is the dominant NYC-metro ballroom look for 2026 (Ashley AV).
- **Celebration despite economic jitters:** The Knot's 2025 study theme — couples are protecting guest-experience spend (food, bar, music) and trimming favors/paper.
- **Local flavor:** Hudson River/foliage October dates, farm-to-table menus, and NYC-couple "escape to the Hudson Valley" weddings keep Westchester's fall market the most competitive stretch of the calendar.

### Quick planning takeaways
1. **Budget $60k–$110k** for a classic 125–150 guest Westchester wedding at a named venue; $40k–$55k is achievable off-peak at per-person venues like Whitby or Glen Island off-season.
2. **Book 14–24 months out** for a Sept/Oct Saturday; consider Friday/Sunday or November for 15–35% savings at the same venues.
3. **Best value plays:** Whitby Castle package (~$19k base), Glen Island off-season (~$105 pp quotes), Hampshire CC (~$166 pp off-peak); **splurge tier:** Tappan Hill (~$285–295 pp), Blue Hill at Stone Barns.
4. **License is easy:** $40, any NY clerk, 24-hr wait, 60-day window, one witness.
