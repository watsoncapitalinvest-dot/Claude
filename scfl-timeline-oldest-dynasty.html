Where SCFL Actually Falls: 1962 to Now

By The Record Room · Plotted from the oldest-dynasty-league research · 1962–2026

Fantasy football itself, SCFL's own start, and every rival dynasty league this research turned up, on one line — plus the one personal date that puts it all in real perspective: the year the core group graduated. Where a rival's exact founding year isn't confirmed, it's marked as a range or estimate rather than a fixed point — the goal here is an honest picture, not a clean one.

Every Point, In Order

①
1962
Fantasy football is invented Context
Bill Winkenbach and two writers create the GOPPPL in Oakland — 8 teams, AFL-only, straight redraft. Not dynasty format. The true ceiling on "how old could any fantasy claim be" — 43 years before SCFL, and 25 years before the oldest dynasty-format claim this research found.
②
1987
Mega Football League founded Rival
Kingman, Kansas. Independently described as "a Dynasty League" — the oldest rival claim found, and the only one where the word "Dynasty" came from the source itself. Team count never confirmed despite repeated searches.
③
1990
The 16-team rival league founded Rival
Footballguys forum. SCFL's exact size — 16 teams — with, per the poster, all 16 original owners still in it as of a 2023 post. The size-and-age match that matters most for SCFL's own claim.
④
~1985–96
"Oldest running dynasty league" thread — DLF forum Rival, undated
Posted to a Dynasty League Football thread with that exact title: original title roster had Marcus Allen and Jerry Rice, pen-and-paper record-keeping before spreadsheets, 10 stable owners for 17 years. Real texture, but this desk could not date the post itself — shown here as a range, not a point, because pinning an exact year would overstate what's actually known.
⑤
~1993
Two more rival leagues founded Rival
A 14-team league (11 of 14 original owners) and a 10-team league (5 of 10 original owners), both from the same footballguys thread, both stated as "31st year" in an August 27, 2023 post — backed out to a ~1993 founding.
⑥
1993–94
MyFantasyLeague's underlying tech is built Context
Kevin Austin and Mike Hall's tools — one of the earliest web platforms able to support a persistent, dynasty-style league. Not a league itself, but proof the format was technically possible over a decade before SCFL started.
⑦
1998
We graduate Personal milestone
Seven years before SCFL's first season. For scale: the oldest confirmed-dynasty rival (Mega Football League, 1987) was already in its 11th season by the time this group graduated.
⑧
2005
SCFL is founded SCFL
Confirmed by four independent internal record files — records.json, league-titles.json, dossier.json, and scfl-franchises.json — all landing on the same year. 21 completed seasons on the books.
⑨
2006
Dynasty League Football's forum founded Context
The format's oldest dedicated public community hub. SCFL's 2005 start predates the point "dynasty" had an organized name and community around it at all — by a year.
⑩
2023
The footballguys thread is posted Context
August 27, 2023 — the source date behind three of the five rival claims (the 16-team, 14-team, and 10-team leagues). Their "year N" figures are snapshots from this date, not live status three years later.
⑪
2026
Today — SCFL's 22nd season SCFL
The only entry on this entire timeline confirmed active right now, as far as this research could establish. None of the five rival claims (points ②③④⑤) have been confirmed still running.
How this was built: every date is plotted to scale on one linear 1962–2026 axis — no breaks, no compression. Where a founding year was stated directly in a source (1962, 1987, 1990, 2005, 2006), it's plotted as a point. Where a year had to be computed from a relative claim ("31st year" as of a dated post), it's marked "~" and the computation is shown in the card. Where no date could be established at all, it's drawn as a dashed range, not a point.
Sourcing: matches the full research packet and magazine article on this topic — records.json, league-titles.json, dossier.json, scfl-franchises.json internally; live web search externally, including the confirmed August 27, 2023 post date for the footballguys thread.
