Market Laws — how SCFL actually prices assets

Players buy picks. The vast majority of pick trades are player-for-pick, not pick-for-pick.
Current mid-1st ≈ a future 1st. A pick in hand this year trades flat for next year’s first.
Top-10 pick = a genuine star or a startable Superflex QB. Below that you’re buying upside.
Early 2nd (17–22) ≈ a solid vet starter. That’s the Hubbard / early-RB2 band.
~5 slots of climb in the 20s ≈ one future 2nd. Cheap to move up late.
Late 2nd (25–32) ≈ depth vet or a future 2nd. The league’s loose change.

The red line is the tell: in July only 18% of pick currency is future picks — everyone’s trading this year’s spot picks before the draft. By September–November the draft is over, so 93–100% of every pick that moves is a future pick. Spot picks have a two-month shelf life; future picks are the year-round money.
