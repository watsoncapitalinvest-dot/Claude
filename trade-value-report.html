Market Laws — how SCFL actually prices assets

Players buy picks. The vast majority of pick trades are player-for-pick, not pick-for-pick.
Current mid-1st ≈ a future 1st. A pick in hand this year trades flat for next year’s first.
Top-10 pick = a genuine star or a startable Superflex QB. Below that you’re buying upside.
Early 2nd (17–22) ≈ a solid vet starter. That’s the Hubbard / early-RB2 band.
~5 slots of climb in the 20s ≈ one future 2nd. Cheap to move up late.
Late 2nd (25–32) ≈ depth vet or a future 2nd. The league’s loose change.

THE SUPERFLEX QB TAX — the single most important price in your league

Because you start two QBs, quarterbacks are the most expensive assets in the SCFL — the ledger proves it. Dak (aging) fetched two future firsts (~$110). Goff in his MVP year cost picks 9 + 25 + a future first (~$158). Jimmy G — a mid QB1 — still cost Kamara plus a pick (~$85). A merely-startable QB1 is worth more than any pick outside the top 5. You are thin at QB: expect to pay the tax, and pay it early — the price only rises as the league’s startable QBs dry up.

How to Price a Trade Idea — add up both sides, then apply three adjustments

Within 8% — dead fair, do it 8–18% — a winner, still gets done Over 18% — a fleecing / veto zone

1 · The Superflex QB premium

A QB is worth +15–25% over grid to a team that needs to fill an SF slot. Paying “too much” in raw dollars for a QB you start every week is not an overpay — it’s the tax. That team is you.

2 · Timeline tilt

Contenders pay +10–20% for win-now vets; rebuilders discount any player 30+ by 20–40% and pay up for youth and picks. Price the deal from the other owner’s window, not just yours.

3 · Consolidation premium

The single best player in a deal carries +10–15% — one elite roster spot beats two good ones. It’s why Kupp cost three firsts, not two, and why packaging picks to move up always costs a little extra.

4 · Age & role haircuts

Off the grid: RB over 27 −20%, WR over 30 −15%, QB over 34 −10% (SF softens it). Committee/backup roles sit at the bottom of their tier; locked-in every-week roles at the top.

Worked example — the real Mahomes/Lamar swap
Machines give: Mahomes ($105) + pick 6 ($74) = $179
NWO give: Lamar ($118) + pick 20 ($45) = $163
Machines paid ~$16 (9%) to slide down the pick board but get younger at the game’s premium position. Inside the fair band — a defensible win-now-vs-age bet, not a fleece. This is the QB tax in action.
Worked example — your picks 15 & 16
Your ammo: pick 15 ($53) + pick 16 ($51) = $104
Top of board: 1.01 ($100) · 1.02 ($93) · 1.03 ($87)
Your back-to-back firsts equal a top-3 pick on paper. Add the consolidation tax and 15+16 realistically lands you around 1.03–1.04; toss a future 2nd to reach 1.01/1.02. If there’s one guy you love, you have the ammo to go get him. If not — two swings beat one.
