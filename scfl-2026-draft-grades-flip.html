Heavy Hitters
B+
1.01RBJeremiyah Love Cardinals
1.09WROmar Cooper Jr. Jets
2.03WRZachariah Branch Falcons
2.11WRTed Hurst Buccaneers

The Hitters showed a lot of faith in their young Ward/Dart QB tandem by leaving Mendoza on the board and drafting RB Jeremiyah Love. Love is the only true elite talent in this year’s draft who is explosive with the moves to match. Will he be the player that leads the Hitters out of the SCFL basement?

Back up at 9 the Hitters had to be excited that 1st round WR Cooper was still available. His strong hands and ability to make players miss make him an exciting player. The Hitters will unfortunately push him into a big role before the Jets, but he should be a solid WR3 for years to come if the Jets can sort out their QB situation and find ways to use him.

A major portion of this draft after pick 9 was picking a WR drafted in rounds 2-4 and hoping you find a Puka Nacua type diamond in the rough. The Hitters took two shots at this, but with a crowded WR room already it may have been wiser to take a shot at a RB with pick 19. Branch is an extremely athletic WR who was highly touted heading into USC, but outside of kick returns the big time plays never materialized at USC/Georgia. He’s extremely fast, but very small. Hurst is the more exciting prospect if he can carry his game over from Georgia State to the NFL. He’s got the size and tracking ability to make a difference.

🍦
Still the Creamiest
A+
1.02QBFernando Mendoza Raiders
1.04RBJadarian Price Seahawks

Creams had to be psyched to see Mendoza sitting there at 2. Solidifying your QB room is key in the SCFL and Creams hopes the future is bright now with Mendoza-Love. The Heisman winner is smart, accurate, and confident. The mobile Bernie Kosar comparisons should excite the Creams fans who haven’t had much to look forward to lately.

WR Scout showed great restraint by skipping over a couple exciting WR prospects and taking RB Jadarian Price. He’s well built for the NFL, works hard, and has great acceleration. Creams is going to have to work on his hands in the receiving game and hope he can handle a larger workload than he did in college.

Killer Klowns
A-
1.03WRCarnell Tate Titans
2.02WRGermie Bernard Steelers

Klowns established his Nix-Daniels QB room two years ago so seeing Mendoza go wasn’t a problem. Carnell Tate instantly becomes the top WR on the Klowns. Is that a role Tate can handle? He was never relied upon to be the #1 in college, so will he be able to handle that in the NFL. The next in line of the great Ohio State WRs, Tate has great hands, awareness, and balance. A solid addition for Klowns if he can remain a consistent WR2 in the future.

Germie Bernard is a solid swing at that WR pool. A Michigan State/Washington/Alabama athlete Germie learned alongside a lot of NFL talent. Will he be able to get enough action alongside Metcalf and Pittman in Pittsburgh to be a weapon for the Klowns? Often described as “just a good football player” The Klowns hope he is a solid WR3/backup for years to come.

🐉
The Smoke Dragons
C+
1.15WRMalachi Fields Giants
2.06QBDrew Allar Steelers

Did fandom play a role in the Fields selection? On the one hand if Fields reaches his high end comp of Michael Pittman that’s a solid pick at 15. On the other hand Giants fans are surely hoping that Malik Nabers returns to elite form, which caps Fields production. 75% of Fields catches were for a first down or touchdown, so Dragons fans can hope he becomes a Dart favorite with his size going up for the ball in the endzone.

Allar was a 5 star QB with a lot of buzz around him heading into Penn State. He was the leader of a national championship contender until everything fell apart and he broke his ankle last fall. Allar has always had the physical tools, but he never developed as a QB. Will staying in state and developing behind Aaron Rodgers be what Allar needs to finally live up to his potential? The Dragons sure hope so with a shaky QB room. Considering the WR room is full of a bunch of guys who are ready to retire or get cut the Dragons may have wanted to take two dives into the WR pool.

Wookie Leaks
A
1.05WRJordyn Tyson Saints
1.10RBJonah Coleman Broncos
1.11RBNicholas Singleton Titans

The Wookie Leaks has to be pleased with how things fell position wise, but also a little disappointed with the level of talent in this year’s draft. Creams RB need dropped Tyson, a top 10 pick, right into his lap. If Tyson can stay healthy he has the talent to create a 1A/1B situation in New Orleans with Chris Olave. Tyson has the ability to get open and drew Stefan Diggs comparisons, which is exciting if he can just remain healthy.

In most drafts if you came away with the 3rd and 4th RB off the board you would be ecstatic, but the talent this year is questionable. Wookie definitely took the two you would want here, but these are 4th and 5th round picks. Singleton was a 5 star stud heading into Penn State, but he was never able to break away from his classmate Kaytron Allen and at a point became Allen’s backup. He was able to break Saquon Barkley’s all purpose yards record and touchdown record with an extra year at Penn State. If he can finally turn his talent into an NFL caliber RB Tennessee presents a good opportunity. Jonah Coleman feels like another swing at RB for the Broncos. Dobbins, Harvey….Will Coleman finally be the hit? Even if he does hit, Coleman doesn’t posses that home run ability. He could be a solid RB2 with his rushing and catching ability. Hitting on both of these RBs would be a huge step in the right direction because outside of the WR room things are shaky with the franchise.

🦫
The Beaver Eaters
D
2.05RBMike Washington Raiders
2.07WRSkylar Bell Bills
2.10TEOscar Delp Saints

Historically one of the best drafters in the SCFL, the Beaver Eaters were put to the test with three late picks and a Friday work-night draft. Mike Washington out of Utica, NY is the right RB to take here, but will he get the opportunity in Vegas to be more than just depth in a really good Beavers RB room. Washington was the fastest RB at the combine which is impressive at 223 pounds. He’s not afraid to take a beating and has shown flashes of big play ability in the rushing and receiving department.

The Skylar Bell and Oscar Delp selections are baffling! Unless there is a trade, one of these two should probably be cut to make room for a kicker. Bell, out of the Bronx, had 24 touchdowns at UConn, but also had 24 drops. He doesn’t stand out in any area. Delp is not from NY and he failed to take over the lead role after Brock Bowers left Georgia. He does have the physical tools to develop into a better pro, but do the Eaters have the time to wait for him in New Orleans as they are one of the top contenders in the SCFL?

Hairy Gumbas
C
1.08QBTy Simpson Rams
2.15RBJordan James 49ers

The selection of Ty Simpson seems like a reminder of the painful cycle the Gumbas are in. Tyreek, Chubb, Kittle, and Stafford are beginning to age out and it’s time to replace them. Will they warn Caleb, Egbuka, and Pierce of the hurt of rising up to division champ/SCFL runner up only to be left aging on a dying Gumbas roster? A selection of Omar Cooper or a trade of the 8th pick could have presented the Gumba legends, Stafford and Kittle, with a shot to go for it one more time with those youngsters. After only starting for one year Simpson was a first round pick to the Superbowl favorite Rams. How long does he need to develop behind Stafford? Described as a less athletic Daniel Jones, he doesn’t give Gumbas fans a lot to be excited about in the short term. He is a leader and extremely smart so if he does become the starter in 3 years, Gumbas fans may look back fondly at this pick.

Second year player Jordan James at pick 31 makes sense if he can earn the backup role behind Christian McCaffrey. A productive runner at Oregon, James doesn’t have the big play speed, but he is a well rounded RB. James could be a productive back for the Gumbas if he can carve out a role in San Francisco.

Lil’ Chops
B-
2.09RBEmmett Johnson Chiefs

Emmett had an incredible senior season at Nebraska with close to 2,000 all-purpose yards and 15 touchdowns. Projected as a change of pace back, can Johnson carve out enough of a role in Kansas City to make a difference for the Chops? He has the skills to develop a nice pass catching situation with Mahomes. The Chops have built a solid RB room so they can have patience with Johnson, but they don’t want to be stockpiling duds.

New World Order
B
1.06WRMakai Lemon Eagles
1.07WRKC Concepcion Browns
1.16WRAntonio Williams Commanders

Grabbing two first round receivers in a draft looks good at first glance, but what was the cost? Is the 5 year age difference worth the risk in giving up Waddle? Then going back and diving into the WR pool again has created a WR Scout level overflow in the NWO. Our guess is that NWO will be looking to balance out that 9-4 WR-RB ratio.

Lemon models his game after Amon-Ra (a tremendous NWO draft pick) and his draft stock soared thanks to St. Brown’s success in the NFL. The Biletnikoff award winner is a smooth route runner who should immediately gain a role in the Eagles offense.

Concepcion shined at NC State and Texas A&M. He glides on the field which allows him to get open and make plays with the ball in his hands. His biggest issue will be the Browns inability to find a franchise QB.

Things didn’t go as planned for Antonio Williams at Clemson. A big time recruit Williams was an immediate contributor and earned Freshmen All American honors. His last three years were a disappointment though as he failed to top the excitement of that year. He has excellent body control and will look to immediately become a threat alongside Terry McLaurin in Washington.

💀
The Machines
B+
2.01WRChris Bell Dolphins
2.04WRDe’ Zhaun Stribling 49ers
2.12RBKaytron Allen Commanders

We all thought we would live under the Mahomes-Jefferson reign for a long time, but the Machines had different plans. The start of the Lamar-Puka era saw the Machines with only 3 second round picks. That’s never a problem for the #1 drafter in the SCFL though.

The Machines took two shots at the second tier WR pool. Bell jumps into a disgusting Dolphins WR room that hopefully won’t rush him back from his ACL injury sustained In November. Bell was big, strong, and explosive prior to the injury and machines fans hope he can return to that form by the end of this season. Stribling joins a 49ers team still looking for a true #1 receiver. He is strong with steady hands, but he needs to work on getting open if he wants to succeed in the NFL.

The steal of the draft could very well turn out to be Kaytron Allen at pick 28. Singleton was the more gifted recruit at Penn State, but Allen overtook him because he had better vision and just an overall better feel for the position. Can Allen do the same in Washington where they lack a true #1 RB?

Big Blue
A
1.12TEKenyon Sadiq Jets

Big Blue has set up there roster to gain the luxury of taking best player available. The final first round pick on the board falls to them at 12 and they build depth at the TE position. Sadiq is an athletic freak with both speed and strength who teammates say is the toughest player to cover. He’s an explosive play waiting to happen when he gets the ball in his hands. Sadiq’s biggest issue is he hasn’t played a lot and will the Jets be able to utilize and develop him properly. Big Blue can afford to wait as Sadiq adjusts to the NFL.
