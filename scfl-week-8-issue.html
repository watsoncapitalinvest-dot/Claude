Kupp Wins It Late for Smoke Dragons
Smoke Dragons 105.1, StillTheCream 97.74. 7.4 points in it.

Nobody could put this away, which is the polite version. Smoke Dragons were behind this football game until the fourth quarter and won it 105.1 to 97.74, which tells you most of what you need and none of how it felt.

That put Smoke Dragons in front at 103.9–97.74, and for all the traffic that had come before it, nobody took it back. There were 10 touchdowns in this game and the one that mattered went Smoke Dragons’s way last.
