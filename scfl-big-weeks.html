As we go to press

Beaver Eaters are on 177 in week 6 with players still to play — 7 points short of the record on this page. They may well take it tonight.

They will not take the other one. The league is averaging 96.6 a week this season, so 177 is 1.83× the field. Tenth place on the adjusted board is 1.98×, which in this scoring environment is 191 points. First place is 2.28× — 220.

That is the argument of this piece, happening live: a number can be the biggest ever recorded and not be among the ten best weeks anybody has had.

The biggest score anybody has ever put up in this league is 184, by BIG BLUE in week 13 of 2022, against Powers of Pain, who managed 99. That is the record and it is not in dispute.

What is in dispute is whether it was the best week anybody has had. The average team week in this league was 78.5 points in 2011 and 101.2 in 2018. Those are different sports. A number put up against the first one is worth considerably more than the same number put up against the second, and the record book has never said so.

THE LEGION OF DOOM did not score the most points. They beat their league by more than anybody ever has.
One Franchise, Five Entries

5 of the ten biggest weeks in league history belong to The Machines — 2013, 2014, 2016, 2018, 2021. No other franchise has more than one. Whatever else the record book says about that team, it says they have been capable of an afternoon nobody else can reach, across 8 years and every scoring era this league has had.
