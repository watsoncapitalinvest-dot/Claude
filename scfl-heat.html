Where The Arguments Are

Sixteen managers, a hundred and twenty pairings, and a very dumb list of twenty-four words. This is the whole of it.

A volley is two managers speaking back to back, in the same room: one posts, a different one answers inside 7 minutes. There are 146,582 of them in the league record. A volley is heated if either of its two messages contains one of twenty-four strings on a fixed list — no sentiment model, no cleverness, no judgement about who started it.

5,399 volleys trip the list, which is 3.7 per cent of everything ever said. Below is where they sit, and then the list itself, so you can argue with the list instead of with the chart.
