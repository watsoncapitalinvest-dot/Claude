# SCFL SPORTSCENTER — Video Production Bible (Google AI Studio / Veo)

Workflow: ~8s clips with native spoken audio. Attach harry-hero-source.png to EVERY
generation as the character reference and paste the three blocks below into every
prompt. Game highlights are NOT generated — screen-record the engine tape
(broadcast-demo.html?tv=1&hl=1&n=6&spd=2&wk=N&h=KEY&a=KEY). No text in generations;
all graphics overlaid in post from repo assets (scfl-network-emblem.png, award
backgrounds, matchup boards screenshotted from the show).

## BLOCK 1 — STYLE
Stylized 3D-animated premium sports broadcast, the look of a high-end animated
sports promo: cartoon-realism, warm cinematic TV lighting, shallow depth of field,
subtle film grain, rich golds and deep navy tones, prime-time energy. Fixed
broadcast studio camera. No text, no captions, no graphics, no watermarks.
(Photoreal variant: swap first sentence for "Live-action premium sports broadcast
footage, shot on broadcast cameras".)

## BLOCK 2 — CHARACTER
HARRY DOYLE (match the attached reference image exactly): heavyset American sports
anchor, late 60s, warm suntanned face, deep smile lines, thick white-gray hair
swept back, dark eyebrows. Navy blazer over a cream Hawaiian shirt with green palm
leaves and orange flowers, open collar, no tie, small red-white-blue shield badge
on the chest. Gold wristwatch, gold ring. Lowball glass of whiskey with ice at his
right hand. VOICE: gravelly, cheerful, wry American sports broadcaster in his 60s,
quick deadpan delivery, slightly amused, never shouting.

## BLOCK 3 — STUDIO
Moody premium sports TV studio at night. Curved dark-wood anchor desk with gold
trim and glowing gold front panel. Wall of three large dark monitors with thin
glowing gold edges, gold rim-lighting on navy walls, faint city skyline through a
window, polished reflective dark floor, overhead spotlights. Palette: charcoal,
metallic gold, deep red, navy.

## SHOT GRAMMAR
SHOT A medium straight-on desk · SHOT B chest-up close, low angle · SHOT C
three-quarter side with monitor wall + skyline · SHOT D glass business (toast/drink).

## EPISODE FORMAT (per episode, ~6 min)
Cold open (2 clips) -> featured games (2 clips + tape each) -> quick-scores wall
clip (+1 gag clip) -> awards (3 clips, Stud gold card / Dud crimson card GFX) ->
sign-off (2 clips, ends on SHOT D toast). Dialogue per clip: max ~22 words.
Pull each week's lines from the show engine (SEGS dump) and trim to 8s bites.
Episode 1 full script lives in the chat log from 2026-07-27; regenerate any week
via scfl-sportscenter.html?week=N and condense the desk lines the same way.
