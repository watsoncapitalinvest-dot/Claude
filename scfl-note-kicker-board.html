The Kicker Is The Easiest Fix On The Roster

Start with the rule this league's settings create. A missed field goal costs nothing. A missed extra point costs nothing. So a kicker is not an accuracy position here, he is a volume and distance position: every attempt is free money and a fifty-yarder pays five where a thirty-yarder pays three. The kicker you want is on an offence that moves the ball and then stops.

The median kicker week in this league pays 8.0 and the mean is 8.7, which puts the slot ahead of both wide receiver and tight end. It is not a throwaway seat, and it is the cheapest one on the roster to improve, because a kicker swap costs nothing but the claim.

The problem

Mevis is not inaccurate — 5 from 7, with three of those from 40 to 49, is respectable work. He is starved. Seven field goal attempts in four games is the problem, and he has not had a single look from fifty, which is where the money is. A kicker with no volume and no distance in a format that charges nothing for missing is the one player on a roster who is underperforming for reasons that have nothing to do with him.

The fix, and it is free
Ten from ten, three of them from fifty, and nobody owns him.

Chase McLaughlin is the claim. Ten field goals from ten attempts, three of them from fifty and out, ten extra points, and a median week of 11 against Mevis's 5.5. On the averages it is 9.6 against 7.0; on the medians it is twice the man. Over the nine weeks left in the regular season the mean gap alone is better than twenty points, which is more than any defence on the wire would move this roster.

If somebody takes him first, Andre Szmyt is the same profile a notch down — ten from ten with four between 40 and 49 — and Cairo Santos has a 10.0 median on fourteen extra points, which is the other way to get there: a kicker attached to an offence that keeps scoring touchdowns.

Two things worth knowing

Ryan Fitzgerald has the best mean of any free kicker at 10.5 and is 9 from 9, but on four games. Carolina had not played week five when this was built, so there is one more data point coming before he should be trusted over McLaughlin's five.

Spencer Shrader, at 15.0 a game, is the best kicker in the league by a street and belongs to the Beaver Eaters — who put up 176.4 last week, 21 of it from him. Kickers are not a rounding error in this format.

A note on how this was counted

The first version of this board had Mevis in Philadelphia, Fairbairn in Tennessee and Dicker in Denver. The play-by-play sets the possession team on a kickoff to the team receiving it, so reading a kicker's side off every row he appears on labels each man with whoever he last kicked off to. Only field goals and extra points identify his own team. The scoring was never affected — it is counted per kicker, not per team — but every label on the board was wrong until it was.

How this was worked out. Every kicker in the NFL scored through this league's settings — 3 for a field goal inside 40, 4 from 40 to 49, 5 from 50 and out, 1 an extra point, and nothing at all for a miss — counted out of nflverse play-by-play. Checked against what Sleeper actually paid the seventeen owned kickers in weeks one to four: 63 of 63 weeks match exactly, so unlike the defence board these figures carry no error bar.
