Kickoff
Two fictional squads, one real box score. Hit Kick off and the engine reconstructs how the fantasy points piled up — play by play.
How this works: a deterministic engine owns the truth — the clock, the score, and every scoring event, all summed exactly from the box score (Model A: the scorebug tracks fantasy points; the field action is the story of how they got there). The play-by-play is written on top of validated state, never the other way around. Swap in a real matchup and it plays the same way.
