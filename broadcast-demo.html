Real SCFL rosters, one simulated Sunday. Hit Kick off — the offenses take the field in formation and the broadcast shows how the fantasy points pile up.
How this works: a deterministic engine owns the truth — score, clock and every scoring event, summed exactly from the box score (Model A: the bug tracks fantasy points; the field is the story). The 60 minutes are hashed into alternating possessions; each drive the offense lines up in a real formation — your fantasy starters at the skill spots, generic linemen and defenders around them — and the line of scrimmage marches by each play's yardage. Swap in a real matchup and it replays the same way.
