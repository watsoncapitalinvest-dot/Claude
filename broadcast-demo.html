Two fictional squads, one real box score. Hit Kick off — the teams trade possessions and the engine shows how the fantasy points pile up.
How this works: a deterministic engine owns the truth — score, clock and every scoring event, summed exactly from the box score (Model A: the bug tracks fantasy points; the field is the story). The 60 minutes are hashed into alternating possessions — one team drives, then the other — so it loosely follows game time even though fantasy is all offense. Swap in a real matchup and it replays the same way.
