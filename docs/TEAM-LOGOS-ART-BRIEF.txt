TEAM LOGOS — FIVE REPLACEMENT CRESTS
Square, 1:1, 800x800, JPG or PNG. Digital-illustration team-crest style —
bold black outlines, dramatic comic/tattoo-flash linework, rich saturated
color, a little grit and shading, NOT a flat corporate icon and NOT a photo.
Match the house style already on file for this league's other crests
(Smoke Dragons, Guido Haters, Killer Klowns, Pork Chop Express): a circular
or shield-shaped emblem, mascot or scene filling the badge, the team name
worked into the design as bold integrated lettering (not a caption below
it) — banner ribbon, arched text, or a name worked into the composition.
Background: a single flat, solid color (any color) filling every corner
right up to the edge of the badge — no gradient, texture, vignette, or
scene bleeding into the corners. The app keys out the background
automatically by sampling the corners, so a clean flat fill is what makes
that work; a busy or gradient background will leave visible edges.

These five are currently placeholders (a generic default avatar icon, two
of them literally identical) standing in for real crests. Same format for
all five, one crest each:

---

BIG BLUE (owner: Jim Hunt)
A massive blue ox or bull, folk-hero scale — think Paul Bunyan's Babe the
Blue Ox crossed with a gridiron mascot — charging forward through the
badge, shoulders lowered, steam or breath blasting from its nostrils, a
football gripped or driven under one foreshoot. Deep royal-to-navy blue
hide with darker shading in the muscle grooves, a glint of silver on the
horns or a nose ring. "BIG BLUE" worked in as bold arched lettering across
the top or bottom of the badge, weathered/foil-stamped look.
NEGATIVE: photorealism, real photo, any other team's colors or mascot,
readable text besides "BIG BLUE", gradient or busy background, cartoon
cuteness — this should look powerful, not friendly.

WOOKIE LEAKS (owner: Coach Nick)
A hulking, shaggy brown-furred wookiee-type creature (bandolier strap
across the chest, no readable insignia on it), roaring, one clawed hand
clutching a football, the other hand holding up a torn, dripping document
or scroll like it's mid-leak — a few loose pages and ink drips falling
away from the badge's lower edge as a graphic motif. Warm brown/tan fur
tones against the flat background, dramatic upward angle like the creature
is bursting through the badge. "WOOKIE LEAKS" as bold banner lettering,
lower half of the badge.
NEGATIVE: photorealism, real photo, any specific licensed character face
or recognizable film likeness, readable text on the leaked document beyond
illegible scribble marks, gradient or busy background.

THE BEAVER EATERS (owner: Sheq)
A snarling beaver head and shoulders filling the badge, huge buck teeth
bared and clamped around a football like a chew toy, wood chips flying
off to the sides, thick brown fur with lighter tan on the chest, small
fierce eyes, a couple of claw-marks or scratches across the badge for
grit. Aggressive mascot energy, not cute. "THE BEAVER EATERS" as bold
arched lettering framing the top of the badge.
NEGATIVE: photorealism, real photo, cute/Disney-style beaver, readable
text beyond the team name, gradient or busy background.

HAIRY GUMBAS (owner: Tommy Vertucci)
A thick-set, hairy-chested wiseguy caricature bust — open collar shirt,
gold chain, thick mustache, slicked hair, gruff expression — one arm
flexed and gripping a football against his chest like he's daring someone
to take it. Warm skin tones, heavy black linework, a little cigar smoke
curling up from the corner of his mouth as a small design accent. Same
tough-guy, Jersey-crew energy as this league's Guido Haters crest but a
distinct character, not a copy of it. "HAIRY GUMBAS" as bold banner
lettering across the bottom.
NEGATIVE: photorealism, real photo, any real person's likeness, readable
text beyond the team name, gradient or busy background.

HEAVY HITTERS (owner: Jay)
A single massive boxing glove, deep red or black leather with scuffed
highlights and visible lacing, caught mid-punch as it smashes directly
through a football — laces of the ball bursting, a sharp radial impact
burst and a few speed-lines behind it for motion. Dynamic diagonal
composition, high-impact and aggressive. "HEAVY HITTERS" as bold arched
lettering following the top curve of the badge.
NEGATIVE: photorealism, real photo, a full boxer or human figure (glove
only), readable text beyond the team name, gradient or busy background.
