PROJECTILE ART - 6 PICTURES
===========================

Six small pictures. Each one is the HEAD of a projectile: the bright
bit at the front of a shot in flight. Nothing else is needed - the
trail behind it is generated, not drawn by you.


FORMAT
------
PNG, 64 x 64 pixels each, background pure magenta #FF00FF.
Centre the object in the square. Leave the rest magenta.
No drop shadow, no ground, no motion blur streaks, no background.


THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE
-----------------------
These are drawn in the game WITHOUT BEING ROTATED. The same picture is
used whether the shot travels up, down, left or right.

So each one must look correct from any angle. Draw it as if seen
head-on: round, radial, symmetrical.

DO NOT draw a streak, a line, a tracer trail, a comet tail, or
anything with a long axis. A sideways streak would point the wrong way
in seven directions out of eight.

Think "glowing bead seen head-on", not "bullet whizzing past".


THE SIX
-------
1. RIFLE ROUND
   Small, tight, hot. A pale yellow-white core with a thin warm amber
   edge. The smallest and sharpest of the six.

2. SMG ROUND
   Like the rifle round but dimmer and slightly softer. Reads as a
   cheaper, weaker shot.

3. HEAVY MACHINE GUN ROUND
   Bigger and angrier. A white-hot core with an orange halo. Should
   feel heavy next to the rifle round.

4. SNIPER ROUND
   Small and very bright, almost white, with a cold blue-white edge
   rather than a warm one. Precise, surgical.

5. GRENADE / ROCKET
   A dull metallic bead with a hot orange glow around it, like the
   back of a rocket motor seen head-on. The least bright core, the
   widest glow.

6. COBRA PULSE BOLT
   Energy, not metal. An intense acid-green core with a softer green
   corona around it. No metal, no highlight, no reflection - it should
   look like light rather than an object.


SCALE RELATIVE TO EACH OTHER
----------------------------
Smallest to largest core:
   sniper, rifle, SMG, pulse bolt, heavy MG, rocket

The glow around each can be wider than the core. The rocket has the
widest glow and the dimmest centre; the sniper round the tightest and
brightest.


WHAT HAPPENS TO THESE AFTERWARDS
--------------------------------
Each head is used to generate a 35-frame trail: the head at the front
and a thinning tail of fainter, smaller copies behind it along the
flight path. How long and how dense that trail is gets tuned per
weapon on our side, so do not try to show speed or length. Just draw
the head.
