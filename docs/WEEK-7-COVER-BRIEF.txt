A portrait 2:3 magazine cover illustration, 1024x1536, painted in 1980s action
comic and toy-box-art style: bold black ink outlines, flat saturated colour,
hard cel shading with sharp shadow edges, strong rim light, airbrushed
highlights, light halftone grain and faint print misregistration, heroic
exaggerated proportions. Not photographic, not a 3D render, not anime. It should
look hand-painted in 1985 for the lid of a toy box.

The scene is a tug of war across the churned midfield of an American football
field at night under stadium floodlights. Sixteen mascots, eight on each side,
hauling on one thick muddy rope. Low olive and amber smoke drifts across the
turf. Floodlights burn through it behind as hard white flares and long god-rays.
Blurred empty stands high and far back, nearly lost in the haze. Palette of
near-black, deep red, gold, warm amber, olive-grey smoke and cream.

THE ROPE IS DEAD LEVEL. It runs straight across the frame from the left edge to
the right edge, perfectly horizontal, with a red marker rag tied at its middle
sitting exactly over the centre line of the field. Nobody is winning. Every
mascot is at maximum strain — feet dug in, bodies leaning back at a hard angle,
heels ploughing furrows, arms locked, teeth bared — and the rope has not moved.
Directly under the marker the turf is destroyed: a wide band of churned black
mud scored with heel-ruts running back and forth across the centre line many
times over, so it is obvious the rope has been dragged one way and then the
other all night and ended exactly where it started.

EVERY ONE OF THE SIXTEEN IS AN ANTHROPOMORPHIC TEAM MASCOT. Full body, upright
on two legs, with the exaggerated proportions of a sports mascot — oversized
head, big hands, big feet, broad readable silhouette, personality pushed to the
front. Each is a character in its own right, never a crest, never a logo, never
a floating head and never an object. All sixteen are clearly distinct from one
another at a glance. They are original characters invented for this picture.

LEFT SIDE, hauling to the left, nearest the camera first and getting smaller
toward the centre:
1. A battle-damaged humanoid robot mascot. Hulking, in a battered black leather
   jacket and dark glasses, half of its rubbery face torn away to show a
   polished chrome skull beneath with one glowing red lens. Hauling with no
   visible effort and no expression at all.
2. A giant beaver mascot. Oversized head, huge buck teeth bared, thick brown fur
   with a tan chest, flat paddle tail braced behind it like a third leg.
3. A big cartoon pig mascot. Round belly, sleeveless denim vest, heavy work
   boots, grinning around a toothpick.
4. A cartoon carthorse mascot. Long face, big square teeth, an oversized padded
   leather harness collar around its neck, a fat cigar clamped in its teeth and
   smoke curling up past one eye.
5. A small piglet mascot, a quarter the size of the big pig above and obviously
   the same family — same pink, a tiny version of the same sleeveless denim
   vest, a backwards cap — hauling right alongside it with its heels dug in.
   Place these two next to each other on the rope.
6. A lean gothic mascot in a tall black top hat and a long black coat worn open
   over a cream-white suit and cream spats, pale grinning face under the brim,
   spindly limbs.
7. A massive blue ox mascot. Royal-to-navy hide, steam blasting from its
   nostrils, a glint of silver on the horns, shoulders lowered.
8. A pair of face-painted strongman mascots hauling together, one just behind
   the other. Bare-chested, studded black leather, spiked shoulder pads, broad
   bands of war paint across the eyes. They count as one of the sixteen.

RIGHT SIDE, hauling to the right, nearest the camera first and getting smaller
toward the centre:
1. A heavyset moustached man mascot. Big round head, thick black moustache, in a
   plain teal and orange sleeveless football jersey stretched tight over his gut
   and his chest so his nipples show through the fabric, face red with effort.
2. A green dragon mascot. Big head, smoke curling from its nostrils, wings
   half-folded for leverage, tail braced against the ground.
3. A huge shaggy brown-furred ape-like beast mascot. A wide leather strap across
   its chest, head thrown back roaring, a torn page still crushed in one fist.
4. A bleached-blond showman mascot in black and white from head to foot — black
   bandana, black sleeveless shirt with white trim, black and white boots, dark
   sunglasses, bleached horseshoe moustache — smirking while he pulls.
5. A scowling mascot in a green, white and red striped football shirt.
   Clean-shaven, no chain, no cigar, no moustache, both hands wrapped in the
   rope and a thoroughly disgusted expression on his face.
6. A thick-set hairy-chested wiseguy mascot. Open-collar shirt, gold chain,
   thick black moustache, cigar clamped in the corner of his mouth.
7. A leering carnival clown mascot. Oversized teeth, white ruff, striped
   sleeves, enormous shoes dug into the mud.
8. A hooded robed warrior-monk mascot in rough brown cloth, a plain sword hilt
   at his belt throwing a soft glow, both hands on the rope, hood up.

The two nearest mascots, at the far left and far right, are the largest and may
be cropped by the left or right edge of the frame. Nothing is cropped by the
bottom edge.

No two of them may look like each other. The two heavyset moustached men in
particular must read as plainly different people, and the man in the green,
white and red stripes has no moustache, no chain and no cigar at all.

Composition is load-bearing. Keep the top 20 per cent of the frame simple and
uncluttered — smoke, floodlight glare and haze only, nothing important, no heads
and no rope. The rope runs across the frame at about 40 per cent of the height,
with the mascots filling the band from 22 to 58 per cent. It must read instantly
at phone-thumbnail size as two mobs on one rope that is not moving. Keep the
bottom 40 per cent visually quiet and low in contrast — churned mud, heel-ruts,
feet, smoke and deep shadow, no faces and no bright highlights — because type is
laid over it and it is darkened.

No lettering anywhere in the image: no words, no team names, no numbers, no
wordmarks, no watermarks, no scoreboard or banner text, no signage, no sponsor
boards. No real sports team crests and no league marks on any jersey.

Leave the top of the frame clear and draw no masthead, title, seal or logo of
your own.
