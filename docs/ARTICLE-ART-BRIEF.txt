ARTICLE OPENER IMAGES - KICKOFF ISSUE
5 images. Landscape, 1200x800 (3:2), JPG.

Cropped to 3:2 and centred, so keep the subject in the middle.
These sit at the top of each article's opening page, above the headline,
so they read at about half a phone screen wide. Keep them simple - one
clear subject, no small detail, no text.

Add to the end of every one:

Editorial photograph, dramatic single-source lighting, muted desaturated
colour, heavy film grain, deep shadows, shallow depth of field, 3:2
landscape. No text, no lettering, no logos, no watermarks.


================================================================================
1. scfl-art-champion.jpg
"The Champion Who Lost More Than He Won"
================================================================================

A cheap gold trophy standing alone on a folding card table in an empty
function room. Stacked chairs against the back wall, a single strand of
leftover streamers, one overhead bulb. The trophy is dented and slightly
crooked. Nobody in the frame.


================================================================================
2. scfl-art-namechange.jpg
"Two Wins, Eight Hundred and Twenty-One Points, One New Name"
================================================================================

A team jersey hanging alone on a wire hanger against a locker room wall,
the name plate on the back torn off, leaving a pale rectangle and a few
loose threads. Hard light from above, everything else in shadow.


================================================================================
3. scfl-art-blackandblue.jpg
"The Group of Death"
================================================================================

Four heavyweight boxers standing in a tight circle facing inward, shot
from directly overhead, lit by one hard overhead light on a dark canvas.
Shoulders and gloves only, no faces. They fill the centre of the frame.


================================================================================
4. scfl-art-preview.jpg
"The Season Preview"
================================================================================

Sixteen numbered wooden lockers in a row along a dark corridor, all shut,
shot straight down the line so they recede into shadow. One bare bulb.
Worn brass numbers, chipped paint.


================================================================================
5. scfl-art-hillstandoff.jpg
"The Hill Standoff"
================================================================================

A phone face-up on a kitchen table at night, screen lit, beside a cold
mug of coffee and a scatter of handwritten notes. The room around it is
dark. Nobody in the frame. The light comes from the screen.


================================================================================
INSTALLING
================================================================================

Drop all five in the repo root with those exact filenames, then:

  python3 scripts/build_issue.py

Any that are missing are skipped and that article just opens with type,
so you can add them one at a time. The Grudge Report already has its
opener image and needs nothing.
