1. THE FRAME - READ THIS FIRST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The app draws live type OVER this image:
  - Masthead across the TOP in cream, with "Chasers" in red
  - Lead headline near the BOTTOM in cream

So:
  - Top fifth of the image must be DARK AND EMPTY (black ceiling / rigging)
  - Bottom fifth must be DARK AND EMPTY (shadowed ringside floor)
  - The ring sits in the middle three fifths, lit hard from directly above
  - NO TEXT ANYWHERE in the image. No banners, signage, numbers or logos.
    Image models garble lettering and the real type is drawn on top anyway.

Specs:
  Aspect ....... 2:3 portrait
  Min size ..... 1400 x 2100
  Format ....... JPG
  Look ......... 1980s closed-circuit pay-per-view photography


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. MAIN COVER PROMPT  ->  scfl-kickoff-cover.jpg
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A packed wrestling ring in a dark arena, shot from ringside at ropes height
looking slightly up. Two enormous face-painted wrestlers in spiked leather
shoulder pads and fur-trimmed boots are stepping through the ropes into the
ring, backs half to camera, lit hard from above - they are the focus and they
are entering.

Waiting for them, ranged around the inside of the ring and crowding the ropes,
are fifteen other wrestlers, every one a different creature: an armored teal-
and-red dragon with spread wings; a hulking figure in a weathered white hockey
mask; two grinning circus clowns with wild hair and striped ruffs; a gothic
showman in a purple top hat with glowing eyes; a muscled bruiser with a black
pompadour and wraparound shades; a cartoon sheep in a football jersey; a pink
pig trailing speed lines; a spray-paint biker in black and white; a hooded
robed figure with a glowing blade; a draft horse in a heavy leather collar; a
shaggy brown-furred beast in a rumpled trench coat clutching folders; a
colossal blue-skinned giant in a stretched letterman sweater; a wild-eyed
mountain man with a napkin at his collar and a fork in his fist; a squat hairy
mushroom-headed creature with an underbite; and a barrel-chested heavyweight
with taped fists. All of them are turned toward the two entering wrestlers,
coiled and ready.

One hard overhead light cone on the center of the ring; everything at the edges
falls into deep shadow. Shot on expired film: heavy grain, slightly soft, muted
contrast. Deep black, dirty cream canvas, bleeding red turnbuckles. 1980s
closed-circuit pay-per-view photography. The top fifth of the frame is black
arena ceiling and lighting rigging in shadow. The bottom fifth is dark ringside
floor in shadow. Vertical composition, 2:3 portrait.


NEGATIVE PROMPT:

text, lettering, words, numbers, signage, banners, logos, watermarks, modern
LED lighting, HDR, oversaturated, clean digital sharpness, bright evenly-lit
arena, cute, chibi, flat vector illustration, empty ring


NOTE: No model renders 16 characters cleanly. Expect 4-5 to land in the
foreground and the rest to become a crowd of shapes. That is the right outcome
- only the two entering wrestlers should be fully lit. Generate several and
pick for menace and density, not for a headcount.

SHORTER VARIANT that lands more often: cut the creature list to the five most
distinctive (dragon, hockey mask, clowns, top hat, pompadour) and end with
"and ten more creatures crowding the ropes in shadow."

4. FIVE NEW TEAM LOGOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

These five teams have been running on the default ESPN avatar (Wookie Leaks
and Big Blue are literally the same file as each other). Each prompt below is a
standalone team crest that also doubles as that team's wrestler on the cover.

APPEND THIS STYLE BLOCK TO ALL FIVE so they match the existing crests:

  Team sport-logo crest, bold arched lettering, thick outline, dramatic rim
  lighting, dark background, slight grain and print texture, centered emblem
  composition, square. Fantasy football badge art.


-- WOOKIE LEAKS (slug: wookieleaks) --
The pun is the logo: Wookiee plus whistleblower.

A massive shaggy brown-furred beast standing in a rumpled trench coat and dark
sunglasses, one arm cradling a stack of manila classified folders, loose
documents spilling and blowing past him, every page stamped with a redaction
bar. He is mid-stride, glancing back over his shoulder like he is being
followed. Ammunition bandolier across his chest under the coat. Deep browns and
black, a single hazard-orange accent on the redaction bars.


-- BIG BLUE (slug: bigblue) --
A giant, taken literally.

A colossal blue-skinned giant seen from below, filling the frame, shoulders
like a mountain range, wearing a battered varsity letterman sweater stretched
over his chest and a football gripped in one enormous fist. Square jaw, heavy
brow, faint stubble, calm and immovable rather than angry. Deep navy and royal
blue with cold white rim light, a single red seam on the football.


-- BEAVER EATERS (slug: beavereaters) --
The name names the eater, not the beaver.

A wild-eyed backwoods mountain man with a matted beard and a fur cap, grinning
enormously, a checkered napkin tucked into his collar, a fork in one fist and a
knife in the other, leaning over a tin plate. Behind him, a gnawed log and a
scatter of wood chips. Rich browns and rust orange, firelight from below, one
cold blue highlight on the cutlery.


-- HAIRY GUMBAS (slug: gumbas) --
Squat, hairy, and much angrier than it should be.

A squat brown mushroom-headed creature with a broad flat cap, furious
downturned eyebrows and two blunt fangs in an underbite, its entire body
covered in thick shaggy hair, planted wide with tiny fists clenched. Comically
stubby and genuinely menacing at once. Warm browns, burnt orange cap, cream
fangs.


-- HEAVY HITTERS (slug: heavyhitters) --
Heavyweight, not baseball.

A barrel-chested heavyweight brawler shot from a low angle, taped and bloodied
fists raised, shoulders and forearms enormous, cauliflower ear, a flat scarred
nose and a thousand-yard stare. Sweat catching hard overhead light. Deep
charcoal and cyan with a hot white key light, a single red accent on the hand
tape.


COLOUR NOTE: Beaver Eaters, Gumbas and Heavy Hitters currently carry pink,
orange and cyan stock avatars. Orange and cyan are kept above so those two stay
recognisable in the app. The pink is dropped because it fights the Beaver
Eaters concept - easy to put back if you want it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. FALLBACKS IF THE PACKED RING DOESN'T LAND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

OVER THE TOP ROPE
Tight and low, shot from outside the ring looking up: one pair of boots going
backward over the top rope, mid-fall, out of the light. The winner is only a
silhouette behind.
  -> Plays elimination instead of victory. Meaner, and it suits a 7-7 champion.

THE ENTRANCE
A dark tunnel mouth from inside the arena, two hulking silhouettes standing in
it against a wall of white light, smoke pouring past.
  -> Easiest for the model to land, and the darkest top and bottom of the three.

7. WHAT TO SEND BACK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  scfl-kickoff-cover.jpg .... 2:3 portrait, the cover
  scfl-kickoff-og.jpg ....... 1200 x 630, the share thumbnail
  five square crests ........ wookieleaks, bigblue, beavereaters, gumbas,
                              heavyhitters (any size, any format)

The two cover files just get dropped in the repo root and the next build picks
them up.

The five logos are NOT a file drop - the app reads them from an embedded map,
so they get installed one command each:

  python3 scripts/add_logo.py wookieleaks wookie.png

Run it with no arguments to list the slugs. It resizes to 160x160, embeds into
the map the app reads, and keeps the full-size original alongside it.
