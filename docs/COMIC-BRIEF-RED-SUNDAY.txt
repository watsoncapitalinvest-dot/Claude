SCFL COMIC — "RED SUNDAY"


STYLE — APPLIES TO EVERY IMAGE BELOW

1987 American newsstand comic book interior art. Bronze Age rendering: heavy
black ink spotting, confident brush linework with varying weight, crosshatched
shadow, visible Ben-Day dot screens in the flat areas, four-colour separation
with slight registration drift so the magenta and cyan sit a hair off the black
plate. Printed on cheap newsprint — the whites are warm grey, the colours sit
matte and a little muddy, there is paper tooth in the flats. Dramatic low
camera angles, exaggerated foreshortening, figures breaking the panel border.
Hard directional light, deep cast shadows, rim light on every silhouette.
Saturated primaries: fire-engine red, cadmium orange, chrome yellow, process
cyan, flat black.

Leave every balloon, caption box and sound effect EMPTY — draw the shapes, not
the words. Blank white balloons with black outlines and tails. Blank yellow
caption boxes with black borders. Sound effects as empty bold outlined display
shapes with speed lines and impact bursts behind them, no letters inside.


THE ADVERSARY — INVENTED, NOT ANY REAL COUNTRY

A fictional Cold War army called the IRON VANGUARD. Steel-grey greatcoats and
ushanka fur caps, blocky riot visors, high black boots, red armbands bearing a
plain red five-pointed star inside a white circle — that star is their ONLY
insignia. No real flags, no real national emblems, no Cyrillic or any other
real-world lettering, no identifiable real nation, no real political figures.
Faceless, numerous, rendered as a grey mass so the colour in every panel comes
from the heroes. Their vehicles are boxy grey half-tracks and a squat tank with
a red star on the turret.

THE SIXTEEN

Use these for any figure that needs to be identifiable. Each one keeps its own
colours. Every one of them wears battered football shoulder pads over 1980s
military webbing, with its own colours on the pads and the torn jersey beneath.

N.W.O — a lean biker in black and white, wraparound shades, spray-paint can
holstered at the hip, black and white only, no other colour on him.

BEAVER EATERS — a wild-eyed bearded mountain man in a fur cap, checkered napkin
still tucked in his collar, a fork gripped like a knife. Rich brown and tan,
firelight from below.

THE MACHINES — a huge silent figure in a weathered white hockey mask, silver and
flat black, moving without hurry while everything around him burns.

SMOKE DRAGONS — an armoured dragon with spread wings, deep purple scale with
black plating, smoke curling from the nostrils, lit from inside the throat.

GUMBAS — a squat shaggy mushroom-headed creature with a broad flat cap, blunt
fangs in an underbite, tiny fists clenched. Forest green cap, cream fangs.

WOOKIE LEAKS — a massive shaggy brown-furred beast in a rumpled trench coat and
dark glasses, manila folders under one arm, loose redacted pages blowing past.
Deep brown and gunmetal silver.

LIL' CHOPS — a cartoon sheep standing upright in a football jersey, cream wool,
royal blue trim, absurdly calm in the middle of a firefight.

THE POWERS OF PAIN — two enormous face-painted wrestlers in spiked leather
shoulder pads and fur-trimmed boots, always drawn as a pair, always in front.
Fire-engine red and black.

HORSECOLLARS — a heavy draft horse standing on two legs in a thick leather
collar, green and white, hauling something no one else can move.

PORK CHOP EXPRESS — a pink pig in pads, trailing heavy speed lines, never once
drawn standing still. Red and black.

KILLER KLOWNS — two grinning circus clowns with wild hair and striped ruffs,
red and electric blue, enjoying this far more than anyone else is.

STILLTHECREAM — a gothic showman in a tall purple top hat with glowing eyes,
black frock coat, deep wine-red lining.

PHINS PHAG — a barrel-chested heavyweight brawler, taped and bloodied fists
raised, cauliflower ear, flat scarred nose. Teal and white.

BIG BLUE — a colossal blue-skinned giant in a stretched varsity letterman
sweater, shoulders like a mountain range, calm rather than angry. Royal blue and
cold white.

MASTER-JETI — a hooded robed figure with a glowing blade, face in shadow under
the hood. Brown robes, cream under-tunic, the blade the only light on him.

GUIDO HATERS — a muscled bruiser with a black pompadour and wraparound shades,
gold chain over the pads. Flat black and antique gold.
