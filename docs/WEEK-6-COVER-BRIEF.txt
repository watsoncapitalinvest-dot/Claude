WEEK 6 COVER — SCFL / SKIRT CHASERS

Portrait, 1024x1536 (2:3), JPG.

This one does NOT use the house photographic style. Ignore the photographic
instructions in WEEKLY-COVER-BRIEF.txt for this cover. Everything below
replaces them, except the composition rules at the end, which still apply.


STYLE

1980s G.I. Joe comic and box art. Hand-painted action poster. Bold black ink
outlines, flat saturated colour blocked in, hard cel shading with sharp
terminator edges, strong rim light, light halftone grain and faint print
misregistration. Heroic exaggerated proportions, square jaws, muscular builds,
dynamic foreshortening. Airbrushed highlights on helmets and pads.

Not photographic. Not a 3D render. Not modern digital painting. Not anime. It
should look like it was painted in 1985 for a toy box lid.


UNIFORMS

Real NFL uniforms, drawn accurately — correct team colours, correct helmet
shell colour, correct helmet logo, correct jersey and trouser combination.
Helmets, facemasks, shoulder pads, jerseys, trousers, socks and cleats on
every figure. Everyone is in full game kit.

Do not attempt portrait likenesses of the real players. The uniform identifies
each man, not his face. Most of them have helmets on; the two whose faces show
are generic comic-art faces.


THE STANDING FIGURE — centred, the only man on his feet

TYLER SHOUGH, quarterback, NEW ORLEANS SAINTS. Black jersey, old gold
numerals, white trousers with black and gold stripe, black helmet with the
gold fleur-de-lis.

He stands alone and upright at the dead centre of the frame, facing the
viewer, feet planted wide. Helmet on, facemask up, eyes visible beneath it.
Uniform scuffed and mud-flecked but intact — he is the only one who came
through it. A football in his right hand, held down at his side the way a
soldier holds a rifle at rest. Other hand open. Chin level, jaw set, staring
straight out of the frame. Exhausted and unbroken.

Light him hard from behind so a cold white rim separates him from the smoke,
with warm amber bouncing up onto his chest and face from the turf.


THE EIGHT DOWN — arranged in a ring spreading out from his feet

All in full uniform, all injured and not dead: mud, torn jerseys, field
bandages, taped joints, arms in improvised slings, a knee splinted with a
piece of sideline marker. Grimacing, eyes shut, exhausted. No blood, no
wounds, no bodies. A field hospital, not a massacre.

Closest to him, catching the light, at his knees and hips:

  JALEN HURTS, quarterback, PHILADELPHIA EAGLES — midnight green jersey,
  white numerals, midnight green helmet with the silver-white eagle wings. On
  his back, one arm raised toward the standing man. The most prominent of the
  fallen.

  MIKE EVANS, wide receiver, SAN FRANCISCO 49ERS — scarlet red jersey, gold
  numerals and pants, gold helmet with the red SF oval. Sitting propped on one
  arm, head bowed, other arm across his chest.

  LAMAR JACKSON, quarterback, BALTIMORE RAVENS — purple jersey, black and gold
  trim, black helmet with the gold-and-purple raven shield. Face down, helmet
  beside him on the turf, one hand reaching forward.

Mid-distance, half-lit, falling back into the smoke:

  DAVANTE ADAMS, wide receiver, LOS ANGELES RAMS — royal blue jersey with sol
  yellow numerals and horn, royal blue helmet with the yellow ram horn. Being
  half-carried by a trainer who has himself gone to one knee.

  ROMEO DOUBS, wide receiver, NEW ENGLAND PATRIOTS — nautical navy jersey, red
  and silver trim, silver helmet with the Flying Elvis. On one knee, head
  down, one hand flat on the turf.

  KYLE MONANGAI, running back, CHICAGO BEARS — navy jersey with burnt orange
  numerals, navy helmet with the orange wishbone C. On his side, knees drawn
  up, both hands on one leg.

Furthest back, nearly silhouettes in the haze:

  JEREMIYAH LOVE, running back, ARIZONA CARDINALS — cardinal red jersey, white
  helmet with the cardinal head. Being lifted on a stretcher at the edge of
  frame.

  TYLER WARREN, tight end, INDIANAPOLIS COLTS — white jersey with royal blue
  numerals, white helmet with the blue horseshoe. Sitting against a fallen
  goalpost, helmet off, looking upward.


SETTING

A churned and torn football field at dusk, standing in for a war zone. Chewed
turf, mud, standing water, scattered helmets and a lost cleat. Low drifting
smoke and haze across the whole field, lit olive and amber. Stadium
floodlights behind, burning through the smoke as hard white flares and long
god-rays. Blurred empty stands high and far back, almost lost in the haze. A
bent goalpost leaning in the background.

Palette: near-black, deep red, gold, warm amber, olive-grey smoke, cream. Let
the team colours carry the saturation against that.


COMPOSITION — load-bearing, the layout depends on it

Top 20 per cent of the frame: keep simple and uncluttered. Smoke, haze,
floodlight glare only. A cream masthead panel is composited across it and a
red circular seal overhangs its lower left corner. Nothing important here.

Middle band, 22 to 58 per cent of the height: Shough, head near the top of
this band, centred left-to-right. He must read instantly at phone-thumbnail
size as one man upright among many down.

Bottom 40 per cent: the nearest fallen men, mud and smoke. A dark gradient is
laid over this and headline type sits on top, so keep it visually quiet and
low-contrast — shapes and silhouettes, no faces that would be missed, no
bright highlights. It must still look deliberate when darkened.

Compose for a tall narrow crop. Keep Shough well away from the left and right
edges.


ABSOLUTE

No lettering anywhere in the frame. No words, no team names spelled out, no
wordmarks, no watermarks, no scoreboard text, no banner text, no signage, no
sponsor boards. Team logos on helmets and jersey crests are wanted; written
words are not. Jersey numerals are fine.

The masthead, issue line, week seal, cover lines and league shield are all
composited on top afterwards and must not be drawn.


IF REAL TEAM MARKS ARE REFUSED

If the generator will not draw actual NFL logos, keep every team's colours and
uniform layout exactly as described and leave the helmet shells plain. Correct
colours with blank helmets is the fallback. Do not substitute invented logos.
