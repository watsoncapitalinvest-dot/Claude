WEEK 6 COVER — SCFL / SKIRT CHASERS

Portrait, 1024x1536 (2:3), JPG.

This one does NOT use the house photographic style. Ignore the photographic
instructions in WEEKLY-COVER-BRIEF.txt for this cover. Everything below
replaces them, except the composition rules at the end, which still apply.


STYLE

1980s G.I. Joe comic and box art. Hand-painted action poster. Bold black ink
outlines, flat saturated colour blocked in, hard cel shading with sharp
terminator edges, strong rim light, light halftone grain and faint print
misregistration. Heroic exaggerated proportions, square jaws, muscular builds,
dynamic foreshortening. Airbrushed highlights on helmets and pads.

Not photographic. Not a 3D render. Not modern digital painting. Not anime. It
should look like it was painted in 1985 for a toy box lid.


SUBJECT — one figure standing, centred

A quarterback stands alone and upright at the dead centre of the frame,
facing the viewer, feet planted wide. He is the only man still on his feet.

He is unhurt and immaculate where everyone else is wrecked. Full football
uniform, clean: helmet on with the facemask up and his eyes visible beneath
it, shoulder pads, long-sleeve jersey, numbers on chest and sleeve, trousers,
socks, cleats. His jersey is cream and deep red. He holds a football in his
right hand, down at his side, gripped the way a soldier holds a rifle at rest.
His other hand hangs open. Chin level, jaw set, staring straight out of the
frame. Exhausted and unbroken.

Light him hard from behind so a cold white rim separates him from the smoke,
with warm amber bouncing up onto his chest and face from the turf.


AROUND HIM — ten to twelve players down

The ground around him is covered with other football players, all of them
down, all of them in full uniform, helmets and pads on. Arrange them in a ring
that spreads outward from his feet and recedes into the smoke behind him.

Vary the uniforms — different colour combinations, different numbers — so it
reads as players from many different teams, not one team. Dark navy, forest
green, burnt orange, steel grey, maroon, white. All of them scuffed, torn,
caked in mud and turf.

Vary how they are down. One on his back with one arm raised toward the
standing man. One face down, helmet beside him, hand reaching. Two propped
against each other sitting, heads bowed. One being half-carried by another who
has himself gone to one knee. One on a stretcher being lifted at the edge of
frame. Some with field bandages, taped knees, arms in improvised slings, a leg
splinted with a piece of sideline marker. Grimacing, eyes shut, one looking
upward.

Injured, not dead, and not gory. Mud, bandages, dirt, sweat and exhaustion —
no blood, no wounds, no bodies. The tone is a field hospital on a battlefield,
not a massacre.

Place three or four of the fallen high enough to catch the light and flank the
standing figure at his knees and hips. Let the rest fall away into the darker
lower third and the smoke behind.


SETTING

A churned and torn football field at dusk, standing in for a war zone. Chewed
turf, mud, standing water, scattered helmets and a lost cleat. Low drifting
smoke and haze across the whole field, lit olive and amber. Stadium
floodlights behind, burning through the smoke as hard white flares and long
god-rays. Blurred empty stands high and far back, almost lost in the haze.
A bent goalpost leaning in the far background.

Palette: near-black, deep red, gold, warm amber, olive-grey smoke, cream.


COMPOSITION — load-bearing, the layout depends on it

Top 20 per cent of the frame: keep simple and uncluttered. Smoke, haze,
floodlight glare only. A cream masthead panel is composited across it and a
red circular seal overhangs its lower left corner. Nothing important here.

Middle band, 22 to 58 per cent of the height: the standing quarterback, his
head near the top of this band, centred left-to-right. He must read instantly
at phone-thumbnail size as one man upright among many down.

Bottom 40 per cent: the nearest fallen players, mud and smoke. A dark gradient
is laid over this and headline type sits on top, so keep it visually quiet and
low-contrast — shapes and silhouettes, no faces that would be missed, no
bright highlights. It must still look deliberate when darkened.

Compose for a tall narrow crop. Keep the standing figure well away from the
left and right edges.


ABSOLUTE

No lettering anywhere in the frame. No words, no numbers that spell anything,
no logos, no team names, no wordmarks, no watermarks, no scoreboard text, no
banner text, no signage. Jersey numbers may appear as plain digits only.

No real NFL team names, logos, wordmarks or exact team uniform designs. Invent
the colour combinations.

The masthead, issue line, week seal, cover lines and league shield are all
composited on top afterwards and must not be drawn.
