Wookie Leaks Paid a First and a Second for a Receiver Already in Freefall

“A 2027 first and a 2028 second bought Chris Godwin at $11 — down from a $47 peak four years earlier. The market hasn't stopped marking him down since.”
By The Trade Court Desk · Graded at-the-time and in hindsight · Sources: trades.json, dp-values.json, history.json, Mos Eisley group chat

In 2025, Wookie Leaks sent two future picks — a 2027 first and a 2028 second — to Powers of Pain. In return: Chris Godwin, and their own 2026 second coming back home. On paper it read as a win-now move. The numbers say they paid a first-round tax for a receiver who'd already lost more than half his value, and who kept losing it after the ink dried.
