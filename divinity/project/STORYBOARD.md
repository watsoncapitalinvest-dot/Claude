# DIVINITY — STORYBOARD
### 77 key frames · prompts written for image generation

---

## HOW TO USE THIS

Paste the **STYLE BLOCK** and the relevant **CHARACTER** line in front of each panel prompt. Panels marked **[GOLD]** use the second style block instead.

Nothing in these prompts describes anything a camera cannot see. No emotions, no backstory, no dialogue — image models cannot draw those and they poison the result.

### STYLE BLOCK — prepend to every panel

> Cinematic film still, anamorphic widescreen 2.39:1, shot on 35mm, desaturated cold palette of pine green, slate grey, wet brown and bone white, flat overcast northern daylight, naturalistic, visible film grain, shallow depth of field, muted contrast, no text, no captions, no lettering.

### GOLD STYLE BLOCK — panels marked [GOLD]

> Cinematic film still, anamorphic widescreen 2.39:1, shot on 35mm, cold slate and pine palette pierced by sourceless warm gold light, chiaroscuro in the manner of a Renaissance ceiling fresco, visible film grain, heavy atmosphere, no text, no captions, no lettering.

### CHARACTERS — keep these identical in every prompt

*(Ethnicity deliberately unspecified — pick one for each and then never vary it, or the faces will drift between panels.)*

- **JOHN** — man of 37, tall, heavy through the shoulders, dark hair cut short and grown out, three days of stubble, deep-set eyes, filthy canvas work coat, wool watch cap, work gloves. His hands are always dirty.
- **EMMA (young)** — woman of 21, small and wiry, long dark hair, university hoodie under a borrowed coat.
- **EMMA (older)** — woman of 25, same face, hair cut short and badly by herself, layered wool and canvas, forearms of someone who works.
- **RUTHIE** — woman of 22, round-faced, soft, pale, fair hair to the shoulders, college sweatshirt under a coat two sizes too big, always visibly cold.
- **HAZEL** — woman of 71, very small, white hair, heavy square glasses, quilted coat worn over everything.
- **DELIA** — woman of 53, broad-shouldered, grey-streaked hair tied back hard, reading glasses on a cord, a bound ledger always under one arm.
- **RAY** — man of 66, tall and stooped, white beard, plaid wool shirt, carpenter's pencil behind his ear.
- **ADAM (boy)** — boy of 14, skinny, dark hair too long, hard face, always carrying something heavy.
- **ADAM (young man)** — man of 19, same face, taller, rifle slung.
- **EVIE (girl)** — girl of 13, small, freckled, swamped in a dead man's brown overcoat three sizes too big, hands black with dirt.
- **EVIE (young woman)** — woman of 17, same face and same overcoat, sleeves rolled.
- **WARD (early)** — man of about 32, slight, soft-featured, entirely unremarkable, volunteer's armband, clipboard, cheap parka.
- **WARD (later)** — same man, grey administrative uniform, immaculate, no insignia of rank.
- **THE PRESIDENT** — man of 60s, silver hair, excellent dark suit, open pleasant face.
- **GREER** — man of 40s, thin, mild, wire-rimmed glasses, layered rags.

# PART ONE — THE FIRST DAYS

**1.1** — Interior of a moving sedan from the back seat, four young women, windows down, hair whipping, all four mid-laugh, morning sun flaring across the windscreen, summer green blurring past.

**1.2** — Tight on **RUTHIE** in the back seat, head tipped back against the headrest, mouth open singing, sunlight strobing across her face through roadside trees.

**1.3** — Tight profile of the young woman driving, hand loose at the top of the wheel, mouth open mid-word — eyes half closed, face gone slack, hand sliding off the wheel.

**1.4** — Wide from a low roadside angle: a grey sedan leaving a highway at speed, front wheels off the pavement, dust and gravel exploding out from under it.

**1.5** — **EMMA (young)** hanging upside down in a seatbelt inside a crushed car interior, glass in her hair, blood at her hairline, eyes open and moving.

**1.6** — Extreme wide, high angle: an eight-lane interstate stretching to the horizon, every single vehicle stopped at odd angles across all lanes, no movement anywhere, low overcast sky.

**1.7** — Wide landscape: two small figures standing on a highway verge, and beyond the treeline a mile off, a vast column of black smoke going up from something burning on the ground.

**1.8** — Interior of a plain Catholic church, wide from the rear, half the congregation collapsed forward into the pews and half still standing, a hymnal open on the floor in the aisle.

**1.9** — **JOHN** standing at the very back of the church against the doors, hands at his sides, looking down the aisle over the heads of standing people.

**1.10** — A middle-aged priest in vestments standing alone in the central aisle surrounded by seated and collapsed parishioners, everyone in the frame turned toward him.

**1.11** — Low angle from floor level between pews: a forest of legs and shoes shifting, movement blurred, one dropped hymnal in focus in the foreground.

**1.12** — **JOHN** alone at dusk in a rough churchyard, waist-deep in a freshly dug hole, shovel in both hands, spoil heaped beside him, the church windows dark behind.

**1.13** — Wide: an unfinished three-storey timber-frame apartment building on a cleared lot, plastic sheeting snapping in the wind, an excavator standing idle with its door open.

**1.14** — Interior of a bare stud-framed apartment unit, sawdust on the subfloor, **JOHN** kneeling to shim a single hung door in an otherwise empty wall, a stack of unfitted doors under plastic behind him.

**1.15** — **JOHN** sitting on the subfloor with his back against a stud wall, knees up, staring at the hung door across the empty room, late light through unglazed window openings.

**1.16** — Wide from behind a chain-link fence: a long open trench along a churchyard fence line, forty mounded graves beside it, a row of fence pickets driven in as markers, two men working at the far end.

**1.17** — **JOHN** and **RAY** shoulder to shoulder in the trench, both filthy, both mid-shovel, neither looking at the other, overcast light.

**1.18** — Interior of a church parish hall converted to a dormitory, nine people on mattresses and cots among stacked hymnals and folded tables, one oil lamp lit.

**1.19** — **EMMA (young)** alone in a suburban backyard at dusk, standing in a shallow grave with a garden spade, two finished mounds beside her, the kitchen light of the house on behind her.

**1.20** — Wide: two young women in loaded backpacks walking a two-lane road north between dead cars, treeline closing in on both sides, low grey sky.

**1.21** — **EMMA** and **RUTHIE** stopped at a wire fence looking into a field containing eleven squared and mounded graves with hand-lettered fence pickets, nobody in sight to the horizon.

**1.22** — Wide from a ridge: a woman cresting a rise in the foreground, and forty yards below her a man standing chest-deep in a hole in the ground, both stopped, looking at each other.

**1.23** — Two shovels, one in a man's hands and one being lifted from the dirt by a woman's hands, tight, no faces.

# PART TWO — THE FIRST WINTER

**2.1** — Wide, first snow: a treeline camp of tarpaulins and salvaged plywood shelters among enormous pines, a cook fire, nineteen people visible, no wall yet.

**2.2** — Tight: sealed metal seed tins and four burlap sacks stacked in a cut earth cellar, lantern light.

**2.3** — Interior of a rough log meeting shelter at night, **HAZEL** kneeling at a plank laid across two barrels, burning letters into the wood with a poker pulled from a fire, the whole camp watching from the dark behind her.

**2.4** — Tight, low angle: a rough plank board mounted on a log wall, twelve lines of crude burned lettering, lantern light raking across the char.

**2.5** — Wide in falling snow: a timber palisade wall being raised, men and women hauling a log upright with ropes, **JOHN** directing with one arm out.

**2.6** — **RUTHIE** alone walking away from the camp along a snow-covered logging road at dawn, backpack, coat too big, seen from behind at distance.

**2.7** — Night, wide: a military checkpoint at a road's edge — floodlights, razor wire, a generator, steam rising from a field kitchen, a line of ragged people.

**2.8** — **WARD (early)** crouched on his heels in front of **RUTHIE**, who is seated on a crate wrapped in a grey blanket holding a tin bowl in both hands, floodlight from behind him, his face open and kind.

**2.9** — Tight over Ward's shoulder: a clipboard, a printed form, small neat handwriting filling a line.

**2.10** — **RUTHIE** alone in the cellar hole under the store hut, lantern on the floor, lifting sealed seed tins into a pack, the burlap sacks left behind her.

**2.11** — **RUTHIE** walking back through the camp gate in daylight, holding a stamped paper receipt out in front of her at arm's length, people beginning to turn.

**2.12** — **RUTHIE** hauling a water yoke through the camp, and in the mid-ground three people who have all turned to look at something else.

**2.13** — Interior meeting shelter, packed, everyone standing, **DELIA** with her ledger open, **JOHN** halfway to his feet at the back, **HAZEL** near the board.

**2.14** — **EMMA (young)** and **RUTHIE** at the treeline at dusk, a packed bag on Ruthie's back, neither touching the other.

**2.15** — **RUTHIE** in the lit interior of a canvas military tent, blanket round her shoulders, bowl untouched in her lap, **WARD (early)** seated across from her leaning forward with his hands clasped.

**2.16** — Extreme wide: a very small lone figure walking east across an enormous frozen bog under an immense grey sky, low sun, wrong direction, no path.

**2.17** — Wide from behind: two men in fatigues at fifty yards, rifles raised, the small figure ahead of them on open ground.

**2.18** — Four people carrying a wrapped body on a makeshift litter through deep snow between pines, exhausted, **EMMA** at the front left.

**2.19** — **JOHN** digging in frozen ground at the edge of the camp clearing, breath steaming, the whole settlement standing back in a loose ring behind him.

**2.20** — **EVIE (girl)** on her knees in fresh turned earth over a grave, dragging a burlap sack, pushing seed into the dirt with both hands, other people beginning to kneel around her.

**2.21** — Tight: a plank grave marker driven into raw earth, four crude carved letters, snow beginning to settle on the top edge.

# PART THREE — EDEN

**3.2** — Inside the wall: cultivated beds, bee skeps, a goat pen, timber cabins, laundry, three children running.

**3.3** — **EMMA (older)** kneeling to change a dressing on a man's leg by lamplight in a cabin.

**3.4** — **EVIE (young woman)** on her knees in a seed bed in the same enormous brown overcoat, sleeves rolled, dirt to the elbow, talking to someone out of frame.

**3.5** — **JOHN** and **EMMA (older)** standing in a cabin doorway a foot apart, not touching, both looking out at the settlement.

**3.6** — **GREER** at the settlement gate in daylight, thin and ragged, hands visible and open, four people facing him inside the gate, **ADAM (young man)** at the back with a rifle.

**3.7** — Thirty-one ragged people standing in a loose crowd outside the wall with packs and children, seen from the walkway above the gate.

**3.8** — Interior meeting hall at night, packed and standing, **DELIA** with the open ledger, the burned board on the wall behind her, **EVIE** near the door with her arms folded.

**3.10** — **DELIA** alone in the store cellar with a lantern, a pencil and the ledger, one hand flat on a heaped mound of potatoes.

**3.11** — A single oil lamp burning on a plank table in an otherwise dark empty meeting hall.

# PART FOUR — THE SIEGE

**4.1** — Dawn, wide from the settlement wall: hundreds of soldiers and vehicles emerging from the treeline across an open slope and stopping.

**4.2** — Mid-morning wide: a military camp fully established on the slope — tents in rows, trucks, a field kitchen with steam rising, four hundred men, all of it facing an unimpressive timber wall.

**4.3** — **WARD (later)** standing alone and unarmed in wet grass forty feet from the wall, hands loose at his sides, looking up.

**4.4** — Reverse from behind Ward: nineteen adults spaced along a timber walkway on top of a wall, looking down.

**4.5** — An eleven-foot section of timber wall blown out, splintered ends, daylight through the gap, nobody in it.

**4.6** — Two people sitting on crates on the inside of the wall at night facing an open gap in it, blankets round them, a lantern turned low, nothing coming through.

**4.7** — **RAY** carrying a timber toward the gap in daylight, stopped mid-stride, a fresh divot of earth kicked up eight feet in front of him.

**4.8** — Long lens through the gap in the wall: the enemy field kitchen two hundred yards away, steam, men queueing with mess tins.

**4.9** — Wide inside the settlement at midday: everybody sitting down — on steps, on the ground, against walls — tools laid on the ground beside them, nobody working.

**4.10** — **WARD (later)** in the wet grass with his hands behind his back, looking up at a wall where nothing at all is happening, his face changed.

**4.11** — Interior meeting hall, an argument, people on their feet and pointing, **RAY** seated alone in the middle of it with his hands on his knees.

**4.12** — **JOHN** standing in the middle of the meeting hall floor, everyone else seated or standing back from him, the burned board on the wall behind his shoulder.

**4.13** — **WARD** at the wall in daylight speaking upward, and above him on the walkway **EMMA (older)** alone, gripping the rail.

**4.14** — Night, torchlight: the settlement gate unbarred and swung fully open, six children being walked out through it into darkness by two adults.

**4.15** — **ADAM (young man)** holding one leaf of the open gate with both hands, braced, watching people walk out past him.

**4.16** — Dawn on the walkway: nineteen people standing spaced along the wall, the open gate below them, mist on the slope beyond.

**4.17** — Wide from behind the defenders: the entire slope in motion, four hundred men advancing on a timber wall in dawn light.

**4.18** — Close, chaotic: the wall walkway under attack, splinters, smoke, **ADAM** firing, **DELIA** dragging someone by the collar.

**4.19** — **HAZEL** face down in mud between cabins, her glasses a yard away.

**4.20** — **JOHN** and **EMMA (older)** back to back on the ground against an overturned cart, both out of ammunition, both filthy.

**4.21** — **[GOLD]** Extreme wide low angle: the entire overcast sky torn open above a burning settlement, sourceless gold light pouring down through it onto the slope.

**4.22** — **[GOLD]** Descending figures in gold light — impossible anatomy, six wings, faces that will not resolve, edges dissolving into air, painted in the manner of a Renaissance ceiling.

**4.23** — **[GOLD]** A ruined wall walkway: filthy exhausted human defenders standing shoulder to shoulder in a line with towering luminous winged figures, all facing the same direction, the figures level with them and not in front.

**4.24** — **[GOLD]** Wide on the slope: hundreds of soldiers running away downhill in every direction, and scattered among them figures standing perfectly still.

**4.25** — **[GOLD]** **HAZEL** standing up out of the mud, glasses gone, white hair loose, her edges faintly dissolving into gold light, laughing.

**4.26** — **[GOLD]** **RAY** and **DELIA** standing among wreckage, filthy, faintly translucent at the edges, picking rifles up off the ground.

**4.27** — **[GOLD]** **EMMA (older)** turned toward the host, and thirty feet away among luminous figures, **RUTHIE** — unhurt, twenty-two, looking straight back at her.

**4.28** — **[GOLD]** Enormous scale: a single vast winged figure descending on a small human figure in a dark suit standing alone on a road, everything else in the frame flattened by light.

**4.29** — **[GOLD]** **WARD (later)** on his knees in mud with one arm stretched upward, being pulled backward and downward out of frame, uniform in ruin.

**4.30** — **[GOLD]** **JOHN** on his knees in the mud with one arm fully extended, hand open, reaching toward empty ground.

**4.31** — **[GOLD]** **EMMA (older)** standing, edges dissolving into gold, looking across the ruined settlement at **EVIE (young woman)**, who is standing filthy and upright and looking back.

**4.32** — **[GOLD]** Extreme wide from above: the entire host rising off a ruined burning clearing into an open sky, the field emptying, two small figures left standing in it.

# EPILOGUE — THE GARDEN

**5.1** — High wide in high summer: a clearing entirely overgrown into orchard and garden, the palisade wall collapsed and green in three places, two small figures working in it.

**5.2** — **EVIE (young woman)** and **ADAM (young man)**, older, in simple woven and leaf-tied coverings, planting a knee-high sapling in a row of eight others along a garden's south edge.

**5.3** — Extreme wide, camera pulling back and up: two tiny figures in a green clearing under a mature apple tree, forest to every horizon, no wall, no road, no smoke.

**5.4** — Very tight, low to the ground: long summer grass, and in it the movement of a snake, out of focus behind, a bare human heel in the foreground.
