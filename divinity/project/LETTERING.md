# DIVINITY — LETTERING SCRIPT
### Captions and dialogue for the 77-panel board

---

## NOTES FOR THE LETTERER

**CAP** = caption box. Square, hard-edged, bone white on the cold panels — **gold-bordered from 4.21 onward**, and never again after.

**Silence is the point.** Panels marked *— silent —* carry no text at all. That is deliberate and roughly a third of the book. Do not fill them.

**Balloon discipline:** nothing over about twenty words. Two balloons per panel maximum. If a line won't fit, it's the wrong line.

**No lyrics.** The song in 1.1–1.3 is rendered as ♪ only — printing the words is a licence nobody has.
