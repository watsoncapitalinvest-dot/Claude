DECISIONS WAITING ON JOHN
=========================
10 Nov. Nothing here is urgent. Each one is a choice I could have made

built rather than how.

Ordered by what it blocks. The first three are holding up work.


BLOCKING
--------------------------------------------------------------------

1. WHAT DOES A COBRA PROJECT DO WHEN IT LANDS?
   Blocks: research labs doing anything at all.

   The research lab produces "project progress" and raiding one delays
   and reveals a project. All of that is built and stored. What is
   missing is the other end: when a project completes, what happens?

   Candidates, cheapest first:
   - Cobra troops get a new weapon or armour in that region
   - A new site type unlocks for Cobra to build
   - A one-off event: a Joe base attacked, a country signs a pact
   - A countdown to something that ends the act

   Until this is answered a lab is an expensive building that does
   nothing, and projectProgressPerMonth stays unread.

2. GARRISON REFILL HAS NOTHING TO REFILL.
   Blocks: training camps doing anything at all.

   I found this while building and it is a real wrinkle rather than a
   preference. X-COM does not store a garrison on a site. The defenders
   are generated fresh from the deployment every time you raid it, so
   there is no number to deplete and no number to top up.

   So a training camp needs a mechanism invented, not just a value:
   - It raises the garrison size of every site in its region
     (cheapest, and reads well: starve the camp, thin the defenders)
   - It gates which Cobra ranks can appear
   - Sites gain persistent garrison state (biggest change by far)

   My recommendation is the first.

3. DOES COBRA'S CAPTURED MONEY BECOME YOURS?
   Blocks: nothing, but it changes the feel a lot.

   Today a raid DENIES Cobra the funds and gives you score. The
   alternative is that you also bank it. Denial alone makes raiding a
   strategic act; banking it makes raiding an income stream and
   competes with your council funding.


NAMING AND COPY
--------------------------------------------------------------------

4. TWO THINGS CALLED TERROR DROME.
   The mod already renames vanilla alien bases to "Terror Drome" in the
   language file, and one of the eight Cobra site types is a Terror
   Drome of its own. Both work; the globe would show two different
   things under almost the same name. Either vanilla bases retire once
   Cobra sites replace them, or one gets renamed.

5. "COBRA SUPPLIES DENIED: 640" ADDS DOLLARS TO CRATES.
   The debriefing line sums funds and materiel into one number. Fine as
   a score, wrong as a sentence. Reporting them separately is a small
   change and I did not want to pick the wording for you.


THE NUMBERS, WHEN YOU WANT TO TUNE
--------------------------------------------------------------------

6. EVERY ECONOMY NUMBER IS A PLACEHOLDER.
   Site income, upkeep, build costs, loot, and how hard Cobra tries to
   build each kind. They are internally coherent, not balanced, and
   nothing has been played.

   The one shape worth preserving: as priced, one of each site nets
   -190 funds a month, so Cobra needs two or three front companies to
   keep a full set alive. If the earners ever get plentiful enough to
   lose painlessly, the target list stops mattering, which was the
   whole reason for this side of it.

7. CAN A REGION GO BROKE, AND THEN WHAT?
   Stockpiles are floored at zero today. Cobra does not borrow, sell up
   or abandon sites. Probably wants an answer eventually.

8. DOES THE PLAYER SEE EXACT STOCKPILES OR ESTIMATES?
   The intel screen currently reports what KNOWN sites earn per month,
   never what a region is actually holding. That was deliberate, and it
   makes finding sites worth doing. Worth confirming you agree.
