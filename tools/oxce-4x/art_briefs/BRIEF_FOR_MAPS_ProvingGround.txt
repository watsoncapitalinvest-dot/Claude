BUILD -> MAPS, 13 Nov 2026: BEACH HEAD'S PROVING GROUND
Design: claude/BUILD_DESIGN_ANSWERS_13Nov_Evening.md, A4. Same kit, camera, scale and delivery as the van stop and the ambush.
It is a drill, not a fight: every hit only stuns. Beach Head and 6-8 of his cadre defend a training course; the Joes have to put all of them down.
Until this map arrives the game uses the Cartel Ranch as a stand-in.

MAP: PROVING (Beach Head's course)
- Name: PROVING. 50 x 40 x 2 (sizes in multiples of 10), ground on level 0.
- An Army training area in scrub country, daytime: an obstacle course (log walls, a rope climb frame, a crawl pit under wire, tyre runs), a shoot house (a plywood building of 4-5 rooms with no roof, open doorways, target cut-outs inside), a two-storey wooden range tower, sandbag lines, a few parked jeeps and a field tent with folding tables.
- Joe start: 12 rank-1 nodes on the south edge, by a gravel road.
- Cadre: 1 node for Beach Head at the top of the range tower (rank 0), 8 rank-2 spawn nodes spread over the shoot house, the obstacle course and the sandbag lines, patrol links between them.
- Reward spot: the range tower (whoever holds it sees most of the course). A clue spot is not needed.
