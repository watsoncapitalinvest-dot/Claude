3D MODELS - THE SEPARATE LIST

These cannot be drawn as pictures. Each is a body the battlefield sprites are
RENDERED from, turned through eight facings and run through the walk, aim,
fire and die animations. A painting cannot do that job.

The spec every one of them has to meet is in MODEL_SPEC.txt, which is already
written: Mixamo skeleton, bone names unchanged, A-pose, feet at Y=0, facing
+Z, head about one seventh of total height.

The character pictures in COBRA_CHARACTERS_BRIEF.txt and
JOE_CHARACTERS_BRIEF.txt are NOT replaced by this list. They are the textures
painted onto these bodies. Pictures first, then the body wears them.


HUMAN BODIES (3)
One body serves many characters - a character is a change of colours and of
which gear is switched on, not a new model. So this is a short list on
purpose.

m_body_male.png / .fbx - the standard male body. Serves most of the roster.
  This is the one that already exists; everything else here is new.

m_body_female.fbx - the female body. Different proportions, same skeleton and
  same bone names so every animation retargets. Needed for the Baroness,
  Zarana, Vypra, Scarlett and the Cobra women.

m_body_heavy.fbx - a heavy build on the same skeleton, for Road Pig, Big Boa,
  Gnawgahyde and the Dreadnok bruisers. Without it they are the standard body
  in different paint and they stop reading as heavies.


NON-HUMAN UNITS (5)
These have nothing in common with the human skeleton and each needs its own
rig and its own walk.

m_bat.fbx - the Battle Android Trooper. Humanoid but mechanical: stiff
  joints, exposed actuators, no neck. A separate body, not a repaint.

m_crocodile.fbx - Croc Master's crocodiles. Four legs, low to the ground.

m_falcon.fbx - Raptor's birds. Wings, perched and in flight.

m_boar.fbx - Gnawgahyde's boars. Four legs, charging.

m_toxo_zombie.fbx - the Toxo-Zombies. Can reuse the male skeleton but wants
  its own shambling walk, so it is listed here rather than as a repaint.


BATTLEFIELD VEHICLES (4)
Vehicles the player meets ON the ground, which need a model for the same
reason the men do. The craft in VEHICLES_BRIEF.txt that only ever appear on
the world map or in a dossier do NOT need models - a picture is enough.

m_hiss.fbx - the H.I.S.S. tank. A large unit, two tiles square.
m_stinger.fbx - the Stinger armed jeep.
m_fang.fbx - the F.A.N.G. one-man helicopter.
m_water_moccasin.fbx - Copperhead's swamp boat, for the water battles.


WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT HERE

Weapons. Held weapons are painted flat, not modelled - the game draws them
from the hand sprites, and ITEMS_BRIEF.txt already covers how they are
painted. Do not model guns.
