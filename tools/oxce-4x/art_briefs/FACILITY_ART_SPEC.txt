BASE FACILITIES - ROOM ART FOR THE BASE PLANNING MAP
====================================================

This is the art for the base screen: a 6 x 6 grid seen from directly above, one
room per square or per block of squares, the way a commander sees the floor
plan of his headquarters. The list of every room, its size and its file name is
in FACILITY_MANIFEST.txt, which comes with this.


THE VIEW - THE ONE RULE THAT MATTERS MOST
-----------------------------------------
Straight down. Look at each room from directly overhead with the ceiling
removed, like an architect's cutaway floor plan drawn in full colour.

    - NOT isometric. NOT at an angle. NOT a 3D render seen from the side.
      A previous set came back isometric, and an angled room inside a square
      grid reads as a diamond with empty corners. It could not be used.
    - Walls are seen from the top, as thick borders.
    - Furniture and equipment are seen from the top: the tops of beds, desks,
      vehicles, crates, machines.

Fill the room. A real floor plan has its furniture lined along the walls and an
empty middle, and at this size an empty middle reads as a blank square. Put the
room's character in the centre - rows of bunks, a vehicle on its bay, a reactor
core, a firing lane - so every room is recognisable at a glance.


SIZES
-----
One grid square is 192 x 192 pixels. A room is as many squares as its
footprint, delivered as ONE image:

    1 x 1 room   192 x 192
    2 x 1 room   384 x 192     (two squares side by side)
    1 x 2 room   192 x 384     (two squares one above the other)
    2 x 2 room   384 x 384

Draw a multi-square room as one continuous room. The game cuts it into squares
itself. Do not draw seams or borders where one square meets the next inside the
same room.


EDGES - LEAVE A GAP FOR THE FLOOR PLATE
---------------------------------------
Every room sits on a floor plate the game draws underneath it, and the plate
has to show as a thin rim so the base reads as separate rooms rather than one
grey field.

    - On every OUTSIDE edge of the room, leave a clear margin of 12 pixels.
    - Cut the four outside corners back by 12 pixels as well (a chamfer).
    - INSIDE a multi-square room there is no margin - it is one room.

The margin is background (see below), not a drawn border.


BACKGROUND
----------
Flat pure magenta, hex FF00FF, in the margin and anywhere that is not room.
Hard edges only: a pixel is room or it is magenta, no soft blend between them.
Do not use transparency and do not paint a chequerboard.


THE THREE BASES - THEMES IN THE MANIFEST
----------------------------------------
    joe      The Pit: the team's underground headquarters. Poured concrete,
             olive drab paint, yellow-and-black hazard striping at doorways,
             caged ceiling lamps seen from above, worn rubber matting.
    naval / outdoor / space
             Joe outposts. Same team, same kit: naval rooms add steel deck plate
             and marine fittings; outdoor rooms are open-air pads, revetments
             and sandbags seen from above; space rooms are clean white launch
             hardware.
    cobra    Cobra installations. Polished dark steel and glossy deep blue, with
             red accent lighting and a cold, clinical look. Cobra emblem is NOT
             to be used - see below.

Within a theme every room must look like part of the same building: same floor
material, same wall thickness, same lighting.


LIGHT AND READABILITY
---------------------
    - Light from the upper left, same in every room.
    - On screen a square is about 170 pixels across. Strong shapes, clear
      contrast between floor and objects. Fine detail that only shows at full
      size is wasted.
    - Keep each room's floor a little lighter than its walls so the walls read.


ALSO NEEDED - SHARED TILES (one set, used everywhere)
-----------------------------------------------------
    ground.png              192 x 192   empty rock or earth with no room in it,
                                        seen from above. Tiles seamlessly with
                                        itself in all four directions.
    corridor_h.png          192 x 192   a short corridor running LEFT TO RIGHT
                                        through the middle, 48 pixels wide, ends
                                        open at both sides. Magenta everywhere
                                        else. It is drawn across the line
                                        between two rooms that sit side by side.
    corridor_v.png          192 x 192   the same corridor running TOP TO
                                        BOTTOM.
    plate_*.png             one per footprint (1x1, 2x1, 1x2, 2x2) at the
                                        sizes above: a plain concrete floor plate
                                        with a raised rim, the thing every room
                                        sits on.
    plate_*_build.png       the same plates under construction: bare outline,
                                        scaffold and tape, no floor yet.


DO NOT INCLUDE
--------------
    - Any text, letters, numbers, room names or signage.
    - Any logo, emblem, insignia or trade dress from an existing toy, film or
      game - this includes the Cobra symbol and any G.I. Joe logo.
    - People. No figures at all; the rooms are shown empty.
    - Vehicles or aircraft parked in hangars - the game draws the craft itself
      on top of the hangar. Leave the hangar floor clear in the middle.


FILE NAMES
----------
Use the file name given for each room in FACILITY_MANIFEST.txt exactly, for
example j_barracks.png or c_reactor.png. One file per room, PNG.


CHECK BEFORE SENDING
--------------------
    1. Is the room seen from DIRECTLY ABOVE, not at an angle?
    2. Is the image exactly the size the manifest gives for that room?
    3. Is there a 12-pixel magenta margin on every outside edge, with the
       outside corners cut back, and no margin inside a multi-square room?
    4. Is the middle of the room occupied by what makes it that room?
    5. No text, no logos, no people, no parked craft?
