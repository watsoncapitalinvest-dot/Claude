MAP 1: PIT PERIMETER (a strike team at the Joes' own base)
- Name: PITGATE. 50 x 50 x 2 (sizes in multiples of 10), ground on level 0. Night or dusk.
- What the Pit looks like from outside: its cover story. A fenced motor-pool yard and a run-down vehicle depot (two big shed buildings, one with roll-up doors), a guard hut at the gate, floodlight poles, a fuel bowser, parked trucks, an access road in from the south, scrub and a drainage ditch outside the fence.
- One shed hides the Pit's lift: a 2 x 2 lift pad inside it (mark it in the spots file). That is what the hunters are trying to reach.
- Joe start: 12 rank-1 nodes inside the fence, around the lift shed (the Joes come up from below).
- Hunters: 8 rank-2 spawn nodes outside the fence on the north and east sides (in the ditch, behind parked trucks), patrol links toward the gate and the lift shed. One cut in the fence on the east side.
- Reward spot: the guard hut roof or a floodlight gantry (sees the whole yard). A clue spot: a hunter's dropped dossier on the target, near the fence cut.

MAP 2: HUNTER AMBUSH (a craft forced down to meet them)
- Name: HUNTAMB. 50 x 40 x 2, ground on level 0. Daytime.
- A rural airstrip or open field where a Tomahawk has had to set down: a grass landing area in the middle (leave a clear 4 x 4 for the craft), a farm road along one side, a hay barn, a pump house, a stand of trees, a stalled truck the hunters arrived in.
- Joe start: 12 rank-1 nodes around the landing area.
- Hunters: 8 rank-2 nodes in the barn, the trees and behind the truck, patrol links closing in on the landing area from three sides.
- Reward spot: the barn loft (sees the field). A clue spot: the hunters' radio in the truck cab.
