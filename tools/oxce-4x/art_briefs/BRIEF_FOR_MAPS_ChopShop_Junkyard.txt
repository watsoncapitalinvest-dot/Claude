MAP 1: CHOP SHOP (rescue Clutch)
- Name: CHOPSHOP. 40 x 40 x 2 (sizes in multiples of 10), ground on level 0.
- A run-down garage on a back road: a two-bay workshop with roll-up doors, a back office, a fenced yard of stripped cars on blocks, engine hoists, tyre stacks, an oil drum or two.
- Clutch is held in the back office: 1 civilian node (rank 0) there. He stays put until freed.
- Joe start: 12 rank-1 nodes at the road edge, south side.
- Cartel: 10 rank-2 spawn nodes in the workshop and the yard, 2 in the back office doorway, patrol links between the bays and the yard.
- What decides it (BUILD wires it): the Joes win by reaching Clutch and clearing the site.

MAP 2: JUNKYARD (retake his VAMP)
- Name: JUNKYARD. 50 x 50 x 2, ground on level 0.
- Stacks of wrecked cars two high, narrow lanes between them, a car crusher, a crane, a site office trailer, a corrugated shed.
- The VAMP: a 2x2 jeep under a tarp inside the shed. Mark the 4 tiles in the spots file; BUILD places the vehicle.
- Joe start: 12 rank-1 nodes at the gate, west side.
- Cartel: 12 rank-2 spawn nodes spread through the stacks and the office trailer, patrol links along the lanes.
