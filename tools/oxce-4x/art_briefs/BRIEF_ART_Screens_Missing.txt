SCREENS STILL MISSING - ART BRIEF (from BUILD, 13 Nov 2026)

These are the screens that still show the old 1994 X-COM pictures, or a plain dark panel.
Style: the same as the turn screen you just made (G.I. Joe logo art, olive metal frame with bolts, green radar corners), 1980s Saturday-morning cartoon, bold black outlines, flat colour with two-tone shading, no photo textures.

GENERAL
- PNG, 1920 x 1200, not transparent, unless it says otherwise.
- The game writes its own text, numbers and buttons on top. Where a section says "keep clear", that area must be plain and dark: no figures, no logos, no detail.
- No text in the pictures except the G.I. Joe: USA Defense logo where it says so.
- No real-world brand marks.
- Name the files exactly as below. Zip them as screens_missing.zip, one folder per section, into Reference\.

1. COBRA'S TURN (shown while Cobra moves)
- cobra_turn_1.png, cobra_turn_2.png, cobra_turn_3.png: three different pictures, so the screen changes from turn to turn.
- Each one: Cobra on the move at night, seen from a distance. Picture 1: Cobra troopers running in a line past a wire fence. Picture 2: a Cobra H.I.S.S. tank rolling past ruins. Picture 3: Cobra Commander watching a monitor bank in a dark command room.
- Keep clear: a band across the middle, from y 520 to y 680, the full width. The words HIDDEN MOVEMENT go there.
- Keep clear: the top right corner, from x 1150 to x 1880 and y 40 to y 200. A progress bar goes there.

2. COBRA SIGHTING ALERT (the pop-up on the world map when a Cobra site, truck, convoy or robbery is spotted)
- alert_cobra.png: a radar operator's console in The Pit at night, a big round green radar screen with one red blip, red warning lights.
- Keep clear: a box in the middle, from x 400 to x 1520 and y 300 to y 900. The alert text goes there.

3. BATTLE POP-UPS (small boxes during a battle: abort the mission, end the mission, the turn diary, saving and loading equipment)
- battle_popup.png: a dark olive metal plate with rivets and a thin worn edge, nothing else on it.
- The whole picture is plain, because the game only shows a small piece of it behind each box.

4. BASE INFORMATION (the screen listing the base's personnel, space and storage)
- base_info.png: a wall-mounted status board in The Pit: olive metal frame, a G.I. Joe eagle emblem small in the top left corner.
- Keep clear: from x 120 to x 1800 and y 140 to y 1120. The game writes the lists and bars there.

5. JOE STATS IN BATTLE (the screen showing one Joe's stats during a battle)
- unit_info.png: a personnel file on a clipboard, seen from straight above, lying on an olive canvas map case.
- Keep clear: the clipboard's paper, from x 160 to x 1760 and y 120 to y 1100. The game writes the stats and draws the bars there.

6. MOTION SCANNER (the hand-held scanner a Joe uses in battle)
- scanner_screen.png: a 1980s military hand-held scanner filling the frame, seen straight on, chunky olive casing, knobs, a big round green radar face in the middle.
- The radar face is a plain dark circle with a faint grid and nothing drawn on it: centre at x 960, y 600, diameter 760.
- scanner_blip_1.png to scanner_blip_4.png, 64 x 64, transparent: one contact on the radar, a green dot pulsing from small and bright to large and faint. 4 frames.

7. MEDKIT (treating a wounded Joe in battle)
- medkit_screen.png: an open olive field-medic case lying flat, seen from above, with a large plain body diagram card on the left half (front view of a standing man, outline only, arms slightly out).
- Keep clear: the right third of the case, plain dark cloth. The game puts its buttons and numbers there.
- medkit_part_head.png, medkit_part_torso.png, medkit_part_left_arm.png, medkit_part_right_arm.png, medkit_part_left_leg.png, medkit_part_right_leg.png: each part of that same body diagram on its own, same size and position as on medkit_screen.png, transparent everywhere else, filled solid red.

8. REPORTS / GRAPHS
- graphs_screen.png: a situation-room wall chart board in The Pit. Olive metal frame.
- Keep clear: a plain dark-green plotting area with a faint grid, from x 360 to x 1840 and y 120 to y 1080. The game draws the lines and numbers on it.
- Keep clear: the column on the left, from x 0 to x 340, a plain metal panel. The game's buttons go there.
