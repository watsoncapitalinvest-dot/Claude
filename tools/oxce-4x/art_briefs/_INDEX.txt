RULES THAT APPLY TO EVERY BRIEF
-------------------------------
One subject per file. Background pure magenta FF00FF everywhere the subject is
not, through every gap, hard edges. No lettering. No logos, emblems, insignia,
flags, unit numbers or brand names - the Cobra symbol and G.I. Joe logo
included. No people unless the brief is for a person. Exact file names. If the
artist does not know what a named character or vehicle looked like, it says so
and skips it.
