RULES THAT APPLY TO EVERY BRIEF HERE

  One subject per file. Never a chart, a sheet of variants or a row of
  things on one page.

  Background pure magenta, hex FF00FF, everywhere the subject is not,
  including through every gap.

  Flat even overcast light. No sun, no rim light, no cast shadow, no ground
  under the subject. The lighting is added later in the game.

  No lettering anywhere. No logos, no insignia, no unit numbers, no brand
  names.

  Exact filenames. They are read by a script.

  If you do not know what a named character or a named vehicle actually
  looked like, SAY SO AND SKIP IT. Do not invent one. A missing item can be
  chased; a wrong one looks right until it is in the game beside the others.
