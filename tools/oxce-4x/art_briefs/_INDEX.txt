RULES THAT APPLY TO EVERY BRIEF
-------------------------------
One subject per file. Background pure magenta FF00FF everywhere the subject is
not, through every gap, hard edges. No lettering the game sets itself (labels,
titles, captions). LOGOS ARE INCLUDED (John, 12 Nov, permanent): the G.I. Joe
logo, the Cobra emblem, insignia, roundels and markings are drawn wherever the
real thing carries them, and they go in the repo. No people unless the brief is for a person. Exact file names. If the
artist does not know what a named character or vehicle looked like, it says so
and skips it.
