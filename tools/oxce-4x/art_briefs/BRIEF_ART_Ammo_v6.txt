AMMO - ART REQUEST v6 (from BUILD, 13 Nov 2026)
This replaces every earlier ammo request.

WHAT THIS IS
Eight magazines, one for each SIZE of ammunition. Every gun of that size loads the same magazine.

STYLE
Same as the gun art:
- 1980s Saturday-morning cartoon
- bold black outlines
- flat colour with two-tone shading
- no photo textures
- no text except the stencils named below

TWO PICTURES PER MAGAZINE
1. <file>_big.png, 128 x 192 pixels, transparent background.
   - The magazine in the equipment screen, seen STRAIGHT FROM ABOVE, lying flat. Not tilted.
   - It must FILL its footprint, with 4 px clear at the edges. One slot is 64 x 64 px.
     - 1 x 1 fills a 56 x 56 box in the middle of the canvas.
     - 1 x 2 fills 56 x 120.
2. <file>_floor.png, 128 x 160 pixels, transparent background.
   - The same magazine on the battle floor, seen from the game's camera, centred on the 128 x 64 tile diamond at the bottom of the canvas.
   - It must be 40 to 60 px across so it reads on the map.

THE EIGHT (game name - file - footprint - what to draw)
1. Small Magazine - mag_small - 1 x 2
   A long straight black steel magazine, short brass pistol rounds showing at the top.
2. Medium Magazine - mag_medium - 1 x 2
   A curved dull grey-black rifle magazine, long pointed brass rifle rounds showing at the top. Must read as clearly bigger than the small one.
3. Large Magazine - mag_large - 1 x 2
   An olive steel ammo can, lid shut, with a short length of big linked brass rounds hanging out of it. Stencil "LINKED" in yellow.
4. Shotgun Shells - mag_shotgun - 1 x 1
   Eight red plastic shells with brass bases, side by side, tied with a band.
5. Rocket - mag_rocket - 1 x 2
   An olive sealed rocket tube with end caps and a yellow band. Stencil "HE" in yellow.
6. Mortar Bomb - mag_mortar - 1 x 2
   A teardrop bomb with tail fins: olive body, yellow band, brass nose fuze.
7. Energy Cell - mag_energy - 1 x 1
   A chunky rectangular power pack, dark grey, glowing green charge strip, two contacts. 1982 sci-fi.
8. Fuel Tank - mag_fuel - 1 x 2
   A steel fuel bottle, olive with a red band, a valve and a short hose stub on top.
