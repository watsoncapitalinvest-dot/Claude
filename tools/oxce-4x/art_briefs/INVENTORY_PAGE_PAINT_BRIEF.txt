EQUIPMENT SCREEN - PAINT THE WHOLE PAGE
=======================================

Two images come with this:

    inventory_for_painting_1920x1200.png   the page to paint over
    inventory_guide_1920x1200.png          the same page with every fixed area outlined

Deliver ONE image back: the painted page, exactly 1920 x 1200, PNG.

This is the screen where a soldier is loaded out before and during a mission:
his figure in the middle, compartments for each place he can carry gear, a
long tray along the bottom for what is on the ground, and keys on the right.


WHAT TO DO
----------
Paint it as the equipment locker panel inside a G.I. Joe command vehicle:
olive-drab painted steel, the compartments cut into it, the keys set into it.

1. The steel. Painted olive drab over plate steel. Add:
     - chipped paint at the edges of the compartments and along the bottom tray
     - scuffs and polish where hands reach in, around the compartments and keys
     - grime settled into the bottom edge of each compartment
     - faint scratches, mostly vertical; a few weld seams and rivet lines
       running between the groups of compartments are welcome
   Light from the upper left. Worn and used, not wrecked - no rust holes,
   no dents, no bullet holes.

2. The compartments (magenta on the guide). Each is a recess cut into the
   steel. Weather the rim around each one freely. Keep the floor of every
   one flat and DARK, near black - equipment pictures are laid into them and
   must read clearly.

3. The keys (cyan on the guide). Five physical push-keys: pressed steel or hard
   rubber caps set into the panel. Make them read as buttons a gloved hand
   presses. Leave their faces BLANK - the game writes OK, the arrows and
   UNLOAD on them itself.


WHAT MUST NOT MOVE
------------------
  MAGENTA boxes   Compartments. Do not move, resize or reshape any. Inside
                  each: flat, near black, no texture, no grime, no light.

  CYAN boxes      Keys. Each key must sit inside its own cyan box and fill
                  most of it. Faces blank.

  YELLOW boxes    Where the game prints text: the soldier's name, his time
                  units, slot names, the name of the item in his hand. Keep
                  these as plain painted metal - no marks, plates, stencils,
                  scratches heavy enough to compete with text, or symbols.

  WHITE boxes     Top left: his rank badge. Centre left, tall: his figure.
                  Both are drawn over the page by the game. Keep them plain,
                  quiet metal; do not draw a figure, a badge or a frame there.


DO NOT INCLUDE
--------------
    - Any text, letters, numbers or symbols anywhere.
    - Any logo, insignia, emblem, flag or trade dress from an existing toy,
      film, cartoon or game.
    - A soldier, a badge, weapons or equipment. The page is empty; the game
      fills it.
    - Anything outside the 1920 x 1200 frame, and no change to the frame size.
    - Transparency: the page fills the whole image, edge to edge.


CHECK BEFORE SENDING
--------------------
    1. Lay your result over the guide. Does every magenta box still sit
       exactly over a dark compartment of the same size?
    2. Is every compartment floor still flat and near black?
    3. Is each key inside its own cyan box, with a blank face?
    4. Are the yellow and white areas plain metal with nothing drawn in them?
    5. Is it exactly 1920 x 1200?
