BATTLE BAR ICONS - EIGHT ACTION ICONS
=====================================

LOGOS ARE INCLUDED (John's permanent rule, 12 Nov 2026) - not that these
need any.

STYLE - REAL SHAPES, CARTOON FINISH (approved 12 Nov)
Bold clean dark outlines, flat colour, no texture, grain or gradient. They
must sit beside the approved soldiers and the rest of the cartoon art.

WHAT THEY ARE
Eight small icons for the action buttons on the battle control bar. Each
sits on a dark steel button (colour 2A3033) with the word under it, so the
icon must read at a glance at about 40 pixels across on screen.

EACH ICON
    Size:     exactly 128 x 128 pixels, PNG.
    Background: pure magenta FF00FF everywhere that is not icon, hard edges.
    Colours:  TWO only, plus the outline -
                bone   E9E6DC   the main shape
                yellow E8B923   the one part that says what the button does
                (an arrow, the moving part)
              and a dark outline 14181A round every shape, about 6 px.
    Fill:     the icon fills the square with about 8 px clear all round.
    No text, no letters, no numbers, no frame, no button shape - only the
    symbol. The game draws the button and the word.

THE EIGHT
    icon_level_up.png     go up a floor: a stack of two flat floors seen at
                          an angle, a yellow arrow pointing up out of the top
    icon_level_down.png   the same stack, a yellow arrow pointing down
    icon_kneel.png        a soldier silhouette down on one knee, rifle held
                          upright; the bent knee in yellow
    icon_center.png       a crosshair ring with a small soldier silhouette in
                          the middle; the four crosshair ticks in yellow
    icon_next.png         two soldier silhouettes side by side, a yellow
                          arrow from the left one to the right one
    icon_map.png          a folded paper map, three panels; a yellow location
                          marker on it
    icon_gear.png         a backpack seen from the front with a rifle across
                          it; the pack's buckles in yellow
    icon_options.png      a cog wheel; the hub in yellow

All eight must look like one set: the same outline weight, the same two
colours, the same amount of detail. Simple - a shape you can recognise when
it is the size of a fingernail.


CHECK BEFORE SENDING
--------------------

    2. Only bone, yellow and the dark outline?
    3. No letters or numbers?
    4. Do all eight look like one set?
