FRONT END ART - THE SCREENS BEFORE THE GAME STARTS
==================================================

STYLE - REAL SHAPES, CARTOON FINISH (approved 12 Nov)
Real-world shapes and proportions, drawn the way the 1980s Sunbow G.I. Joe
cartoon draws things, leaning cartoon: bold clean dark outlines, flat colour
with one flat cel-shadow tone (two at most), no texture, grain, noise or
photographic detail. It must sit beside the approved soldiers
(Reference\character_art\approved\duke_1984.png and snake_eyes_1985.png).

EVERY PICTURE HERE
    Size:    exactly 1920 x 1200 pixels, PNG.
    Text:    NONE. No title, no lettering, no button labels, no numbers.
             The game sets every word itself, live and sharp.
    Logos:   none - no G.I. Joe logo, no Cobra emblem, no insignia, flags
             or badges. Leave a plain patch where one would be.
    Colours: dark olive steel and gunmetal, with signal yellow (E8B923) and
             Cobra red (C0392B) used sparingly as accents. The game's buttons
             are dark steel with cream lettering, so the art behind them must
             be darker than mid-grey wherever a button or text sits.

Four jobs. Do them in this order.


1. THE TITLE SCREEN
-------------------
    File:   title_screen.png
    What:   the key art for the game. A G.I. Joe strike team - Duke, Snake
            Eyes, Roadblock, Scarlett - moving in on a Cobra installation at
            dusk, Cobra troopers and a H.I.S.S. tank in the distance. Heroic,
            mid-action, the Joes large and to the LEFT.
    Keep clear:
      - TOP BAND, the whole width, top 240 pixels: sky or dark, even and
        calm. The game title is set there in large stencil letters.
      - RIGHT COLUMN, x 1260 to 1860, y 520 to 1140: dark and even (shadow,
        smoke, night sky, the side of a building). Six menu buttons sit
        there in a column: New Game, New Battle, Load Game, Options, Mods,
        Quit.
    The Joes must not stand behind the button column or under the title.


2. THE MENU PLATE - ONE PICTURE FOR FOUR SCREENS
------------------------------------------------
    File:   menu_plate.png
    Used behind: New Game, Options, Load / Save, Mods. All four fill the
            whole screen with lists, sliders, buttons and text.
    What:   the surface of a command console in The Pit seen straight on: a
            big dark steel plate, bolted, with a raised rim round the edge.
    Rules:
      - The INSIDE, from 60 pixels in on every side, is ONE flat even dark
        tone (about 1E2426) with nothing on it - no panels, vents, screws,
        seams or gauges. Lists and text are drawn there.
      - Detail only in the rim: bolts, a hazard stripe along the top edge,
        a little wear on the corners.
      - Flat and straight on, no perspective, no light from one side.


3. THE OPENING BRIEFING - FOUR STILLS
-------------------------------------
The campaign opens on four pictures with a line of text under each, like a
storyboard. Each still is a full picture, 1920 x 1200.
Keep the BOTTOM 300 pixels of every still dark and calm: the narration is
set there.

    brief_1_situation_room.png
        General Hawk at a briefing table in a dim situation room, a wall
        screen behind him showing a world map with a few red markers.
        He is pointing at the map.
    brief_2_broadcast.png
        A large TV monitor filling most of the picture: a masked Cobra leader
        speaking to camera on the screen, the image a little rough like an
        intercepted broadcast. NO emblem on his uniform or behind him.
    brief_3_trace.png
        Two Joes at a bank of 1980s tracking consoles - green radar sweep,
        a line drawn across a map to one point - one of them turning to call
        out the result.
    brief_4_launch.png
        A twin-rotor transport helicopter lifting off a pad at night, Joes
        boarding the open rear ramp, rotor wash and landing lights.

-----------------------------------
"Watson Capital presents" is in the game. Nothing to do.
