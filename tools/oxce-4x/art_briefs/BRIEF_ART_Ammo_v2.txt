AMMO - ART BRIEF v2 (from BUILD, 13 Nov 2026)
Replaces section 8 of UI REMAINING. Do not use the autocannon or heavy-cannon boxes from that delivery; those guns are gone.

WHAT THIS IS
One picture set for every ammunition item in the approved armory. Each must look like the real thing it is:
- the right shape
- the right length
- the right curve
- the right colour of metal or plastic
- the rounds visible where the real item shows them
The setting is 1982, so nothing more modern than that.

STYLE
Same as the gun art:
- 1980s Saturday-morning cartoon
- bold black outlines
- flat colour with two-tone shading
- no photo textures
- no text or markings except where it says so below

TWO PICTURES PER ITEM
1. <name>_big.png, 128 x 192, transparent.
   - The item in the equipment screen, seen STRAIGHT FROM ABOVE, lying flat, the same way the guns are shown there.
   - Not tilted, not 3/4 view.
   - Draw it inside its slot footprint (below), centred on the canvas. One inventory slot is 64 x 64 px.
2. <name>_floor.png, 128 x 160, transparent.
   - The same item lying on the battle floor, seen from the game's camera.
   - The floor tile is a 128 x 64 diamond at the bottom of the canvas. The item lies on that diamond, small, at true scale next to a man.

THE ITEMS (name - real item - slot footprint - what it must show)
1. mag_556_20 - M16 20-round magazine - 1 x 2 slots - short STRAIGHT aluminium box magazine, dull grey-black, rounded floor plate, brass round showing at the top.
2. mag_556_30 - M16 30-round magazine - 1 x 2 slots - longer, CURVED aluminium magazine, dull grey-black, brass round at the top. Clearly longer and curved next to item 1.
3. mag_762_ak - AK-47 30-round magazine - 1 x 2 slots - the deep banana curve, steel, dark brown-grey, ribbed sides, copper-washed round at the top. This one is Cobra's.
4. mag_9mm_smg - Uzi-type 9 mm 32-round magazine - 1 x 2 slots - long STRAIGHT thin steel magazine, black, brass round at the top. One magazine serves all three SMGs.
5. mag_45_pistol - M1911A1 7-round magazine - 1 x 1 slot - short straight single-stack steel magazine, blued black, brass round at the top.
6. speedloader_44 - .44 revolver speedloader - 1 x 1 slot - a round six-hole loader with six brass cartridge bases showing, seen from above, black knob in the middle.
7. shells_12g - 12-gauge shells - 1 x 1 slot - eight red plastic shotgun shells with brass bases, lying side by side, tied with a band.
8. belt_762 - M60 7.62 mm belt - 2 x 2 slots - an olive steel M19A1-type ammo can, lid shut, with a short length of linked belt hanging out of the lid. Brass rounds, dark links. Stencil "7.62" in yellow on the can.
9. box_50cal - M2 .50 cal ammo box - 2 x 2 slots - a bigger olive M2A1 ammo can, lid shut, with a short length of large linked .50 rounds hanging out. Stencil ".50 CAL" in yellow on the can. It must be clearly bigger and chunkier than item 8.
10. rocket_83mm - SMAW 83 mm rocket - 1 x 3 slots - an olive sealed launch tube: the round as it is carried, a fat tube with end caps. Stencil "HEDP" in yellow.
11. bomb_81mm - 81 mm mortar bomb - 1 x 2 slots - a teardrop bomb with tail fins, olive body with a yellow band (high explosive), brass-coloured fuze on the nose.
12. bolt_xk1 - XK-1 power crossbow bolt - 1 x 2 slots - one short heavy bolt, black shaft, grey steel broadhead, three short olive vanes.
13. battery_laser - XMLR-1A laser battery - 1 x 1 slot - a chunky rectangular power pack, dark grey with a green charge strip and two contacts on top. Sci-fi but 1982 sci-fi.
14. missile_javelin - power javelin missile - 1 x 3 slots - a long slim missile with folded fins, olive with a yellow band, black seeker nose.
15. flares_rescue - rescue flare cartridges - 1 x 1 slot - three fat short cartridges, red with brass bases, in a little cloth bandolier.
