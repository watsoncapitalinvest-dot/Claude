AMMO - ART BRIEF v2 (from BUILD, 13 Nov 2026)
Replaces section 8 of UI REMAINING. Do not use the autocannon or heavy-cannon boxes from that delivery; those guns are gone.

WHAT THIS IS
One picture set for each KIND of ammunition. Each must look like real 1982 ammunition of that kind:
- the right shape
- the right length and curve
- the right colour of metal or plastic
- the rounds visible where the real item shows them
The setting is 1982, so nothing more modern than that.

STYLE
Same as the gun art:
- 1980s Saturday-morning cartoon
- bold black outlines
- flat colour with two-tone shading
- no photo textures
- no text or markings except where it says so below

TWO PICTURES PER ITEM
1. <name>_big.png, 128 x 192, transparent.
   - The item in the equipment screen, seen STRAIGHT FROM ABOVE, lying flat, the same way the guns are shown there.
   - Not tilted, not 3/4 view.
   - Draw it inside its slot footprint (below), centred on the canvas. One inventory slot is 64 x 64 px.
2. <name>_floor.png, 128 x 160, transparent.
   - The same item lying on the battle floor, seen from the game's camera.
   - The floor tile is a 128 x 64 diamond at the bottom of the canvas. The item lies on that diamond, small, at true scale next to a man.

THE ITEMS (name - what it is - slot footprint - what it must show)
Ammo is by KIND, not one item per gun.
1. ammo_small - small-calibre rounds (pistols and SMGs) - 1 x 2 slots - a straight black steel magazine with brass rounds showing at the top, plus a small open cardboard box of brass pistol rounds beside it.
2. ammo_rifle - rifle rounds (assault rifles) - 1 x 2 slots - a CURVED grey-black rifle magazine, brass pointed rounds at the top, clearly longer and slimmer-tipped than the small-calibre rounds.
3. ammo_large - large-calibre belted rounds (machine guns) - 2 x 2 slots - an olive steel ammo can, lid shut, with a length of linked big brass rounds hanging out of the lid. Stencil "LINKED" in yellow on the can.
4. ammo_shells - shotgun shells - 1 x 1 slot - eight red plastic shells with brass bases, side by side, tied with a band.
5. ammo_rocket - rockets and missiles - 1 x 3 slots - an olive sealed rocket tube with end caps, a yellow band near one end. Stencil "HE" in yellow.
6. ammo_mortar - mortar bombs - 1 x 2 slots - a teardrop bomb with tail fins, olive body, yellow band, brass-coloured nose fuze.
7. ammo_bolt - crossbow bolts - 1 x 2 slots - three short heavy bolts in a small quiver clip: black shafts, steel broadheads, olive vanes.
8. ammo_cell - energy cells (lasers now, other energy weapons later) - 1 x 1 slot - a chunky rectangular power pack, dark grey with a green charge strip and two contacts. Sci-fi, but 1982 sci-fi.
9. ammo_flare - flare cartridges - 1 x 1 slot - three fat short cartridges, red with brass bases, in a little cloth bandolier.

NOT IN THIS BRIEF
- Frag and smoke grenades: John has them modelled in 3D, and BUILD renders them.
- Sword and swagger stick: melee, no ammo.
