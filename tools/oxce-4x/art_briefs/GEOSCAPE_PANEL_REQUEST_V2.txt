GEOSCAPE SIDE PANEL - REQUEST 2
===============================

The look you delivered is right. Olive painted steel, recessed slots, machined
bevels, the knurled dials and the directional arrows - keep all of it exactly as
it is. Nothing below is about style. It is about size and position only.

Three deliveries have come back with the same three faults, so this request asks
for a DIFFERENT SHAPE OF FILE that removes two of them entirely.


WHAT TO DELIVER
---------------
ONE image. Just the control panel. Nothing else in the picture.

    Size:        384 wide by 1200 tall. Exactly.
    Background:  pure magenta, hex FF00FF, in every gap and around every edge.
    Format:      PNG.

Do NOT deliver a wide image with the panel sitting on one side. Do not include
the world map area, or black space, or a border. The file is the panel and
nothing but the panel, from its left edge to its right edge.

If 384 x 1200 exactly is not possible, any size with that same tall narrow shape
is fine - 512 x 1600, 768 x 2400 - as long as the proportions are exactly 1 wide
to 3.125 tall and the panel fills the whole file edge to edge.


WHY MAGENTA AND NOT TRANSPARENCY
--------------------------------
Two of the three deliveries came back as a picture OF a transparency chequerboard
- the grey and white squares painted into the image as actual pixels, with no
transparency in the file at all. Delivered that way it paints a grey chequer over
the game.

So do not use transparency. Fill every area that is not panel with flat pure
magenta, FF00FF, including through any gap in the metal. Magenta is removed
automatically afterwards. This is the same convention the weapon pictures use and
it has never failed.


DRAW NO TEXT, NO NUMBERS, NO SYMBOLS
------------------------------------
The game prints every word and every digit itself, on top of this picture, while
it runs. Anything written into the art collides with the game's own text and both
become unreadable.

Draw the empty bezel only. Every slot listed below is an EMPTY recess.


THE SLOTS - EXACT POSITIONS
---------------------------
Positions are in pixels on the 384 x 1200 file: x across from the left edge, y
down from the top, then width and height. If you work at a different size, scale
all of these by the same factor.

These are not a guide to be spaced by eye. The game draws its text at these exact
places. In all three deliveries so far the top few slots were close and then every
slot below the clock sat 38 to 60 pixels too low, because they had been spaced
evenly instead of placed. By the bottom row that is most of a button's height, and
the game's text lands on the metal between two slots.

    SIX COMMAND BUTTONS, each 378 wide by 66 tall, left edge x=6:
        y = 0
        y = 72
        y = 144
        y = 216
        y = 288
        y = 360

    ONE MONEY STRIP, 354 wide by 48 tall:
        x = 18,  y = 438

    THE CLOCK. Treat all five of these as ONE recessed readout window with these
    areas kept clear inside it, not as five separate buttons:
        hours     x = 18,  y = 444,  120 wide, 96 tall
        colon     x = 138, y = 444,   24 wide, 96 tall
        minutes   x = 162, y = 444,  120 wide, 96 tall
        colon     x = 282, y = 444,   24 wide, 96 tall
        seconds   x = 306, y = 480,   66 wide, 48 tall

    THE DATE, three strips:
        day       x = 18,  y = 564,  174 wide, 48 tall
        month     x = 192, y = 564,  174 wide, 48 tall
        year      x = 18,  y = 606,  354 wide, 48 tall

    SIX SPEED BUTTONS, each 186 wide by 78 tall, in two columns:
        x = 6,   y = 672          x = 198, y = 672
        x = 6,   y = 756          x = 198, y = 756
        x = 6,   y = 840          x = 198, y = 840

    THE CONTROL CLUSTER:
        from y = 912 to y = 1200, the full 384 width.
        This is the one area with freedom. Four directional arrows around a
        centre, and two round controls. Draw them as real physical controls -
        pressed metal arrows, knurled dials. These are the only shapes in the
        whole image that may read as icons, because the game draws nothing on
        top of them.

CHECK BEFORE SENDING: the lowest speed button starts at y=840 and is 78 tall, so
its bottom edge is y=918, and the control cluster begins below that at y=912.
If your lowest button's bottom edge is not within a few pixels of y=918, the
spacing has drifted and the whole column needs placing again from the numbers.


INSIDE EACH SLOT
----------------
Keep the interior of every slot flat and DARK. The game prints pale text on it and
that text has to stay readable. No texture, no highlight, no engraving, no rivets
inside a slot. Put all the detail into the metal between and around the slots.


THE REST
--------
    - Olive drab and gunmetal for the body.
    - Slot interiors near black, very slightly warm.
    - Light from the upper left.
    - Sparing amber or red indicator details in the metal BETWEEN slots.
    - Honest wear: chipped paint at the edges, grime in the recesses. Not rusted.
    - It must read as one continuous machined panel with slots cut into it, not
      as a row of separate floating buttons.

Do not include any lettering, any real military insignia or national markings, or
any logo or trade dress from an existing toy, film or game.
