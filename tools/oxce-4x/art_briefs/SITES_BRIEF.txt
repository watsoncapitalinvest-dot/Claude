COBRA SITES - ONE PICTURE EACH

These are the eight kinds of Cobra installation the player finds on the world
map. Each picture is the dossier photograph shown when a site is identified -
what a surveillance plane or a long lens would have brought back.

EVERY PICTURE
- The whole installation in its setting, seen from the air at a shallow angle,
  as if from a plane a mile out, not straight down.
- Real ground: the terrain the site sits in, and nothing else of interest.
- Daylight, overcast, flat even light. No sunset, no dramatic sky, no rain.
- No Cobra logo, no insignia, no lettering, no flags, no banners. These are
  places that are trying not to be noticed.
- No people, no figures, no vehicles in motion.
- 1536 by 1024 pixels, landscape.
- Photographic and plain, not heroic. It is evidence, not a poster.

Save each as a PNG named exactly as listed.


THE EIGHT (8)

s_front_company.png - an ordinary mid-rise office block in a city business
  district. Completely unremarkable. The whole point is that it looks like
  every other office on the street.

s_factory.png - a working industrial plant: sawtooth roofs, a chimney, a
  loading yard with containers, a perimeter fence. An arms plant pretending
  to be a machine shop.

s_training_camp.png - a cleared compound in remote scrub or jungle: rows of
  tents and low huts, a parade square, an obstacle course, a vehicle park.

s_depot.png - a supply depot: long warehouses, stacked crates and containers
  under tarpaulins, a truck yard, a fuel bowser.

s_research_lab.png - a low windowless concrete building in isolated country,
  with air-handling plant on the roof and its own power compound. Clinical
  and sealed.

s_terror_drome.png - the big one. A fortified regional hub: a wide circular
  concrete structure part-sunk into the ground, a launch opening at its
  centre, radar masts, hardened outbuildings, blast walls, a landing apron.
  Clearly military and clearly expensive.

s_broadcast_station.png - a transmitter site: a tall guyed radio mast, a
  small equipment building at its base, a dish or two, a fenced compound on
  high ground.

s_hidden_hq.png - a command bunker built into a hillside or a quarry face:
  a blast door, vents, a track in, almost nothing above ground. The hardest
  one to spot from the air, and it should look it.
