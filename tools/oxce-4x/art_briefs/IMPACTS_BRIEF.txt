HITS AND EXPLOSIONS ON THE BATTLEFIELD - SIX ANIMATIONS
=====================================================

LOGOS ARE INCLUDED (John's permanent rule, 12 Nov 2026) - not that these
need any.

STYLE - SATURDAY-MORNING CARTOON (approved 12 Nov)
Bold clean dark outlines, flat colour, one shadow tone, no texture, grain,
photo smoke or gradient. These sit next to the approved cartoon soldiers and
the new shots: Joe fire is BLUE, Cobra fire is RED, bullets are yellow-white
tracers. A hit should look like the 1980s cartoon - a bright star-burst that
pops and is gone, not a realistic muzzle flash.

BACKGROUND
Pure magenta FF00FF everywhere that is not drawing. Hard edges against the
magenta, no soft glow fading into it, no anti-aliased pink fringe. A glow
may be drawn as a flat lighter ring with its own edge.

EXACT SIZES - EVERY FRAME OF A SET THE SAME SIZE, ONE PNG PER FRAME
The game plays these frames in order, one after another, at the size given.
Do not crop. Do not centre each frame on its own drawing - the anchor point
below must be the same pixel in every frame, or the animation will jump.


1. BULLET SPARK
---------------
What: a bullet striking a wall, the ground or a man.
    Frames:   10
    Size:     128 x 160 pixels each
    Anchor:   the spark's centre at pixel x 60, y 60 (from the top-left)
    Colour:   white-hot centre, yellow E8B923, a little orange at the tips,
              dark outline
    Motion:   frame 1 a small bright star; frames 2-4 it bursts to its full
              size (about 60 px across) with four to six sharp points and a
              few chips flying out; frames 5-8 it shrinks and the chips fall;
              frames 9-10 a small grey wisp, then nothing.
    Names:    hit_spark_01.png ... hit_spark_10.png


2. LASER HIT - BLUE (Joe) AND RED (Cobra)
-----------------------------------------
What: a laser bolt striking something. Two sets, the same drawing in two
colours.
    Frames:   10 each
    Size:     128 x 160 pixels each
    Anchor:   the burst's centre at pixel x 60, y 60
    Colour:   BLUE set - white core, bright blue 2E8BFF, pale blue 9CD2FF
                rim, dark navy outline 0A1E4A
              RED set  - white core, bright red E8261E, pale red FF8C70 rim,
                dark maroon outline 4A0A0A
    Motion:   frame 1 a white flash; frames 2-4 a round burst with a ring
              spreading out from it (about 70 px across at its widest);
              frames 5-8 the ring thins and breaks into a few sparks;
              frames 9-10 the last sparks, then nothing.
    Names:    hit_laser_blue_01.png ... hit_laser_blue_10.png
              hit_laser_red_01.png  ... hit_laser_red_10.png


3. SMALL EXPLOSION (grenade, M-32 round)
----------------------------------------
    Frames:   8
    Size:     512 x 256 pixels each
    Anchor:   the blast's centre at the CENTRE of the frame (x 256, y 128)
    Colour:   white-yellow centre, orange E8781E, red C8281E edge, black
              outline; dark grey smoke 4A4A48 with a lighter top
    Motion:   frame 1 a white flash about 80 px across; frames 2-3 a round
              orange fireball, about 180 px across at its biggest; frames
              4-6 it turns to rolling grey smoke with a few dark bits
              thrown out; frames 7-8 the smoke thins to two or three puffs.
    Note:     the game draws SEVERAL of these at once, scattered over the
              blast area and started a moment apart - so one frame is ONE
              burst, not the whole explosion. Keep it inside the frame with
              about 40 px clear at every edge.
    Names:    boom_small_01.png ... boom_small_08.png


4. BIG EXPLOSION (rocket, missile)
----------------------------------
    Frames:   8
    Size:     512 x 256 pixels each
    Anchor:   the centre of the frame (x 256, y 128)
    Colour:   as the small explosion
    Motion:   the same beats, bigger and angrier: the fireball reaches
              about 360 px across and 200 px high, a mushroom of smoke
              rises on frames 5-7, more debris.
    Note:     the same rule - one frame is one burst; the game scatters
              several. Keep about 20 px clear at every edge.
    Names:    boom_big_01.png ... boom_big_08.png


5. SMOKE PUFF (a smoking tile - smoke grenades, fires)
------------------------------------------------------
What: the cloud that hangs over a smoking tile for several turns.
    Frames:   12 = three thicknesses x a 4-frame loop
                frames 1-4   thin smoke, a loop that repeats
                frames 5-8   medium smoke, a loop
                frames 9-12  thick smoke, a loop
              Frame 4 must lead back into frame 1 without a jump (and 8
              into 5, 12 into 9): the game cycles each group of four.
    Size:     128 x 160 pixels each
    Anchor:   this one sits on the TILE, not on a point. The floor of the
              tile is the diamond in the bottom 64 rows (y 96 to 159),
              128 px wide; the cloud sits on that diamond and rises above it
              into the top of the frame.
    Colour:   pale grey E2E2DE, one shadow tone B2B2B0 on the lower right,
              a soft grey outline 6E6E70 (not black - smoke is lighter than
              the solid things around it)
    Names:    smoke_01.png ... smoke_12.png


CHECK BEFORE SENDING
--------------------
    1. Every frame of a set exactly the size given, magenta background,
       hard edges?
    2. The anchor point in the same pixel in every frame of a set?
    3. Counts: 10 spark, 10 blue laser, 10 red laser, 8 small, 8 big,
       12 smoke?
    4. File names exactly as listed?
    5. Bold dark outline, flat colour, one shadow tone - cartoon, not photo?


SEND THE FIRST AND STOP
-----------------------
Send set 1, the bullet spark (all ten frames), and stop. It is checked in
the game before the rest are drawn, so if the size or the timing is wrong
only one set needs redoing.
