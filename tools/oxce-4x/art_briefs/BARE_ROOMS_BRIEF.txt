LOGOS ARE INCLUDED (John's permanent rule, 12 Nov 2026)
Where the real thing carries a logo, emblem, insignia or marking - the G.I. Joe
logo, the Cobra emblem, unit markings, roundels, a red cross - draw it. Any line
further down this brief that says "no logos", "no emblems" or "leave a plain
patch" is cancelled by this one. Letters and words the GAME sets (button labels,
titles, captions) are still left out of the art.

BASE ROOMS - EMPTY SHELLS, JUST BUILT
=====================================

STYLE - REAL SHAPES, CARTOON FINISH (approved 12 Nov)
Real-world shapes and proportions, drawn the way the 1980s Sunbow G.I. Joe
cartoon draws things, leaning cartoon: bold clean dark outlines, flat colour
with one flat cel-shadow tone (two at most), no texture, grain, noise or
photographic detail, minimal small detail. It must sit beside the approved
soldiers (Reference\character_art\approved\duke_1984.png and
snake_eyes_1985.png) as one world.


Four guides come with this, one per size: guide_room_1x1.png, guide_room_2x1.png,
guide_room_1x2.png, guide_room_2x2.png. Each shows the magenta margin, the cut
corners, the wall band, every door (cyan) and the floor area that must stay
empty (yellow). Lay your room over the guide of its size to check; do not
paint on it, and do not copy its colours or its words.


You are drawing the rooms of a military base as they look the day construction
finishes: walls up, floor laid, lights on, doors fitted - and NOTHING inside.
No furniture, no equipment, no crates, no vehicles, no people. The rooms are
stocked later with separate pictures of equipment laid on top of these floors,
so the floors must be clean and even enough for anything to be set down on them.

These are seen on the base planning map: a 6 x 6 grid seen from directly above.


THE VIEW - THE RULE THAT MATTERS MOST
-------------------------------------
Straight down, from directly overhead, ceiling removed - an architect's floor
plan drawn in full colour.
    - NOT isometric. NOT at an angle. NOT a 3D render seen from the side.
    - Walls are seen from the top, as thick borders round the room.
    - Ceiling lamps are seen from above as fittings on the floor plan's edge or
      as pools of light on the floor - never as hanging objects.


SIZES - EVERY SHELL IN FOUR SIZES
---------------------------------
One grid square is 192 x 192 pixels. Draw each theme's shell in all four
footprints, each as ONE image:

    1 x 1   192 x 192
    2 x 1   384 x 192    two squares side by side
    1 x 2   192 x 384    two squares one above the other
    2 x 2   384 x 384    four squares

A bigger room is one continuous room - not small rooms joined. No seams, wall
lines or floor joins where one grid square meets the next inside it. Floor
panels, tiles or slabs may run across the whole room, but must not line up
with the 192-pixel squares.


EDGES AND DOORS
---------------
    Margin   Leave 12 pixels of background on every outside edge, and cut the
             four outside corners back by 12 pixels (a 45-degree chamfer).
             The wall starts inside that margin.

    Walls    16 to 20 pixels thick, seen from above, the same thickness in every
             room of a theme.

    Doors    Rooms are joined by short corridors that meet the MIDDLE of each
             outside edge of each grid square. So put a door in the wall at the
             middle of every outside edge of every square: a 1 x 1 room has four
             doors, a 2 x 1 room has six, a 2 x 2 room has eight. Each door is
             48 pixels wide, centred on that edge's middle, drawn CLOSED - a
             flush sliding blast door in the wall, clearly a door, not a gap.


THE FLOOR - WHERE THE EQUIPMENT WILL GO
---------------------------------------
    - One floor material across the whole room, even in tone from wall to wall.
    - Keep the inner 80 per cent of the floor free of anything that stands
      out: no drains, hatches, painted markings, stains or cracks in it. Those
      would end up under a bed or a machine and show through.
    - Allowed on the floor: the material's own texture (concrete grain, deck
      plate pattern, rubber matting), and a painted safety line or hazard
      stripe running along the foot of the walls only.
    - The floor is a little lighter than the walls so the walls read.
    - Lamps: light pools spread evenly over the floor, or none - no single
      spotlight in one corner.


LIGHT
-----
Light from the upper left, the same in every room. The walls throw a narrow
soft shadow onto the floor along their lower and right sides only. Matte
surfaces; no glare, no reflections.


THE THEMES - DRAW EACH IN ALL FOUR SIZES
----------------------------------------
    joe        The Pit, the team's underground headquarters. Poured concrete
               floor, walls in olive drab paint over concrete, yellow-and-black
               hazard striping at the foot of the walls by each door.
    naval      A Joe outpost aboard ship. Grey steel deck plate floor, painted
               steel bulkheads, rivet lines, deck-grey and white.
    outdoor    A Joe field outpost. A level concrete pad as the floor, walls of
               sandbags and timber revetment seen from above, olive canvas
               where a wall meets a door.
    space      A Joe orbital or launch facility. Clean white composite floor
               panels, pale grey walls, small blue status lights by each door.
    cobra      A Cobra installation. Polished dark steel floor, glossy deep blue
               walls, thin red accent lights along the walls, cold and clinical.

Within a theme every shell must look like part of the same building: same
floor material, same wall, same doors, same light.

20 images in all: 5 themes x 4 sizes.


BACKGROUND
----------
Flat pure magenta, hex FF00FF, in the margin and anywhere that is not room.
Hard edges only: a pixel is room or it is magenta, no soft blend into the
magenta, no transparency, no chequerboard.


DO NOT INCLUDE
--------------
    - Furniture, equipment, machines, vehicles, crates, people. The room is
      EMPTY.
    - Any text, letters, numbers, signs or room names.
    - Any logo, emblem, insignia or trade dress from an existing toy, film,
      cartoon or game - including the Cobra symbol and any G.I. Joe logo.


FILE NAMES
----------
    shell_<theme>_<footprint>.png
    e.g. shell_joe_1x1.png, shell_joe_2x1.png, shell_joe_1x2.png,
         shell_joe_2x2.png, shell_cobra_2x2.png
Do joe first, all four sizes, and stop - it is checked in the game before the
other four themes are drawn.


CHECK BEFORE SENDING
--------------------
    1. Seen from DIRECTLY ABOVE?
    2. Exactly 192, 384 by 192, 192 by 384 or 384 by 384?
    3. 12-pixel magenta margin and cut corners on the outside, no seam inside?
    4. A closed 48-pixel door at the middle of every outside square edge?
    5. Floor empty and even, nothing standing on it?
    6. No text, no logos, no people?
