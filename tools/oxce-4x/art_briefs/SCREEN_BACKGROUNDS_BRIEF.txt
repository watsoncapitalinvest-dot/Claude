SCREEN BACKGROUNDS - EIGHT ROOMS OF THE PIT
===========================================

LOGOS ARE INCLUDED (John's permanent rule, 12 Nov 2026)
Where the real thing carries a logo, emblem, insignia or marking, draw it.

STYLE - REAL SHAPES, CARTOON FINISH (approved 12 Nov)
Real-world shapes and proportions, drawn the way the 1980s Sunbow G.I. Joe
cartoon draws things, leaning cartoon: bold clean dark outlines, flat colour
with one flat cel-shadow tone (two at most), no texture, grain, noise or
photographic detail. It must sit beside the approved soldiers and the title
screen art as one world.

WHAT THEY ARE
The game's screens - soldier lists, research, purchase, craft and so on -
are lists, buttons and text drawn on top of a picture. These eight pictures
are what shows behind them: a room of The Pit, G.I. Joe's underground
headquarters, that fits what the screen is for.

THE ONE RULE THAT MATTERS
The game puts lists and text over the MIDDLE of every picture. So:
    - The middle of the picture - everything more than 200 pixels in from
      the left and right edges and 160 pixels in from the top and bottom -
      is DIM and EVEN: a dark wall, a dark floor, deep shadow. About a
      quarter brightness, one or two flat tones, nothing that catches the
      eye. Cream text must read on it anywhere.
    - The room's character lives in the EDGES: machines, shelves, consoles,
      lights, pipes, people at work - along the sides, the top and the
      bottom.
    - No people in the middle. People at the edges are fine, small, busy,
      not looking at the viewer.

EVERY PICTURE
    Size:    exactly 1920 x 1200 pixels, PNG.
    View:    straight on, at standing eye height, like a stage set.
    Light:   dim room light, a few glowing screens and lamps at the edges.
    Text:    none that the game would set (no screen titles, no button
             words). Signs, stencils and logos that belong to the room are
             fine.
    Colours: The Pit's olive drab, grey concrete and gunmetal, with signal
             yellow (E8B923) and red (C0392B) as small accents.

THE EIGHT
    bg_situation_room.png  The command centre: a big wall map, plotting
                           table, consoles round the edge. Behind the globe's
                           pop-ups, target information and the debriefing.
    bg_barracks.png        Bunks, footlockers, a rack of rifles, a notice
                           board. Behind the soldier list, soldier files,
                           craft crew, training and the memorial.
    bg_lab.png             Benches with glassware, a specimen fridge, a
                           computer bank, a captured Cobra rifle on a bench.
                           Behind research and the tech tree.
    bg_workshop.png        Lathes, a workbench with a vice, parts racks, a
                           half-built weapon on a stand. Behind manufacture.
    bg_hangar.png          The nose of a Skystriker, tool carts, a fuel
                           bowser, a rack of missiles. Behind craft info,
                           craft weapons, equipment and armour.
    bg_supply_depot.png    Pallet racks, crates, an issue counter with a
                           ledger, a forklift. Behind purchase, sell, stores,
                           transfers and the monthly report.
    bg_radar_room.png      Radar scopes glowing green, a plotting board, an
                           operator with headphones. Behind intercept and
                           Cobra contact tracking.
    bg_base_defence.png    A blast door, sandbags, a mounted machine gun, red
                           alert lights. Behind the base-attack screens.

Make bg_situation_room.png first and stop: it is checked in the game before
the other seven are drawn.


CHECK BEFORE SENDING
--------------------
    1. Exactly 1920 x 1200?
    2. Is the middle dim and even, with everything interesting at the edges?
    3. Would a line of cream text read anywhere in the middle?
    4. Same cartoon finish as the title screen?
