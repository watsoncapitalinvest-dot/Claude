AMMO - ART REQUEST v5 (from BUILD, 13 Nov 2026)
This replaces every earlier ammo request.

WHAT THIS IS
Every gun has its OWN magazine: the real magazine, belt, loader or round that gun takes, drawn as it really looks.
The bullets inside are shared by size (small, medium, large and so on). That is game rules only, not art.

This is mostly a resize of ammo_v2.zip:
- Those drawings were right, but drawn far too small.
- Reuse them, redrawn at the sizes below.
- Add the three new ones (items 4, 5 and 16).

STYLE
Same as the gun art:
- 1980s Saturday-morning cartoon
- bold black outlines
- flat colour with two-tone shading
- no photo textures
- no text except where it says so

TWO PICTURES PER MAGAZINE

THE MAGAZINES (game name - file - footprint - what to draw)
SMALL bullets
1. M1911 Magazine - mag_m1911 - 1 x 1 - short straight single-stack steel magazine, blued black, brass round at the top.

8. AK-47 Magazine - mag_ak47 - 1 x 2 - the deep banana curve, steel, dark brown-grey, ribbed sides, copper round at the top. Cobra's.
