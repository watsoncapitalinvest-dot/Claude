GEOSCAPE SIDE PANEL - WEATHER IT AND REDRAW THE BOTTOM CONTROLS
==============================================================

Two images come with this:

    panel_for_weathering_384x1200.png   the panel to paint over
    panel_guide_384x1200.png            the same panel with every fixed area outlined

Deliver ONE image back: the weathered panel, exactly 384 x 1200, PNG.


WHAT TO DO
----------
1. Weather the whole panel. It is painted olive steel bolted inside a command
   vehicle. Add:
     - chipped paint at the edges of each recess and along the outer rails
     - scuffs and polish where hands would rest, around the buttons
     - grime settled into the bottom edge of each recess and around the screws
     - faint scratches in the paint, mostly vertical
   Light from the upper left. Worn, not destroyed - no rust holes, no dents.

2. Redraw the control cluster at the bottom (everything below the six lower
   buttons). It holds four arrow buttons and two zoom rollers. Make them read as
   real physical controls: pressed-steel arrow keys, knurled thumb-wheel rollers
   set into slots. You may restyle these freely, BUT each one must stay inside
   its cyan box on the guide.


WHAT MUST NOT MOVE
------------------
The guide outlines three kinds of area. Treat them as fixed.

  MAGENTA boxes   Recesses. The game prints text into these at run time.
                  - Do not move, resize, or reshape any of them.
                  - Keep the inside of each one flat and DARK, near black. No
                    texture, no highlights, no grime, no scratches inside. Pale
                    text has to stay readable on it.
                  - You may weather the metal rim AROUND each one.

  CYAN boxes      The arrow keys and the two rollers. These are the places the
                  player clicks. Each control must sit inside its own cyan box
                  and fill most of it. Do not swap them round: up arrow top
                  centre, down arrow bottom centre, left and right either side,
                  zoom-out roller far left, zoom-in roller far right.

  YELLOW boxes    Bare metal where four labels will be engraved afterwards.
                  Keep these areas plain painted metal. Do not draw any text,
                  marks, plates or symbols in them.


DO NOT INCLUDE
--------------
    - Any text, letters, numbers or symbols anywhere.
    - Any logo, insignia, emblem or trade dress from an existing toy, film or
      game.
    - Anything outside the 384 x 1200 frame, and no change to the frame size.
    - Transparency changes: the panel fills the whole image, edge to edge.


CHECK BEFORE SENDING
--------------------
    1. Lay your result over the guide. Does every magenta box still sit exactly
       over a dark recess of the same size?
    2. Is every recess interior still flat and near black?
    3. Is each arrow and roller inside its own cyan box?
    4. Are the yellow areas plain metal with nothing drawn in them?
    5. Is it exactly 384 x 1200?
