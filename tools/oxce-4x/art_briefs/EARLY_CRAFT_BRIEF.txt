G.I. JOE AIRCRAFT FOR THE FIRST THREE MONTHS - TWO CRAFT, TWO PICTURES EACH
=========================================================================

A guide comes with this: guide_craft_top_1024.png - the top-view canvas with
the 85 per cent box, the centre line and the nose direction. Lay your top views
over it to check; do not paint on it.


For the first three months of the campaign the player flies two aircraft:

    TOMAHAWK     the twin-rotor troop transport helicopter. Carries the squad
                 to every mission.
    SKYSTRIKER   the do-it-all fighter jet, a Joe F-14 Tomcat. Intercepts Cobra
                 aircraft.

Each needs TWO pictures. Four files in all.


PICTURE 1 - SEEN FROM DIRECTLY ABOVE (the most important one)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Used for the craft parked in its hangar on the base map, on the craft's
equipment screen, and for its outline in an air battle.

    Files:   craft_tomahawk_top.png
             craft_skystriker_top.png
    Size:    exactly 1024 x 1024 pixels.
    View:    straight down from directly overhead, like a photo from a
             satellite. NOT at an angle, NOT three-quarter, no perspective -
             the left and right halves of the aircraft are mirror images.
    Facing:  nose pointing straight UP to the top edge of the picture.
    Fill:    centred. The aircraft's widest dimension - wingspan or rotor
             span - fills about 85 per cent of the picture, nothing cropped.
    Parked:  on the ground, everything still:
               Tomahawk - both rotors shown with their blades still and sharp
               (no motion blur, no disc), front rotor over the nose, rear rotor
               over the tail, blades overlapping where they really overlap.
               Skystriker - wings spread out wide as in flight (not folded
               back), canopy closed.
    Light:   soft and even from directly above, like an overcast day. NO shadow
             of the aircraft, no ground, no tarmac, no hangar floor.
    Background: pure magenta, hex FF00FF, all around and through every gap -
             between the rotor blades, between the tail fins, between the
             wings and the tailplane. Hard edges: a pixel is aircraft or
             magenta, no soft blend.


PICTURE 2 - THE DOSSIER PICTURE
-------------------------------
Shown large in the files screen when the player reads about the craft.

    Files:   v_joe_tomahawk.png
             v_joe_skystriker.png
    Size:    2048 x 1280 pixels.
    View:    three-quarter view from slightly above and in front, nose to the
             LEFT.
    Fill:    the whole aircraft, nothing cropped, centred, filling most of the
             frame.
    Parked:  landing gear down, rotors still, canopy closed.
    Light:   soft and even, like an overcast day. No sunlight, no hot
             highlight, no cast shadow, no ground, no sky.
    Background: pure magenta, hex FF00FF, through every gap as above.


BOTH PICTURES
-------------
    - The same aircraft in both: same shape, same colours, same details, so
      the two read as one machine from two angles.
    - Shape and colour scheme: the known G.I. Joe vehicle - the Tomahawk's
      twin-rotor body and the Skystriker's swing-wing Tomcat shape, in their
      own colours. If you do not know what one of them looked like, say so and
      stop rather than inventing one.
    - A real military aircraft, not a toy: real proportions, panel lines,
      rivets and access panels, matte military paint with light wear. No
      plastic shine, no oversized parts, no stickers.
    - NO markings of any kind: no logos, emblems, roundels, insignia, flags,
      lettering, serial numbers or nose art. Where a marking would go, leave
      the paint plain. Markings are added in the game.
    - No pilot or crew visible, no people, no weapons hanging that the real
      aircraft does not carry.
    - One aircraft per file, PNG.


CHECK BEFORE SENDING
--------------------
    1. Top views: straight down, perfectly symmetrical, nose up, 1024 x 1024?
    2. Rotor blades still and sharp, magenta between them?
    3. Dossier views: three-quarter, nose left, 2048 x 1280?
    4. Magenta everywhere that is not aircraft, hard edges, no shadow?
    5. No markings, no text, no people?
