BASE DEFENCES - FOUR PIECES FOR THE PIT
=======================================

Draw four fixed defences that stand inside G.I. Joe's underground base.
Same camera, scale and style as the Tomahawk door-gun turret frames you already made.

THE FOUR
1. MG TURRET - a heavy machine gun on a low armoured ring mount, bolted to a concrete floor.
   Olive drab and dark steel. Ammo belt feeding from a box on the side. No soldier.
2. MISSILE TURRET - a squat armoured box launcher on a turntable, four to six tubes.
   Olive drab, steel, small yellow and black warning stripes.
3. SECURITY GATE - a heavy sliding steel gate in a short wall section, closed.
   Grey steel, yellow and black hazard stripes along the edge, a red light on top.
4. CHECKPOINT - a sandbagged post, chest high, with a mounted machine gun on top
   and a striped barrier arm beside it. Sandbags tan, gun dark steel.

FOR EACH PIECE
- 8 pictures, one for each facing: north, north-east, east, south-east, south,
  south-west, west, north-west. The gun points the way of the facing.
  The gate and the checkpoint barrier turn with the facing too.
- 1 extra picture: the same piece wrecked (bent, scorched, smoking). Any one facing.
- Each picture exactly 128 wide by 160 tall.
- Transparent background. No floor tile, no shadow outside the piece.
- The piece sits on the bottom middle of the picture, footprint one floor tile,
  the same size on screen as the door-gun turret.
- Seen from above at the game's usual angle, lit from the top left.

FILE NAMES
mg_d0.png ... mg_d7.png, mg_wreck.png
missile_d0.png ... missile_d7.png, missile_wreck.png
gate_d0.png ... gate_d7.png, gate_wreck.png
checkpoint_d0.png ... checkpoint_d7.png, checkpoint_wreck.png
d0 is north, then clockwise to d7 north-west.

DO NOT
- No people in any picture.
- No text, no logos, no frames, no labels.
