AMMO - ART REQUEST v4 (from BUILD, 13 Nov 2026)
This replaces every earlier ammo request. Do not reuse anything from ammo_v2.zip.

WHAT THIS IS
Every kind of ammo comes in TWO forms:
1. ROUNDS: what a soldier carries and loads.
   - The picture is ONE single round of the real ammunition, drawn big.
   - It takes 1 inventory slot.
2. CAN: the bulk container it is shipped and stored in.
   - The picture is the ammo can, box or crate.
11 kinds x 2 forms = 22 items.

STYLE
Same as the gun art:
- 1980s Saturday-morning cartoon
- bold black outlines
- flat colour with two-tone shading
- no photo textures
The only text is the yellow stencil on the cans.

TWO PICTURES PER ITEM
1. <file>_big.png, 128 x 192 pixels, transparent background.
   - The item in the equipment screen, seen STRAIGHT FROM ABOVE, lying flat. Not tilted.
   - It must FILL its footprint, with 4 px clear at the edges. One slot is 64 x 64 px.
     - A 1 x 1 item fills a 56 x 56 box in the middle of the canvas.
     - A 1 x 2 item fills 56 x 120.
     - A 2 x 2 item fills 120 x 120.
   - A single round in a 1 x 1 slot is drawn lying DIAGONALLY, corner to corner, so it is as long as the slot allows.
2. <file>_floor.png, 128 x 160 pixels, transparent background.
   - The same item lying on the battle floor, seen from the game's camera, centred on the 128 x 64 tile diamond at the bottom of the canvas.
   - Make it 40 to 60 px across so it reads on the map. A single round may be a little smaller, but never under 30 px.
