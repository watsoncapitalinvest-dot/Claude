CHARACTERS - ONE PICTURE EACH

Same job as the soldiers you did before: each picture is the figure shown on
the equipment screen, AND the reference painted onto the 3D model that makes
that character's battlefield sprites. So the clothing, the colours and the
gear matter more than the pose.

EVERY PICTURE
- Standing straight, facing the viewer, head on. Arms down and slightly away
  from the body so the belt, the webbing and the hands are all clear.
- Head to boots, nothing cropped, upright, centred, feet on the bottom edge.
- Background pure magenta, hex FF00FF, all around him and in the gaps between
  his arms and his body.
- Evenly lit and flat, as if under an overcast sky. No sunlight, no rim light,
  no cast shadow, no ground beneath him.
- NOTHING HELD IN THE HANDS. No rifle, no pistol, no knife in the fist.
  Holstered and slung gear is fine.
- At least 1024 pixels tall. One character per file.

THE OUTFIT IS THE POINT
Each is a real G.I. Joe or Cobra character with a known action figure. Dress
him in THAT figure's outfit - its colours, its webbing, its headgear. Not
generic modern camouflage. The last batch came back in all-over olive and that
is the one thing to avoid.

IF YOU DO NOT KNOW WHAT A CHARACTER'S FIGURE LOOKED LIKE, SAY SO AND SKIP HIM.
Do not invent one. A missing figure can be chased. A wrong one looks right
until it is in the game beside the others.

Save each as a PNG named exactly as listed.
