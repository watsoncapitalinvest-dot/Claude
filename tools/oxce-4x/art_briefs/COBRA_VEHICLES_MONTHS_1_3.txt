COBRA VEHICLES - FIRST THREE MONTHS
===================================

LOGOS ARE INCLUDED (John's permanent rule). Draw the Cobra emblem and every
marking where the real toy carries it.

STYLE - REAL SHAPES, CARTOON FINISH
The real toy's shape and colour scheme, drawn the way the 1980s Sunbow G.I. Joe
cartoon draws things: bold even black outlines, flat colour with one flat
cel-shadow tone (two at most), no texture, grain, noise or photographic
detail. Attach duke_1984.png and snake_eyes_1985.png as the style anchors.

WHAT FLIES IN MONTHS 1-3
By the campaign calendar, Cobra's first three months are small raids and FANG
landings. The only Cobra vehicle the player meets is the FANG, a one-man
gyrocopter flown by a Viper Pilot. In the game it is every early Cobra
contact: the scout flights, the cargo runs between front companies, and the
landings. The Stinger jeep arrives in months 4-8 and the H.I.S.S. tank by
month 12, so neither is needed yet.

The FANG needs THREE pictures. Background pure magenta FF00FF everywhere the
craft is not, through every gap (rotor blades, skids, under the body), with
hard edges. No pilot, no people, no ground, no sky, no shadow. Exact file
names.


1. fang_dossier.png - THE FILE PICTURE
--------------------------------------
    The Cobra F.A.N.G. gyrocopter (1983 toy), whole, nothing cropped.
    Three-quarter view from slightly above and in front, nose to the LEFT.
    Landing skids down, rotor still, blades clearly visible.
    At least 1536 pixels on the long side, the craft filling most of the frame.
    This is what the player sees in DOSSIERS. Put the detail here.

2. fang_intercept.png - IN THE AIR, FOR THE INTERCEPT WINDOW
------------------------------------------------------------
    The same FANG flying, seen exactly side-on, nose to the LEFT, level.
    Rotor drawn as a flat blurred disc (it is spinning), skids up or folded if the
    toy allows, otherwise as they are.
    1280 x 768 pixels, the craft about two thirds of the width, centred.
    This is the picture in the window when a Joe jet closes on it.

3. fang_top.png - STRAIGHT DOWN, FOR THE MAP
--------------------------------------------
    The same FANG landed, seen from directly above, nose pointing UP the page.
    Rotor still, blades straight, every blade fully visible.
    1024 x 1024 pixels, the craft filling about three quarters of the square.
    The game makes the landed and crashed FANG on the battle map from it.

Send fang_dossier.png first and stop. It is checked before the other two are
drawn.
