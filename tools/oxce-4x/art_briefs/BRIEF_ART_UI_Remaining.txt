UI ART STILL MISSING - ART BRIEF (from BUILD, 12 Nov 2026)

What this is: the last screens and interface pieces still drawn in 1994 pixel art. Use the same style as everything else: 1980s Saturday-morning cartoon, bold black outlines, flat colour with two-tone shading, no photo textures.
This brief does not cover the scanner, the medkit, the graphs screen or the bodies on the ground. Those are in SCREENS AND BODIES.

GENERAL
- PNG at the exact sizes below.
- Full-screen backgrounds are 1920 x 1200 and not transparent.
- Small pieces are transparent unless it says otherwise.
- Do not bake text into any picture. The game writes all words and numbers.
- Logos are allowed.
- Zip it as ui_remaining.zip, with one folder per section, and put it in Reference\.

1. SOLDIER FILE SCREEN (a Joe's stats page in the base)
- soldier_file.png, 1920 x 1200: an open olive personnel file folder on a desk.
- Leave a plain cream sheet on the right two-thirds, from x 640 to x 1880 and y 260 to y 1160. The game writes the stat bars there.
- Leave a plain photo square top left, from x 40 to x 600 and y 40 to y 600. The game puts the Joe's picture there.

2. BASE INFO SCREEN (the base's capacity and upkeep page)
- base_info.png, 1920 x 1200: a clipboard on The Pit's ops desk.
- Leave a plain paper area from x 120 to x 1800 and y 160 to y 1120. The game writes the rows there.

3. INTEL FILE (the encyclopedia: entries on gear, rooms, Cobra and vehicles)
- intel_item.png, 1920 x 1200: a dossier page for a piece of gear. Leave a plain picture panel on the left half and a plain text column on the right half.
- intel_room.png, 1920 x 1200: the same dossier, with a wide plain picture panel across the top two-thirds and a text strip below it.
- intel_text.png, 1920 x 1200: the same dossier, all plain paper, for text-only entries.
- intel_vehicle.png, 1920 x 1200: the same dossier on a blueprint-blue sheet, with a plain picture panel across the top half.
- intel_cobra_craft.png, 1920 x 1200: the same dossier in Cobra red and black, stamped "CAPTURED" in one corner. The stamp is the only text allowed in this brief.
- All five must share the same folder edges and clip, so that paging between entries reads as one file.

4. IN-BATTLE POPUPS (abort mission, save or load in battle, confirm end of mission, turn log, loadout save)
- battle_popup.png, 1920 x 1200: a field radio handset case lying open on the dirt.
- Its middle is plain dark metal from x 320 to x 1600 and y 200 to y 1000. The game puts its buttons and text there.

5. UNIT STATS IN BATTLE (the screen you get by clicking a Joe's rank badge)
- unit_info.png, 1920 x 1200: a field notebook page, with plain ruled lines across the whole page.
- The game draws about 25 rows of bars over it, so keep the page quiet.

6. BATTLE CURSOR (the box that sits on the tile under the mouse)
Each frame is 128 x 160 and transparent, on the same tile diamond as the bodies brief: a 128 x 64 diamond at the bottom of the canvas.
Each cursor comes in two halves, so a soldier can stand inside it:
- the BACK half: the far two edges of the diamond and the top of the box
- the FRONT half: the near two edges
Four kinds, with 4 frames each of a slow pulse:
- cursor_move: yellow, for walking.
- cursor_aim: red, with crosshair corners, for shooting.
- cursor_throw: orange, with a small arc above, for throwing.
- cursor_waypoint: white, for guided rockets.
Name each frame like this: cursor_move_back_1.png, cursor_move_front_1.png and so on, up to _4.
That makes 32 files.

7. BATTLE BUTTON ICONS
128 x 96 each, not transparent, on a plain olive button face:
- icon_launch.png: a guided rocket with a dotted path.
- icon_special.png: a gold G.I. Joe eagle. This is the default for a Joe's special-skill button.

8. AMMO PICTURES STILL IN PIXEL ART
Each item needs two pictures. Use the gun art's colours and lighting.
- <name>_big.png, 128 x 192, transparent: the item seen from above, as it lies in the equipment screen.
- <name>_floor.png, 128 x 160, transparent: the item lying on the battle floor diamond.
Items:
- pistol_clip: a 1980s pistol magazine.
- cannon_ap, cannon_he, cannon_inc: three heavy-cannon shell boxes. Make them the same box with a different coloured band each: black for AP, red for HE, orange for incendiary.
- autocannon_ap, autocannon_he, autocannon_inc: three autocannon belt boxes, banded the same way.
Do not draw grenades or rockets. John already has those modelled in 3D, and BUILD renders them.

NOT IN THIS BRIEF
- Dogfight screen: this waits for the air war design.
- Muzzle flash and tracers: BUILD draws these with the effects.
- Path dots and the numbers on them: the game draws these in its own font.
