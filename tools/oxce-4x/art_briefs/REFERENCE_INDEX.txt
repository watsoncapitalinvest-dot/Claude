CHARACTER REFERENCE - WHERE THE PHOTOGRAPHS ARE

Reference lives on John's machine, not in this repository:

    C:\GI-Joe-Silent-Storm\GIJoe_XCOM_4x\Reference\
        yearbook_carded_figures\   362 files, NAMED BY CHARACTER
        rolodex_file_cards\        975 file-card scans, NOT named

The photographs are product photography and stay out of this repo. Only this
index is committed. Point the art tool at the local file; do not paste the
photograph into anything the project publishes, and keep packaging, logos and
trade dress out of the finished art - the photo is for the FIGURE, not the card.

yearbook_carded_figures is the one to search first, because its filenames are
the character names. rolodex_file_cards is 975 scans with numeric names and no
labels, so finding one means looking through images - use it only for the gaps
listed at the bottom.
