CONVOY RAID MAP - BUILD SPEC (for John's build, from BUILD, 13 Nov 2026)

GOAL
Build one playable convoy raid battle map: two Cobra box trucks stopped on a desert highway.
- Build it as ONE fixed map, the same way the Cartel Ranch (M1RANCH) was built.
- Do not randomise anything yet.
- BUILD writes the mission rules, Cobra units, cargo, objectives and reinforcements once the map files arrive.

USE THE RANCH KIT
- Use the same tools that made the ranch: Reference\maps_m1_sets\tools\ (kitlib.py, run_set.py, build_set.py, testblock.py).
- Same camera and scale:
  - Orthographic, 30 degrees, viewer to the south-east.
  - 1 tile = 1.3 m = a 64 px edge at 4x.
  - 1 level = 96 px.
  - Cell top corner at (64, 96).
- Same look: flat cartoon colour with two-tone shading and black ink.
- Hard alpha edges: every pixel fully solid or fully clear.

FILES TO DELIVER (zip as convoy_m3.zip into Reference\)
For each terrain set:
- <SET>.MCD
- <SET>.PCK and <SET>.TAB (32x40 frames)
- <SET>_4X.PCK and <SET>_4X.TAB (128x160 frames, same order)
- <SET>_4X.png (128x160 full-colour frames in a grid)
TABs are 32-bit, as in the ranch sets.

The map:
- M3CONVOY.MAP, 50 wide x 40 long x 4 high
- M3CONVOY.RMP, the route nodes

Terrain order in the map: BLANKS, DESERT, ROAD, BOXTRUCK.
Keep each set under 255 parts.

TERRAIN SETS
1. DESERT (about 70 parts)
   - sand floor
   - packed dirt
   - gravel
   - rock floor
   - rock walls and boulders, 1 and 2 levels high, for the ridge
   - slope pieces
   - dry-wash bank pieces
   - scrub bushes (no cover)
   - cactus (thin cover)
   - small rocks (half cover)
2. ROAD (about 35 parts)
   - asphalt
   - lane line
   - edge line
   - gravel shoulder
   - culvert: a concrete pipe under the road, 1 tile wide, that a man can crawl through
   - telephone pole (destroyable)
   - burnt-out old car (2x3 tiles, cut per cell)
   - spike strip (floor decal)
   - road-crew yard:
     - gravel piles
     - a parked grader (cut per cell)
     - a one-room site trailer with a door and a window, that units can enter
   - billboard: 2 levels high on legs, climbable, with a deck at level 1. The face is a sign for "Extensive Enterprises".
3. BOXTRUCK (about 75 parts)
   - Box truck: footprint 6 x 3 tiles, 2 levels.
     - Cab, 2 x 3 tiles: units can enter it. It holds an ENGINE BLOCK object, one per truck. Mark that part as an objective part.
     - Cargo box, 4 x 3 tiles: thin walls with low armour, so bullets punch through. The rear door is a roll-up that opens like a UFO door.
     - Inside the box: 2 x 3 tiles of floor.
     - Cab roof and box roof sit on level 1.
   - Wreck chain for every truck part, using its "dies into" part: intact -> shot up (holes, broken glass) -> burning -> burnt hulk.
   - Fuel tank under the cab on both sides: an explosive part that sets the cab on fire.
   - Look: plain white box truck with a dark grey cab.

The design intent is three ways in:
1. Take the ridge and shoot down.
2. Crawl the culvert and come up through the yard.
3. Run the wash east and hit the trucks from the south.

ROUTE NODES (RMP)
- Joe landing zone: 25 rank-1 (XCOM) deployment nodes, in a grid inside x 1-10, y 34-39. Use the same settings as the ranch LZ (z byte 3, priority 5, no links). 25 so a full squad fits.
- Cobra spawn nodes:
  - 2 drivers, one in each cab
  - 4 guards, at the four corners of the convoy
  - 1 officer, beside truck 1
  - 1 lookout, in the yard
- Patrol links: around both trucks, along the north shoulder and up into the yard.
- High guard priority on the gap between the two trucks.
- 4 reinforcement nodes along the east end of the road.
- Mark the road links "Large", so 2x2 escorts can drive it later.

CHECKS BEFORE SENDING
- The map loads in testblock.py with no missing part ids.
- Every node sits on a walkable cell.

WHAT BUILD DOES WHEN IT ARRIVES

- Cargo: random loot in the truck boxes.
- Reinforcements: a timer, stopped by taking out the officer.
- Spawning the mission on the globe.
