VEHICLES, AIRCRAFT AND SHIPS - ONE PICTURE EACH

Each picture does two jobs: it is the dossier picture the player sees in the
files screen, and it is the reference for the craft's small sprites. So the
shape, the markings and the colours matter more than the setting.

EVERY PICTURE
- The whole craft, nothing cropped, centred, filling most of the frame.
- Three-quarter view from slightly above and in front, nose to the left.
- Background pure magenta, hex FF00FF, all around it and through every gap -
  under the wings, through the rotor, between the hull and the outriggers.
- Evenly lit and flat, as if under an overcast sky. No sunlight, no hot
  highlight, no cast shadow, no ground, no water, no sky.
- Landing gear or wheels down for aircraft that have them. Nothing flying.
- No crew, no pilot figure, no people.
- At least 1536 pixels on the long side. One craft per file.

THE REAL TOY IS THE REFERENCE
These are real G.I. Joe and Cobra vehicles with known toys. Use the toy's own
shape and colour scheme. If you do not know a vehicle, say so and skip it
rather than inventing one.

Save each as a PNG named exactly as listed.
