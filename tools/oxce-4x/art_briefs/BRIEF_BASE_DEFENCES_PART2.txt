BASE DEFENCES, PART 2 - THREE MORE PIECES FOR THE PIT
=====================================================

Your MG turret is done and in the game. Make these three the same way.
Match its look exactly: same camera angle, same light from the top left, same scale, same line weight.
Look at APPROVED_STYLE_MG.png and the mg_ files.

PIECES 1 AND 2 - SAME FORMAT AS THE MG TURRET
1. MISSILE TURRET - a squat armoured box launcher on the same kind of ring base as the MG,
   four to six tubes. Olive drab and dark steel, small yellow and black warning stripes.
2. CHECKPOINT - a sandbagged post, chest high, a mounted machine gun on top,
   a striped red and white barrier arm beside it. Tan sandbags, dark steel gun.

For each of these two:
- 8 pictures, one per facing, the same order as the MG: d0 points up-right, then clockwise
  (d1 right, d2 down-right, d3 down, d4 down-left, d5 left, d6 up-left, d7 up).
- 1 wrecked picture (bent, scorched), any facing.
- Each picture 128 wide x 160 tall, transparent background.
- Base sits on the ground line exactly like the MG (see unit_frame_template_128x160.png
  and soldier_scale.png): bottom middle, about as tall as a man.
File names:
  missile_d0.png ... missile_d7.png, missile_wreck.png
  checkpoint_d0.png ... checkpoint_d7.png, checkpoint_wreck.png

PIECE 3 - THE SECURITY GATE (A DIFFERENT FORMAT)
The gate is part of a wall, not a free-standing object. It fills one tile's wall edge.
Use the two templates in GATE_TEMPLATES.png:
- The red diamond is the floor tile.
- The green panel is where the wall stands: along the top-right edge (north wall)
  or the top-left edge (west wall). The gate fills that green panel, no wider, no taller.
Look: a heavy sliding steel gate, grey steel, yellow and black hazard stripes down the edges,
a small red light on top.
Make 6 pictures, each 128 x 160, transparent everywhere outside the gate:
  gate_north_closed.png  - gate shut, in the north wall
  gate_north_open.png    - gate slid open (the frame stays, the opening is empty)
  gate_north_wreck.png   - gate blown in, bent and scorched
  gate_west_closed.png   - the same three for the west wall
  gate_west_open.png
  gate_west_wreck.png

DO NOT
- No people. No text, logos or labels. No floor, no shadow outside the piece.
- Do not change the size or the camera between pictures.
