BUILD -> MAPS, 13 Nov 2026: TWO MAPS FOR CAPTURE (prisoner transport, riot in The Pit)
Design: claude/CAPTURE_DESIGN_v1.md, Part 1b (John 14:45). Same kit, camera, scale and delivery as the van stop and the ambush.
Month 1 and on: handguns, small squads.

MAP 1: PRISONER TRANSPORT (the Joes escort prisoners to the authorities; Cobra may ambush it)
- Name: TRANSPORT. 50 x 40 x 2 (sizes in multiples of 10), ground on level 0.
- An open two-lane country road running west-east across the middle. Shoulders, a ditch on each side, a few trees, fence lines, a culvert or two, a billboard or a broken-down car for cover.
- The prisoner van: an olive or white government van with barred rear windows, parked on the road in the middle of the map, rear doors closed. It never moves. Its rear 2 tiles are the doors.
- The escort: one Joe APC or jeep parked ahead of the van (cover, not a vehicle that moves).
- Joe start: 16 rank-1 nodes in a ring around the van and the escort vehicle (they start beside the van).
- Ambush: Cobra comes in from TWO sides. Put 8 rank-2 spawn nodes in the brush at the north-west corner and 8 at the south-east corner, with patrol links running toward the van.
- Inside the van: 4 civilian nodes (rank 0) for the prisoners. They stay in the van.
- What decides it (BUILD wires it): Cobra wins by getting a man to the van's rear doors; the Joes win by driving the ambush off.

MAP 2: RIOT IN THE PIT (prisoners break out of the cells inside the Joes' base)
- Name: RIOT. 30 x 30 x 2 (multiples of 10), ground on level 0, indoors (The Pit's concrete and steel look).
- The cell block: a corridor with 4 cells along one side (bars, cell doors that open), 2 prisoners per cell.
- Outside the cells: a strip where 2 more men per room sit cuffed to a rail.
- A guard room next to the cell block with a weapons locker the prisoners can break into (a container part; BUILD puts handguns in it).
- A way out: a corridor to the surface lift at the far end. Anyone who reaches the lift escapes.
- The Joes on base come in from the opposite end from the lift: 12 rank-1 nodes in a mess hall or ready room.
- Prisoners: rank-2 nodes in and around the cells (12), patrol links toward the guard room and the lift.
