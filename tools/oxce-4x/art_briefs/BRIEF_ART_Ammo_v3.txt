AMMO v3 - ART BRIEF (from BUILD, 13 Nov 2026)
Replaces both AMMO v2 and the v2 redo note. John: "just use bullets and ammo boxes."

WHAT THIS IS
Six ammo items. Each is an ammo box with its rounds showing, not a magazine.

STYLE
Same as the gun art:
- 1980s Saturday-morning cartoon
- bold black outlines
- flat colour with two-tone shading
- no photo textures
The only text is the yellow stencil named below.

TWO PICTURES PER ITEM
1. <name>_big.png, 128 x 192, transparent.
   - The item in the equipment screen, seen STRAIGHT FROM ABOVE.
   - It must FILL its footprint, with 4 px clear at the edges. One slot is 64 x 64 px.
     - A 1 x 1 item fills a 56 x 56 box in the middle of the canvas.
     - A 1 x 2 item fills a 56 x 120 box in the middle of the canvas.
2. <name>_floor.png, 128 x 160, transparent.
   - The same item on the battle floor, seen from the game's camera, lying on the bottom 128 x 64 tile diamond.
   - It must be 40 to 60 px across, so it reads on the map.

THE SIX
1. ammo_9mm - 1 x 1 - a small olive ammo box, lid open, packed with rows of short brass 9 mm rounds. Stencil "9MM".
2. ammo_556 - 1 x 1 - an olive ammo box, lid open, packed with longer, pointed brass 5.56 rounds. Stencil "5.56". Its rounds must look clearly longer and slimmer than the 9 mm rounds.
3. ammo_50 - 1 x 2 - a big olive ammo can, lid open, with a length of large linked .50 rounds coming out of it. Stencil ".50 CAL".
4. ammo_rockets - 1 x 2 - an open olive crate holding two rockets: fat olive bodies, yellow band, black nose. Stencil "ROCKETS".
5. ammo_flame - 1 x 2 - a flamethrower fuel tank: a steel bottle, olive or red, with a valve and a short hose stub on top. Stencil "FUEL".
6. ammo_bolts - 1 x 2 - a small olive quiver box with six crossbow bolts showing: black shafts, steel broadheads, olive vanes. Stencil "BOLTS".
