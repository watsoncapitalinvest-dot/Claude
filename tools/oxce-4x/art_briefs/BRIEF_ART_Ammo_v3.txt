AMMO - ART REQUEST v3 (from BUILD, 13 Nov 2026)
This replaces every earlier ammo request. Do not reuse anything from ammo_v2.zip.

WHAT THIS IS
Eleven ammo items for the G.I. Joe game. Each is an ammo box, can, crate, tin or case with its rounds showing. There are no magazines.

STYLE
Same as the gun art:
- 1980s Saturday-morning cartoon
- bold black outlines
- flat colour with two-tone shading
- no photo textures
The only text is the yellow stencil named for each item.

TWO PICTURES PER ITEM
1. <file>_big.png, 128 x 192 pixels, transparent background.
   - The item as it shows in the equipment screen, seen STRAIGHT FROM ABOVE. Not tilted.
   - It must FILL its footprint, with 4 px clear at the edges. One slot is 64 x 64 px.
     - A 1 x 1 item fills a 56 x 56 box in the middle of the canvas.
     - A 1 x 2 item fills a 56 x 120 box in the middle of the canvas.
2. <file>_floor.png, 128 x 160 pixels, transparent background.
   - The same item lying on the battle floor, seen from the game's camera.
   - The floor tile is a 128 x 64 diamond at the bottom of the canvas. The item sits in the middle of that diamond.
   - It must be 40 to 60 px across, big enough to read on the map.

THE ELEVEN (game name - file name - footprint - what to draw)
The game names are general on purpose. The drawings must still show the REAL rounds named, with the real calibre stencilled.
1. Pistol Ammo - ammo_pistol - 1 x 1
   A small olive ammo box, lid open, packed with rows of short brass 9 mm rounds. Stencil "9MM".
2. Rifle Ammo - ammo_rifle - 1 x 1
   An olive ammo box, lid open, packed with long, pointed brass rounds. Stencil "5.56". The rounds must look clearly longer and slimmer than the 9 mm rounds.
3. Heavy Ammo - ammo_heavy - 1 x 2
   A big olive steel ammo can, lid open, with a length of large linked .50 rounds coming out of it. Stencil ".50 CAL".
4. Shotgun Shells - ammo_shells - 1 x 1
   A small round-cornered olive tin, lid open, packed upright with red plastic shotgun shells, brass bases showing. Stencil "12 GA".
5. Rockets - ammo_rockets - 1 x 2
   An open olive wooden crate holding two rockets: fat olive bodies, a yellow band, black noses. Stencil "ROCKETS".
6. Mortar Rounds - ammo_mortar - 1 x 2
   An open olive wooden crate holding three mortar bombs: teardrop olive bodies, a yellow band, tail fins, brass-coloured nose fuzes. Stencil "MORTAR".
7. Flamethrower Fuel - ammo_fuel - 1 x 2
   A steel fuel bottle, olive with a red band, with a valve and a short hose stub on top. Stencil "FUEL".
8. Crossbow Bolts - ammo_bolts - 1 x 2
   A slim olive case, lid open, with six crossbow bolts in it: black shafts, steel broadheads, olive vanes. Stencil "BOLTS".
9. Energy Cells - ammo_energy - 1 x 1
   A hard dark-grey carry case, lid open, with three chunky energy cells in foam slots, each with a glowing green charge strip. 1982 sci-fi. Stencil "ENERGY".
10. Grenade Rounds - ammo_grenade - 1 x 1
   An open olive ammo case holding six stubby 40 mm grenade rounds standing upright: brass cases, olive-drab rounded heads with a yellow band. Stencil "40MM".
11. Flares - ammo_flares - 1 x 1
   A small olive cloth bandolier pouch, flap open, holding three fat flare cartridges: red bodies, brass bases. Stencil "FLARE".
