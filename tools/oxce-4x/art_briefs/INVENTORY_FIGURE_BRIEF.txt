INVENTORY FIGURES - THE SOLDIER SHOWN ON THE EQUIPMENT SCREEN

You are painting the full-length figure of a soldier that stands in the middle
of the equipment screen, between his hand slots, while the player loads him
out. It is shown large and sharp, so it must be a finished illustration.


EACH PICTURE
------------
    File:      PNG, named as listed below.
    Canvas:    exactly 640 pixels wide by 1600 pixels tall.
    Background: pure magenta, hex FF00FF, everywhere that is not the figure -
               including the gaps between the arms and the body and between
               the legs. No gradient, no texture, no shadow on it.

    Placement: the whole figure, head to boots, nothing cropped.
               Top of the head (or helmet) at 40 pixels from the top edge.
               Soles of the boots at 1560 pixels from the top edge.
               Centred left to right.

    View:      straight on from the front, at the height of the chest. Not
               from above, not from below, not turned to one side.

    Pose:      standing straight and still, feet a little apart, weight even.
               Arms straight down and slightly away from the body so the
               hands, the belt and the webbing at the hips are all clear of
               each other. Hands open and relaxed.

    Hands:     EMPTY. No rifle, pistol, knife, radio or anything else held.
               The player puts the weapons in his hands in the game.
               Holstered, sheathed and slung gear on the body is fine.

    Light:     soft and even from the front and slightly above, like an
               overcast day. No strong shadows across the body, no rim light,
               no glow around the edges, no shadow on the ground, no ground.

    Style:     a painted illustration of a real soldier, realistic proportions,
               the same style for every character so they sit together. NOT a
               photograph, NOT a toy or action figure, NOT plastic-looking, no
               visible joints or rivets on the limbs.

    Colour:    the screen behind him is dark olive steel. Make sure his
               outline reads against that: a character dressed in black or
               dark green needs his webbing, pouches and straps in clearly
               lighter or different shades so his shape does not disappear.


WHAT HE WEARS
-------------
Each is a real G.I. Joe character. Dress him in his known outfit - its colours,
its webbing, its headgear - not generic modern camouflage.

No logos, emblems, insignia, lettering, badges or flags anywhere. Leave a plain
patch where an emblem would go.

If you are not sure what a character wears, say so and skip him. Do not invent
an outfit.
