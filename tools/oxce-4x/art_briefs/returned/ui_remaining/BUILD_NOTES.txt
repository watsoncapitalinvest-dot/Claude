UI REMAINING — 57 PNGs / 8 sections

All full-screen backgrounds are 1920 x 1200 opaque RGB. The designated photo,
stats, clipboard and popup regions are exact, uniform-color rectangles. The
five intel screens share one folder/clip master with identical outer geometry.
No screen text is baked in except CAPTURED on the Cobra dossier, as requested.

Cursor frames are 128 x 160 RGBA. Tile diamond vertices are (64,96), (127,128),
(64,159), (0,128). Top box vertices are (64,32), (127,64), (64,95), (0,64).
Back half contains the two far floor edges, top diamond and far/side verticals;
front half contains only the two near floor edges. Aim adds corner reticles;
throw adds an arc above the box. Four core-brightness levels are .68/.86/1/.86.
Suggested preview cadence: 400 ms per frame; engine timing remains BUILD's call.

Battle buttons are 128 x 96 opaque RGB on olive #4A5640. Ammo big images are
128 x 192 RGBA; floor images are 128 x 160 RGBA and sit on the bottom tile.
Shell-box and belt-box variants retain identical shapes and differ only by
their classification band: AP #15191B, HE #C0392B, INC #D9822B.

Main colors: ink #101618; panel #1B2023; popup quiet area #232A2D; olive #4A5640;
cream #EEE5CE; paper #F2EBD9; signal yellow #E8B923; Cobra red #C0392B.
Geometry/layout graphics are authored natively. The eagle and handset accents
were created with the built-in image tool, then prepared for palette/size use.

This package excludes scanner, medkit, graph screens, bodies, dogfight,
grenades, rocket ammo, muzzle flashes, tracers, path-dot sprites and labels.
No game files or gameplay settings are modified. Preview labels are only in
the separate preview JPEGs outside this ZIP; shipping art has no such labels.
