These are the one-picture-per-item source paintings. Inventory, floor and held
eight-facing strips are not included; the supplied brief says the game's build
script creates those. No game project/build script was supplied or run.

The exports use the supplied target lengths at 1 metre = 1000 pixels, with
clear margins outside the object. Grenades use upright height. The diagonal
mortar uses its longest image dimension. Backgrounds are hard keyed magenta.

Reference choices
- Retained supplied reference silhouettes and visible fitted parts.
- Original 1985 Snake Eyes sword and Lady Jaye javelin, and 1986 Sgt. Slaughter
  baton were used with the user's approval because the ZIP lacked usable images.
- The pistol and revolver were based on the actual Colt 1911 and Model 29 meshes
  in Handgun History part 3, not the MK23 and SW500 stand-in pictures.
- The launcher retains the supplied RPG7V.png shape. It is an RPG-7 reference
  variant, not an accurate M20 Super Bazooka; the source list mixes these.
- The mortar follows the provided Mortar Defense Unit toy blueprint, which is
  different from the M1 81mm label in the table.
- The shotgun follows M1Super90.png. The sawed-off and SPAS12 alternatives are
  not extra items in this 21-item set.
- The crossbow follows the original toy photo rather than the third-party
  no-derivatives TechCrossBow model.
- The smoke canister follows a supplied grenade-set model. Its preview had no
  material, so an olive color was used. The M67 uses an external Army photo.

Style: dark outlines, flat colors and cel shading. No new laser components or
gadget redesigns. Laser effects and harmless outcomes belong in game behavior.

These are generated style adaptations, not pixel-perfect tracings. Review the
paintings alongside the original references for any final accuracy adjustments.
The original ZIP and source images were not overwritten.
